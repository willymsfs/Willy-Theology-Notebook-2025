# 12 — Method Audit of My Courses

Every set of notes in this vault already **operates by some method**, whether or not it says so. Naming it is the whole point of [[01 - What Is Theological Method and Why It Matters#4. What goes wrong when method is neglected or badly chosen]]: an unnamed method cannot be argued with, only obeyed.

> [!note] Scope of this audit
> Judged from each course's MOC and a sample of its notes, not from reading all ~300 files. Where a verdict is inferred rather than verified, it says so. Treat each "upgrade" line as a proposal, not a correction.

---

## 1. Vatican II
*111 notes — Documents, Concepts, Theologians, Bishops, Popes, Councils*

**Method actually in use** — historical-critical narrative plus documentary analysis: origin, preparatory schemata, participants, sessions, timeline, per-document study. Effectively Lonergan's **positive phase** — research, interpretation, history — done thoroughly.

**Where the methodological question is already live** — `06 - Reception Controversies & Hermeneutics`. Continuity vs. discontinuity is not a quarrel about facts but about **horizon**: what the Council *was* depends on where the interpreter stands. That note is doing Dialectics, the fourth functional specialty, without the name.

**Blind spot** — the normative phase is thin. The folder tells what happened and what was said; less on how the conciliar teaching is to be systematised and communicated now.

**Upgrade** — in `06`, name the two hermeneutics as competing horizons ([[Horizon]]) and add the *ressourcement* backdrop ([[Ressourcement]]) that made the Council methodologically possible.

## 2. Theology of Synodality
*Two parallel stores, ~20 notes*

**Method actually in use** — the synodal process is itself a method: *conversation in the Spirit* — listen, discern, decide — a close ecclesial cousin of **see–judge–act** ([[05 - How to Use the Methods - Step by Step#5. See – Judge – Act (the pastoral spiral)]]), with communal discernment where liberation theology puts social analysis.

**Sharpest methodological claim available** — synodality relocates the *locus theologicus* toward the whole people (*sensus fidelium*), which is a change in the **sources** of theology, not only in Church procedure.

**Upgrade** — one note comparing synodal discernment with see-judge-act step by step: what each does with analysis, with the poor, and with authority.

## 3. Theology of the Trinity
*4 notes, from the Oxford Handbook*

**Method actually in use** — classical **systematics**: biblical data → conciliar definition → conceptual grammar → contemporary systematics. Lonergan's specialty 7 almost exactly.

**Already methodologically loaded** — the psychological analogy is the textbook case of doing systematics by **analogy rather than proof** ([[10 - Major Theologians and Their Methods]] on Aquinas), and Rahner's Rule is a methodological rule before it is a doctrine: it forbids treating the immanent Trinity as a separate object of speculation.

**Upgrade** — add one line to the MOC naming the method as systematics-by-analogy, and link Rahner's Rule to [[Transcendental Method]].

## 4. BTh Pune - Foundations of Catholic Theology
*70 source notes + 12 thematic*

**Method actually in use** — distillation of a 96-source notebook into thematic course notes: essentially **manual method**, doctrine presented in ordered topics with biblical and magisterial supports. Efficient for exams; the weakness is the weakness of every manual — it presents results, not the operations that produced them.

**A live tension inside the folder** — `02 - Indian Christian Theology and Inculturation` records Thomas Stephens substituting Indian terms (*puja*, *jnana-snana*, *Vaikunthnatha*). That is precisely the **translation model** that both true inculturation and the Dalit critique reject ([[09 - Indian Theological Method#1. Beyond the translation model]], [[09 - Indian Theological Method#4. Dalit theological method — the counter-move]]). The notes describe it; they do not yet judge it.

**Upgrade** — add to that note the two-line question: *whose* Indian terms, and at whose cost? Link [[Dalit Theology]] and the claim [[The historical-critical method is necessary but not sufficient for an Indian reading]].

## 5. Prophets - Messengers of Mercy
*11 notes*

**Method actually in use** — biblical-theological with a strong social edge; the prophet as critic of structures.

**Method available and unused** — the prophetic material is the natural home of **praxis method**: the prophet does see–judge–act before the phrase existed, and Soares-Prabhu's ideological suspicion is a prophetic move transposed into exegesis.

**Upgrade** — one note: "the prophet as theologian and the three suspicions" linking [[Praxis]] and Segundo's circle.

## 6. Acts - Great Adventure
*20 sessions, 30 schemas, claims*

**Method actually in use** — **narrative method** ([[03 - Catalogue of Theological Methods#19. Narrative]]) plus session-based inductive reading, already producing claims and schemas. Methodologically the healthiest folder in the vault: it moves from text → structure → claim.

**Upgrade** — none needed. Use it as the model for how other courses should generate claims.

## 7. Biblical courses - Maniparambil and Lizorkin-Eyzenberg
*≈70 notes*

**Method actually in use** — historical-critical and philological, with Second Temple / Jewish-context reconstruction.

**Watch-point** — this is exactly the method Soares-Prabhu called necessary but not sufficient. Excellent for anchoring the text; silent about the Indian situation that will hear it.

**Upgrade** — when preaching from these notes, run the last two steps of the Indian circle (ideological suspicion → new interpretation) before writing the homily.

## 8. St Francis de Sales
*17 notes*

**Method actually in use** — spiritual-affective and pastoral: doctrine applied to the affections, in the same family as Owen's method ([[10 - Major Theologians and Their Methods]]).

**Upgrade** — note the family resemblance explicitly; Salesian gentleness and Puritan affective divinity are two forms of one methodological conviction, that true theology is completed in the ordering of desire.

## Cross-vault verdict

| Phase of method | Well covered | Thin |
|---|---|---|
| Research / interpretation / history | Vatican II, Maniparambil, Lizorkin-Eyzenberg, Acts | — |
| Dialectics (conflicting horizons) | Vatican II §06 only | everywhere else |
| Foundations / doctrines | BTh Pune, Trinity | — |
| Systematics | Trinity | Synodality, Prophets |
| Communications | Homily Log, St Francis de Sales | most course folders |
| Indian correction | BTh Pune §02 (descriptive only) | Trinity, Vatican II, Prophets |

**Two structural observations.**
1. **Dialectics is the missing specialty.** The vault is strong at retrieving and strong at applying, weak at the step where conflicting horizons are named and one's own standpoint declared. That is the step that turns study into theology.
2. **The Indian correction is described but rarely applied.** Inculturation appears as *content* in several folders; it rarely operates as *method* on the material of those folders.
