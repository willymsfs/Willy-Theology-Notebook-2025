# Theological Methods — Map of Content

Study unit on **theological method**: what it is, why it matters, the major methods, how to operate them, and where each belongs. Built from the **Theological Methods** NotebookLM notebook (46 academic sources — Lonergan, Tillich, Kaufman, Burtt, Bleeker, Ormerod, Koyama/Chua, Teel, Pitts, Schoedel and others; PDFs sit in this same folder).

> [!abstract] The one-sentence core
> A method is **"a normative pattern of recurrent and related operations yielding cumulative and progressive results"** (Lonergan) — not a rigid rulebook, not a finished system, but the self-correcting process that *produces* systems and lets theologians collaborate across generations.

## Timeline anchors
| Period | Turning point | Methodological shift |
|--------|--------------|----------------------|
| 2nd–5th c. | Origen, Augustine, Irenaeus | Authoritarian-deductive + allegory; Irenaeus limits speculation (*that*, not *how*) |
| 12th c. | Rise of universities | Exegesis splits from speculation; *sententiae* → *quaestiones* |
| 13th c. | Aquinas | *Ordo disciplinae*; analogy replaces proof in mysteries |
| 15th–16th c. | Valla, Erasmus, Reformers | *Ad fontes*; grammatical-historical exegesis becomes normative |
| 1879 | Leo XIII, *Aeterni Patris* | Neo-scholastic restoration; deductive manuals |
| 18th–19th c. | Historical consciousness | Sources become *data*, not premises; inductive turn |
| 1799+ | Schleiermacher | Turn to religious consciousness |
| 1918+ | Barth | Dialectical rejection of culture-theology |
| 1951+ | Tillich | Method of correlation |
| 1972 | Lonergan, *Method in Theology* | Turn to the subject; eight functional specialties |
| 1962–65 | Vatican II | *Ressourcement* + theology of history break the manuals |
| 1968+ | Medellín, Gutiérrez | Praxis, see-judge-act, option for the poor |
| 1984+ | Lindbeck, postmodernity | Cultural-linguistic; suspicion of meta-narratives |
