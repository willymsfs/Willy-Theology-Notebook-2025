## A. Why read dead theologians

> [!abstract] The Introduction's argument
> 1. **The sea breeze.** Borrowing C. S. Lewis: reading old books keeps "the clean sea breeze of the centuries blowing through our minds."
> 2. **Against the broom cupboard.** Confined to the present and the safely familiar, we become **irrelevant dinosaurs**.
> 3. **Different mistakes.** Past writers erred *differently* from us, so they correct blind spots our own age cannot see. This is the cure for **bumpkinery**.
> 4. **Theology is corporate.** It is the church's task across time, so ignoring the historic church's voices is effectively **an act of schism**.

> [!note] The Intermezzo — Aquinas to Luther
> While monks were venerating Aquinas's relics, Luther was rejecting the contents of his mind. The break is over **Aristotle**: "the entire *Ethics* of Aristotle is the worst enemy of grace"; "the whole Aristotle is to theology as darkness is to light." A revolution in theology that is first a revolution in **method** — see [[11 - Fault Lines and Lineages#1. Lineages of influence]].

## D. Reading list — "Going on with…"

**Apostolic Fathers** — Michael Holmes, *The Apostolic Fathers in English*, 3rd ed. (Baker, 2006) · Clayton Jefford, *The Apostolic Fathers: An Essential Guide* (Abingdon, 2006) and *Reading the Apostolic Fathers* (Hendrickson, 1996).

**Justin and Irenaeus** — Justin, *First* and *Second Apologies*, *Dialogue with Trypho* (ANF 1) · Irenaeus, *Against Heresies* (ANF 1) and *The Demonstration of the Apostolic Preaching*, tr. John Behr (SVS, 1997) · Gustaf Wingren, *Man and the Incarnation* (Oliver & Boyd, 1959).

**Athanasius** — *On the Incarnation* (SVS, with C. S. Lewis's introduction) · *Against the Arians* (NPNF 2.4) · Thomas Weinandy, *Athanasius: A Theological Introduction* (Ashgate, 2007) · R. P. C. Hanson, *The Search for the Christian Doctrine of God: The Arian Controversy 318–381* (T. & T. Clark, 1988).

**Augustine** — *Confessions* · *City of God* · *On Christian Doctrine* · *Enchiridion* · *Augustine: Later Works*, LCC (SCM, 1955) · Peter Brown, *Augustine of Hippo* (Faber, 1967).

**Anselm** — *Monologion* and *Cur Deus Homo* in *Anselm: Basic Writings*, tr. Thomas Williams (Hackett, 2007) · Richard Southern, *Saint Anselm: A Portrait in a Landscape* (CUP, 1990).

**Aquinas** — *Summa Theologiae*, English Dominican Province, 5 vols. (Christian Classics, 1981) · James Weisheipl, *Thomas d'Aquino* (CUA, 1974) · Brian Davies, *The Thought of Thomas Aquinas* (Clarendon, 1992) · Eleonore Stump, *Aquinas* (Routledge, 2003).

**Luther** — Timothy Lull (ed.), *Martin Luther's Basic Theological Writings* (Fortress, 1989) · *The Freedom of a Christian* · *The Bondage of the Will*, ed. J. I. Packer and O. R. **Calvin** — *Institutes*, tr. Ford Lewis Battles, 2 vols., 1559 edition (Westminster, 1960) · John Dillenberger (ed.), *John Calvin: Selections from His Writings* (Anchor, 1971) · François Wendel, *Calvin* (Collins, 1963) · anything by T. H. L. Parker.

**Owen** — *Communion with God* (as *Communion with the Triune God*, ed. Kapic and Taylor, Crossway 2007; or *Works* vol. 2, ed. Goold, Banner of Truth 1965–91) · *Christologia* (*Works* 1) · *Pneumatologia* (*Works* 3) · *Commentary on Hebrews*, with the prefatory articles in *Works* 17 · Peter Toon, *God's Statesman* (Paternoster, 1971) · Sinclair Ferguson, *John Owen on the Christian Life* (Banner of Truth, 1987).

**Edwards** — *Religious Affections* · "A Divine and Supernatural Light" · "Distinguishing Marks of a Work of the Spirit of God" · *The End for Which God Created the World* · *A History of the Work of Redemption* · *Works*, 2 vols. (Banner of Truth) or 26 vols. (Yale, 1957–2008) · George Marsden, *Jonathan Edwards: A Life* (Yale, 2003).

**Schleiermacher** — *The Christian Faith*, ed. Mackintosh and Stewart (T. & T. Clark, 1999) · Keith Clements, *Friedrich Schleiermacher: Pioneer of Modern Theology* (Collins, 1987) · J. Gresham Machen, *Christianity and Liberalism* (Macmillan, 1923) · James Barr, *Fundamentalism* (SCM, 1977).

**Barth** — *Church Dogmatics*, especially III/1 · *Evangelical Theology: An Introduction* (Eerdmans, 2004) · *Dogmatics in Outline* (SCM, 1949) · John Webster, *Karl Barth* (Continuum, 2000) · Colin Gunton, *The Barth Lectures* (T. & T. Clark, 2007).

**Packer** — *Knowing God* · his introduction to Luther's *Bondage of the Will* · Leland Ryken, *J. I. Packer: An Evangelical Life* (Crossway).

**Rahner** (from the other notebook sources, not this volume) — *The Content of Faith* · Karen Kilby on his ecclesiology · John M. McDermott on the Christologies · Patrick J. Burke, *Reinterpreting Rahner* on conceptual thought · Ryan Duns on the *Vorgriff*.
