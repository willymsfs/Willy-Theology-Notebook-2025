## Executive summary
The prophets are not fortune-tellers but **messengers of divine mercy**. A prophet is one *"called out by the Lord to speak forth the word of the Lord under the inspiration of the Holy Spirit"* — one who **afflicts the comfortable and comforts the afflicted** (Heschel). Across the Divided Kingdom, Exile, and Return, God keeps sending messengers to heal a ruptured covenant. Read as poetry and enacted parable, the prophetic books reveal the **fatherly and spousal heart of God**, reach fulfillment in **Jesus Christ** (the ultimate prophet), and hand every baptized believer a **prophetic anointing** to renew the world.

## Most important points
1. **Prophet ≠ predictor.** Hebrew *Nabi* (of the mouth) — a spokesman for God, not a crystal ball.
2. **Threefold prophetic message:** call to repentance · declaration of judgment · hope and consolation.
3. **Prophets were poets and actors** — living, enacted parables aimed at the heart.
4. **Sin has consequences** regardless of privilege — yet God never stops sending messengers.
5. **Hosea = "literary crucifix":** God pays the slave-price (30 pieces of silver) to redeem an unfaithful bride.
6. **Jonah's mirror:** God's mercy reaches our enemies; the question is whether we resent it.
7. **Daniel's rule:** *"purpose in your heart"* beforehand; fidelity in small things builds virtue for the furnace and the lion's den.
8. **Christ walks in the fire** — the "fourth man" — and is the Suffering Servant who *is* the new covenant.
9. **You are chosen** — before the foundation of the world — and anointed at baptism to be a prophet now.

## So what — the call to renewal
Evangelization needs no prediction of the future — only your **personal testimony**, **deeds of mercy**, and **joy**. Modern people trust witnesses over teachers. Like Barnabas, the "Son of Encouragement," step into the prophetic role: **up-build, encourage, console** — become a messenger of mercy. → [[08 Living Your Baptismal Anointing]]
