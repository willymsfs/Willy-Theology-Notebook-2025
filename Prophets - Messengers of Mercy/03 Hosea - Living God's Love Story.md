# Hosea — Living God's Love Story

Part of [[Prophets - Messengers of Mercy (MOC)]].

## Trial
- God commands Hosea to **marry Gomer, a prostitute**.
- She repeatedly returns to former lovers, abandoning Hosea and their three children.

## Message
- Hosea's painful marriage = **living metaphor for Israel's spiritual infidelity and idolatry**.
- Israel forsook the covenant to worship pagan idols — "possessed with a spirit of harlotry."
- God commands Hosea to the red-light district to **buy his wife back from slavery**: 15 shekels of silver + barley = **30 pieces of silver** (the price of a slave).

## Lessons
- **Idolatry is to our relationship with God what prostitution is to a marriage.**
- Hosea is a **"literary crucifix"** — the extreme, passionate lengths God goes to redeem and buy back His bride.
- Connects to [[06 The Heart of God]] — Hosea 11: *"How can I give you up, O Ephraim?"*
