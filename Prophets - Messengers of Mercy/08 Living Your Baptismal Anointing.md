# Living Your Baptismal Prophetic Anointing

Part of [[Prophets - Messengers of Mercy (MOC)]].

## You are called
- Prophets are **not relics of the past**. Before the foundations of the world, **God chose you** to be a prophet for this exact moment.
- At Catholic baptism, anointed with **holy chrism** to live as members of Christ's body: **priest, prophet, and king**.

## Living the call
- Living the prophetic call = accepting the mission of **evangelization**.
- No need to predict the future — simply **share your personal story and the Gospel**.
- Modern man listens to **witnesses more than teachers** — back words with visible **deeds of mercy**.
- Like **Barnabas** ("Son of Encouragement"), fulfill the prophetic role by **upbuilding, encouraging, and consoling** others in the body of Christ.

> [!quote] On joy
> "Joy is the net of love by which we catch souls." — Mother Teresa
>
> "The greatest evidence against the existence of God in the world is a joyless Christian." — Peter Kreeft

## Takeaway
- Stop hiding your faith — become a **messenger of mercy**.
- Know the story of salvation, keep your **personal testimony ready**, radiate **joy** — and step into your anointing to *afflict the comfortable and comfort the afflicted*.
