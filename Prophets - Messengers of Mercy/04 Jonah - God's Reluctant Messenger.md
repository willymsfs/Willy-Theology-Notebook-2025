# Jonah — God's Reluctant Messenger

Part of [[Prophets - Messengers of Mercy (MOC)]].

## Trial
- God commands Jonah to preach to **Nineveh** (capital of Assyria — Israel's enemy).
- Jonah **flees** the opposite way toward Tarshish.
- Thrown overboard in a storm; swallowed by a **great fish for three days**.

## Message
- One-sentence homily: **"In forty days, Nineveh shall be overthrown."**
- Shockingly, the pagan city **repents** in fasting and ashes — God spares them.
- Jonah is **furious**: he fled because he *knew* God was merciful and did not want his enemies forgiven.

## Lessons
- We can **never escape the presence of the Lord**.
- The great fish was **not punishment but divine mercy** — rescuing Jonah from drowning.
- Spiritual mirror: do we harbor resentment? Do we ration God's mercy only to those we deem worthy?
