# Prophets: Messengers of God's Mercy and the Call to Renewal

> [!info] Method
> Biblical-theological with a social edge. The natural home of **praxis method** — the prophet does see-judge-act before the phrase existed. Audit: [[12 - Method Audit of My Courses#5. Prophets - Messengers of Mercy|method audit §5]].


> [!abstract] Overview
> Study of the **biblical prophets** as **messengers of divine mercy** across salvation history. A prophet is one called by God to **speak truth** — to *afflict the comfortable and comfort the afflicted*. Through Hosea, Jonah, and Daniel we see personal trials and courageous fidelity as timeless models. The narratives move beyond prediction to reveal the **fatherly heart of God**, point to fulfillment in **Jesus Christ**, and call every believer to live their **baptismal prophetic anointing**.
