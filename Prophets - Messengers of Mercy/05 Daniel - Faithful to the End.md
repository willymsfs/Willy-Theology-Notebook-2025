# Daniel — Faithful to the End

Part of [[Prophets - Messengers of Mercy (MOC)]].

## Trial
- Exiled to Babylon as a **teenager**; intense cultural indoctrination.
- Babylonians try to change identities: **new names** honoring pagan gods, **unkosher food** from the king's table.
- Friends thrown into the **fiery furnace** for refusing to worship an idol.
- As an **80-year-old**, Daniel thrown into the **lion's den** for refusing to stop praying.

## Message
- Babylon = both a historical empire and a **supernatural spiritual force opposed to God's plans**.
- It seeks to enslave humanity and demand unswerving allegiance.
- Overarching message of the apocalyptic visions: **earthly power will bend its knee to the Lord** — God's goodness and justice prevail.

## Lessons
- **"Purpose in our hearts"** ahead of time to remain faithful.
- Fidelity in **small, everyday decisions** (like dietary laws) builds the virtue needed for major trials.
- Jesus walks with us in suffering — He appeared as the **"fourth in the fire"** with Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego. See [[07 Fulfillment in Jesus Christ]].
