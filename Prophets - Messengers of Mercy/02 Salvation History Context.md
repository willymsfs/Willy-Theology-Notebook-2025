# Salvation History Context

Part of [[Prophets - Messengers of Mercy (MOC)]].

Writing prophets spoke during three critical, dark periods of Israel's history:

## 1. The Divided Kingdom
- After David and Solomon, the kingdom split → **Israel (North)** and **Judah (South)**.
- Prophets: **[[03 Hosea - Living God's Love Story|Hosea]]**, [[04 Jonah - God's Reluctant Messenger|Jonah]], Isaiah.
- Warned of judgment for **idolatry** and reliance on **political alliances**.

## 2. The Exile
- Covenant infidelity → North falls to **Assyria**; South deported to **Babylon**.
- Jerusalem and the **Temple destroyed**.
- **Jeremiah** witnessed destruction firsthand; **Ezekiel** and **[[05 Daniel - Faithful to the End|Daniel]]** ministered to exiles in Babylon.

## 3. The Return
- After ~70 years, Persian King **Cyrus** allows return in **three waves** — rebuild temple, people, city walls.
- **Haggai** and **Malachi** combat spiritual apathy (*acedia*) and drive the rebuilding.

> [!note] Takeaways
> - Sin and covenant infidelity always bring consequences — geography and past privilege do not exempt.
> - Even in the darkest periods, God faithfully sends messengers to guide His people back.
