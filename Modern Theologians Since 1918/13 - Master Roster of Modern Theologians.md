# 13 — Master Roster of Modern Theologians

**The list.** Every figure treated in the Ford–Muers volume, with dates, tradition, central concept, significance, and use today. For the pre-1918 roster (Clement → Packer) see [[10 - Major Theologians and Their Methods]].

> [!tip] Live version
> This table is curated (significance and application need judgment). For **sortable, always-current** views of the same people — by era, region, school or Ford type — use [[Theologians Index (Dataview)]], which queries the frontmatter of every note in `50 People/`.

> [!info] How to read the columns
> **Concept** = the one term you must be able to define. **Significance** = what changed because of them. **Use today** = where they are actually operative now (the last column is my own synthesis, not the book's). Dates are for orientation; a dash means the book does not give them and I have not verified them.

## I. Classics of the twentieth century
| Theologian | Dates | Tradition | Central concept | Significance | Use today |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **[[Karl Barth]]** | 1886–1968 | Swiss Reformed; dialectical | Infinite qualitative distinction; actualism; threefold Word | Overthrew liberal culture-theology; restored theology's nerve | The default point of departure for dogmatics; Barmen as the template for church resistance |
| **[[Dietrich Bonhoeffer]]** | 1906–1945 | German Lutheran; Confessing Church | *Stellvertretung*; cheap grace; world come of age | Redemption located at the centre of earthly life; ethics as relationality | Political discipleship, costly grace preaching, resistance ethics |
| **[[Paul Tillich]]** | 1886–1965 | German-American Lutheran | Ultimate concern; ground of being; New Being; the demonic | Made faith intelligible to secular culture; legitimated theology of culture | Apologetics, theology and the arts, pastoral psychology, defining "religion" in law |
| **[[Henri de Lubac]]** | 1896–1991 | French Jesuit; *nouvelle théologie* | *Surnaturel*; *ressourcement*; against *natura pura* | Broke the nature/grace dualism; catalysed Vatican II | The standing argument against a secular self-sufficient "nature" |
| **[[Karl Rahner]]** | 1904–1984 | German Jesuit; transcendental Thomism | *Vorgriff*; supernatural existential; anonymous Christian | Anthropological turn; the new Catholic mainstream | Theology of religions, grace in ordinary life, mystagogical catechesis |
| **[[Hans Urs von Balthasar]]** | 1905–1988 | Swiss Catholic | Theological aesthetics; *Gestalt Christi*; theo-drama | Restored beauty to theology; the great anti-reductionist system | Aesthetics, Holy Saturday theology, the Catholic counterweight to Rahner |
