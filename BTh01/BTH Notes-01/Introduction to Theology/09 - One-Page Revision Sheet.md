# 09 — One-Page Revision Sheet

> The whole Course on one Screen. Read this the Night before and the Morning of the Oral.

---

## THE COURSE IN ONE SENTENCE

**Theology is Faith seeking Understanding, done by a believing Community, out of the Sources handed down, tested by the Criteria of the Church, in answer to the Cry of the People among whom we actually live.**

**Leitmotif**: the Burning Bush (Ex 3). Turning aside (3) → Holy Ground (5) → the Cry heard (7) → Sent (10).

---

## CH 1 — WHAT IS THEOLOGY

**Theology** = *Theos* + *Logos* = God-Talk.

| Schema | Content |
|---|---|
| **God-Question** | A. Atheism (Camus, Marx, Nietzsche, Freud; also Charvaka, Theravada) · B. Agnosticism · C. **Theism** (Dei Filius 2, DV 6, CCC 36) |
| **Can we speak?** | A. *Neti Neti* · B. Negative Theology · C. **Biblical** (CCC 40-43) |
| **Language** | Univocal (Six Mangoes) · Equivocal (Bank) · **ANALOGICAL** (our Father) |
| **Experience** | Otto: tremendous and fascinating Mystery. Bush · Transfiguration · Emmaus |
| **Symbol** | Points beyond itself **and participates**. Christ; the Eucharist |

**Seven Definitions**: Theologia · **Anselm** *Fides quaerens intellectum* (avoiding Fideism and Rationalism) · **Lex Orandi Lex Credendi** (Prosper, 5th c.) · **Rahner** Theological Anthropology · **Gutierrez** Critical Reflection on Praxis · **Hermeneutics** (GS 4-5) · **Mythos** (Jennings). Then the **Working Definition**.

**Eight Polarities**: Mystery/Intelligibility · Identity/Change · Committed/Critical · Community/Individual · One/Many (*Ekam sat viprah bahudha vadanti*) · Universal/Contextual (**Aquinas' Books burned by the Bishop of Paris**) · Orthodoxy/Heterodoxy (Luther *Sola Fide*) · Orthodoxy/Orthopraxis.

**Philosophy** = *ancilla theologiae*. Plato (Origen, Augustine, Bonaventure) · Aristotle (Aquinas) · Existentialism (Bultmann, Rahner) · **Vedanta** (Upadhyaya, Abhishiktananda, Panikkar) → *Saccidananda*.

**Eight Goals**: awaken · systematize · sift Scripture · prophetic Role · Hierarchy of Truths (UR 11) · pastoral · Mission · **the Kingdom of God**.

## CH 2 — THE SOURCES

**Foundations** = **Faith** (Tillich: Ultimate Concern; Abraham Rom 4:3) + **Revelation** (Dunn; DV 2, 4).
Faith ≠ Belief. *Fides qua* / *fides quae*. Cultural Christianity. Sobrino: "to know Jesus is to follow Jesus."

**Four Models of Revelation** (McGrath): Doctrine · Presence · Experience · History.
Revelation is **Closed** in Christ (CCC 65-66), **but** never exhausted.

> **ONE Source (Apostolic Tradition), THREE Realities: Scripture, Tradition, Magisterium. "One cannot stand without the others" (DV 10).**

- **Scripture**: *norma normans non normata*; "the soul of sacred theology" (DV 24)
- **Tradition (7)**: Ecclesial Traditions (**22 Churches, 6 Families**; India: Syro-Malabar, Syro-Malankara) · Liturgy (SC 10) · Creeds · **Fathers** (749/604/639; four Marks) · Dogmas (UR 11) · Codes (**Gratian** 12th c.; 1917, **1983**, **1990**; two lungs) · Catechisms (Trent 1566; **CCC 1992**)
- **Magisterium (3)**: Collegiality (LG 18-23, 25) · Papal (Mt 16:18; **Vatican I 1870**; Lyons 1274, Florence 1439) · **Sensus Fidei** (LG 12)

**Threefold Blending**: Sensus Fidei + Magisterium + Theologian.

**Resources (5)**: People and Experience (Acts 10:34-35) · Cultures (GS 55, 58) · Religions (**FABC Taipei 1974**) · Peoples' Movements (Exodus) · **the Cry of the Poor** (*Ecclesia in Asia* 34; **Epistemological Privilege of the Poor**).

## CH 3 — DEVELOPMENT AND PLURALISM

**Pluralism ≠ Relativism.** Relativism is "a negation of realism."
**Five Seedbeds**: Fundamental · Speculative · Biblical · Magisterial · Contextual.

**Biblical**: OT 46 (Yahwist, Elohist, Priestly, Deuteronomic, Wisdom); NT 27. **Gospels developed backwards**: Proclamation → Passion Narrative → Ministry prefixed. Mark 1:1 to 16:8, no Birth. Ascending / Descending / Cosmic Christology.

**Patristic** (to mid-8th c.): three Phases (Apologetic, Dogmatic, Theological); three Streams (**Edessa-Ephraem · Alexandria-Cappadocians · Rome-Augustine**).
**Twelve Developments**: a Canon · b Tradition (Irenaeus) · c Creeds · d Christ (**Chalcedon 451**; Alexandrian vs Antiochene) · e Trinity (Latin from One God, Greek from Three Persons) · f Church (**five Patriarchates**) · g Sacramentality (vs Donatists) · h Grace (vs Pelagius) · i Hermeneutics (Origen) · j Culture (Justin, *logos*) · k Reason and Faith (Cappadocians) · l Schools.

**Scholastic**: **Pre 800-1050 · Early 1050-1200 · High 1200-1350 · Late 1350-1500**. Gilson: "cathedrals of the mind." Intellect (Aquinas) vs Will (Bonaventure).
Six Figures: **Anselm** (Father of Scholasticism) · **Bernard** ("to know Jesus and Him crucified") · **Aquinas** · **Bonaventure** (three Stages; Leo XIII "the prince") · **Scotus** (Incarnation willed from Eternity; "co-lovers") · **Ockham** (**Razor**; Nominalist; *via moderna*; Theology not a Science).

**Post-Scholastic**: Trent, defensive. **Aeterni Patris, Leo XIII, 1879**; Mercier, de Wulf, **Maritain** (three Degrees). **Marcel**: Problem/Mystery, Having/Being, I/we. **Vatican II 1962-65**: three Groups (Europe, Latin America, **Asia-Africa multi-religious**).

**Contextual**: *theologia universalis* ends. Three Sensitivities (Context, Procedure, History); three Roots (**Gospel, Church, Culture**). Instances: Continental · Secularisation (**Bonhoeffer**, *Deus ex machina*) · Death of God · Black · Feminist (3 Elements) · Ecofeminism (***naturism***) · **Dalit** · **Minjung** (***han***).

## CH 4 — SEVEN MODELS

**Four Ways of Faith and Culture**: Translation · Anthropological · Synthetic · **Transcendental** (the *how*, not the *what*).

| # | Model | Who | Key |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Syriac-Semitic | **Ephraem** (306-373, Nisibis, Edessa) | Theology as **Poetry**; three Attitudes (Domination, Detachment, **Love and Wonder**); Faith the **Luminous Eye**; ecological Insight |
| 2 | Liturgical | **Schmemann** (1921-83) | Theology as **doxology**; Tradition = "the life of the Holy Spirit in the Church"; the Christian **Priest of the Cosmos**; **1054 / 1453** |
| 3 | Scholastic | **Aquinas** (1225-74) | **Sacred Science**; Philosophy the **Handmaid**; *via inventionis* vs *via doctrinae*; **Lonergan**'s two Reasons |
| 4 | Kerygmatic | **Barth** (1886-1968) | Theology as **Response**; God "with, by, and **for** humans"; four Attitudes (**Astonishment**, Concern, Commitment, Loneliness); *credere in* |
| 5 | Transcendental | **Rahner** (1904-84) | **Supernatural Existential**; Spirit / embodied / historical; "non-philosophical theology is bad theology" |
| 6 | Praxis | Latin America, **1970s** | Three Questions; **the NON-PERSON**; **Orthopraxis**; three Levels; **CDF 1984 and 1986** |
| 7 | Contemplative | **Abhishiktananda** (1910-73) | **Christian Advaita**; "the cave of the heart"; the **Advaitic Night**; **Christophany**; critiqued by **Bede Griffiths** and **Dupuis** |

## CH 5 — THE CRITERIA

**The Question**: where does legitimate Pluralism end and Heterodoxy begin? (EN 65: foster Expression, **but** Faith "neither impaired nor mutilated").
**Panikkar**: "What is the criterion of the criterion?"

**Church as Sacrament**: *martyria · leitourgia · diakonia* (Mt 25).
***Paradosis*** three Elements: Deposit · living Authority · Succession.
**DV 12 three Criteria**: Content and Unity · living Tradition · Analogy of Faith.
**Tillard**: Tradition = Memory, "the humus from which life never stops borrowing."
**Congar**: Tradition contains documents **and** life, external Norm **and** living Subject.

| | **Orthodox** | **Protestant** | **Catholic** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Principle | **Synodality**; ***Sobornost*** | *Sola Scriptura* → *Solus Deus* | One Source, three Realities |
| Authority | Within, never above, the Church | Sovereignty of the Gospel over Offices | **Pluralism of Authority**; Pope **Head and Member** |
| Weakness noted | Underweights the Papal Magisterium | **Dulles' three Objections** | — |

**Exercise of the Magisterium**: Extraordinary (Council; *ex cathedra*) · Ordinary Universal (definitively / not definitively) · Ordinary Non-Universal (Bishops; Conferences and Synod; **the Pope, LG 25**).

**Dogma** = revealed **and** proposed as such. **Doctrine** = broader, revealed or not. Doctrine tends toward **Clarity**, not Comprehensiveness.

| Gradation | Response |
|---|---|
| Definitive **Dogma** | **Assent of Faith** |
| Definitive **Doctrine** | **Firm Acceptance** |
| **Non-Definitive** (most Teaching) | **Religious Submission of Intellect and Will** |
| **Prudential Admonitions** | **Obedience to the Spirit** of the Law |

**Theologian**: twofold Mediation; **"between a creed and a quest."**

**EIGHT PARAMETERS**: Knowability of God · Faith and Reason Friends · Universality · Communitarian Context · Catholicity in Time · **Sacramental Principle** · Scripture within Tradition · Role of Authority.

## CH 6 — THE INDIAN CONTEXT

**St. Thomas, 52 AD.** "Hindu in culture, Christian in faith, **Syrian in worship**" (Persian Immigrants, 4th c.). Now **eight** Churches: Syro-Malabar (5 m) · Jacobite · Malankara Orthodox · Mar Thoma (**1843**) · **Syro-Malankara (1930, Mar Ivanios)** · Chaldean Syrian · Thozhiyur · St. Thomas Evangelical (1961). First Break **1665**, after Portuguese **Romanization**.

**Latin**: Franciscans 1518 · Goa Bishopric **1534** · **Xavier 1542** · de Nobili 1606 · Hierarchy **1886** · today **114 Dioceses, 10 million**.
**Protestant**: **CSI 1947** (4 Churches) · **CNI 1970** (7) · Baptists 3 m · UELCI 1975.
**Three Catholic Individual Churches**; **CBCI 1944**, restructured **1988**.
Christians = **2.5%**. "**The Gospel has not yet fully encountered the Indian genius.**"

**Culture**: **Cosmotheandric** (Cosmic-Divine-Human). Four *Purusharthas* (artha, kama, dharma, moksha); four *Ashramas* (brahmachari, grahastha, vanaprastha, sanyasi). Education as *sadhana* = **Transformation, not Information**.

**Religions**: Adivasi **8%**, *sui generis*, **not Hindu**. Brahman; *maya*; three *margas*. History = **a Pool, not a River**. Hindu Renaissance (Roy, Dayanand, Ramakrishna, Vivekananda, Gandhi, Aurobindo, Vinoba). **Indian Secularism = equal Treatment of all Religions**, threatened by **Hindutva**.

**Philosophy**: Nyaya · Vaisheshika · Sankhya (*prakriti*/*purusha*, three *gunas*) · Yoga · Mimamsa · **Vedanta** (**Shankara**, *atman* = Brahman, **Saccidananda**; qualified by Ramanuja, Madhva, Vallabha).

**Pioneers**: St. Thomas Christians (little Theology: **Isolation, Dependence, Caste Accommodation**; but two Positions vindicated: salvation in one's own Religion; "one law of St. Thomas and another of St. Peter") · **de Nobili** (Tamil and Sanskrit, Brahmin *sanyasi*, **stopped by Opposition**) · Renaissance (**Roy 1828**, **Sen 1881**, Mozoomdar 1883) · Nationalism (*preparatio evangelica*) · **Protestant Pioneers first**: Banerjee, Parani Andi, **Upadhyaya** (Vedanta), Sundar Singh, Appasamy (Bhakti), Chenchaiah (New Man), Chakkarai (*avatara*, Spirit) · **Ashrams** (Monchanin, Abhishiktananda, Bede Griffiths, Vandana, Sara Grant).

**Socio-Economic**: **128 of 174** on Social Development (UN, 1997). Mixed Economy; Caste and Class linked; Globalization.
**Political**: Article 25 (profess, practise, **propagate**); **Hindutva** and the Hindu Rashtra.
**Women**: **47 of 543** MPs (8.5%); **33%** Panchayat Reservation, "proved their mettle."
**Dalits and Tribals = 22.5%**. *dal* = to break, to oppress. **Phule** and **Ambedkar**. **DOUBLE DISCRIMINATION**: State and Church.

> **THE TURN**: the earlier Pioneers used a **Translation Model** with **upper-caste** Categories. But the Majority are *sudras*, Dalits, Tribals. **Whom does Theology address?**

**New Humanity**: "**Authentic humanization is at the same time divinization**." **Devanandan**, then **M.M. Thomas**.
**Indian Liberation**: **Kappen** (all Resources of all Faiths; **Jesuology** in continuity with the Buddha and Bhakti Dissent) · **Rayan** · **Soares-Prabhu**.
**Dalit Theology**: "the **cry** of the Dalit Christians." Rejects upper-caste Categories, uses **"little traditions"**. Crucified Jesus = Power to suffer **and to revolt**; Risen Jesus = Hope. **Suffering and Hope**. Israel = a **Dalit Group**. "He is the God of the Dalits."
**Women**: **Complementarity + Equality**. Vandana Mataji, Sara Grant. "Indian theology is badly in need of more substantial contribution from the part of women."

**Convergence**: the cultural-religious and socio-political Trends **merge**. **Dialogue of LIFE**. Inculturation **natural, not manipulated**, respecting the **smaller traditions**. The Mission is **liberative**; the **Option for the Poor never compromised**.
