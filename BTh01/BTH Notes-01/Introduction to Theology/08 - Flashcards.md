## CHAPTER 1 — WHAT IS THEOLOGY

Theology, etymologically::*Theos* (God) + *Logos* (Word, Discourse, Science) = Discourse on God, God-Talk

The three Positions on the Existence of God::A. Atheism · B. Agnosticism · C. Theism

The four Atheists named by the textbook::Albert Camus, Karl Marx, Friedrich Nietzsche, Sigmund Freud

Two non-theistic Religious Traditions::Charvaka (within Hinduism) and Theravada Buddhism

Theism conceives God as::the Ultimate Reality, the Ultimate Ground of Being, the Ultimate Horizon of Meaning

Which two Councils teach that God can be known by natural Reason::Vatican I, *Dei Filius* 2, and Vatican II, *Dei Verbum* 6 (cited CCC 36)

Newman's Verdict on the Five Ways::they are abstract and philosophical, and provide only a "convergence of diverse probabilities"

*Neti, Neti* means::"Not This, Not This" — the classical Hindu Way of saying only what God is not

Univocal, with Example::the same Word with the same Meaning: *Six* Mangoes, *Six* Books. Impossible for God-Talk

Equivocal, with Example::the same Word for unrelated Things: *Bank* of a River, *Bank* of Money. Would make Theology Nonsense

Analogical::a Word applied by Proportion and Participation. Neither Univocal nor Equivocal. Developed by Aristotle, refined by Aquinas

The Example of Analogy given in the textbook::calling God "our Father" — the earthly Notion purified of Limitation and applied in an Eminent Way

Rudolf Otto's Definition of Religious Experience::the Encounter with a **tremendous and fascinating Mystery**

Three Biblical Theophanies named::the Burning Bush (Ex 3:2-6); the Transfiguration (Mt 17:1-13); Emmaus (Lk 24:13-35)

I. T. Ramsey on Theological Language::"Theology uses ordinary language in an extraordinary way"

Definition of a Religious Symbol::the Meeting-Point between the Human and the Divine; it points beyond itself **and participates in** what it points to

The two Examples of Symbol in Chapter 1::Jesus Christ, the Sacrament of God; and the Eucharist

Anselm's Definition of Theology::*Fides quaerens intellectum* — Faith seeking Understanding

Anselm's other Formula::*Credo ut intelligam* — I believe in order to understand

Fideism::accepts everything revealed by God without any Attempt to scrutinise it by Reason

Rationalism::rejects everything that is not clearly understood by Reason

*Fides qua* versus *fides quae*::*fides qua* = Faith as Trust, the act of believing. *fides quae* = Faith as Content, what is believed

*Lex orandi, lex credendi*::the Law of Praying is the Law of Believing — the Eastern Approach, possibly coined by Prosper of Aquitaine in the fifth century

Rahner's Axiom on God and Humanity::"God and Humanity are correlative Terms" — whatever we say of God says something of us

Gutierrez's Definition::"Theology as a critical reflection on Christian praxis in the light of the Word"

The four Responses to Historical Consciousness::Nihilism, Relativism, Fundamentalism, and Hermeneutics

*Mythos*, per Jennings::"that set of symbols, rituals, narratives and beliefs which taken together announce and mediate the presence and action of the Divine in the life of a community of persons"

The eight Creative Polarities::Mystery/Intelligibility · Identity/Change · Committed/Critical · Community/Individual · One/Many · Universal/Contextual · Orthodoxy/Heterodoxy · Orthodoxy/Orthopraxis

The historical Example proving all Theology is contextual::the Books of Aquinas were **burned by the Bishop of Paris** soon after his Death

*Ekam sat viprah bahudha vadanti*::"Truth is One; the Sages call it by many Names" (RV 1:164:46) — the Sanskrit Seal on the Polarity of One and Many

*Ancilla theologiae*::the Handmaid of Theology — the classical Title of Philosophy

*Saccidananda*::*Sat* (Being) + *Chit* (Consciousness) + *Ananda* (Bliss) — used by Indian Theologians for the Trinity

The three Indian Theologians who used Vedanta::Brahmabandhav Upadhyaya, Swami Abhishiktananda, Raimundo Panikkar

The Hierarchy of Truths, and its Source::truths "vary in their relationship to the foundation of the Christian faith" (*Unitatis Redintegratio* 11); the Foundation is the Mystery of Christ

## CHAPTER 2 — THE SOURCES

The two Foundations of Theology::Faith and Revelation — two Sides of one Event

Tillich's Definition of Faith::**Ultimate Concern** — the State of being ultimately concerned

Who is called the Father of Faith, and where::Abraham, "the father of all who believe" (Rom 4:3; cf. Heb 11:8, Gen 12:1-4)

Cultural Christianity::those baptized, communicated, married and buried in the Church but with no personal Faith

Sobrino's Formula on Faith as Praxis::"To know the truth is to do the truth; to know Jesus is to follow Jesus"

The four Models of Revelation (McGrath)::Doctrine · Presence · Experience · History

Is Revelation closed::Closed in Christ, "there will be no other word than this One" (CCC 65-66). **But** its Understanding is never closed

The Reformers' Principle::*Sola Scriptura* — Scripture alone

How many Sources does Theology have::**One** Source, the Apostolic Tradition, concretely identified in **three** Realities: Scripture, Tradition, Magisterium

*Norma normans non normata*::the Norm which norms all else and is itself normed by no other Criterion — said of Sacred Scripture

Dei Verbum 10, on the three Sources::they "are so linked and joined together that **one cannot stand without the others**"

Scripture is called what, in DV 24::"the soul of sacred theology"

Tradition with capital T versus small t::**Tradition** is the one Apostolic Reality; **traditions** are its many concrete Vehicles

How many Individual Churches in the Catholic Communion::**22** — the Roman and 21 Oriental, in **six** liturgical Families: Roman, Alexandrian, Antiochian, Armenian, Chaldean, Byzantine

The two Oriental Catholic Churches of India::the **Syro-Malabar** and the **Syro-Malankara**

The four Marks of a Father of the Church::Antiquity · Orthodoxy · Holiness of Life · Recognition by the Church

The Patristic Period closes with whom::**John Damascene (749)** in the East; **Gregory the Great (604)** and **Isidore of Seville (639)** in the West

Who made the first proper Codification of Canon Law::**Gratian**, in the twelfth century

The two modern Latin Codes, and the Eastern Code::1917 and **1983** (Latin); **1990** (Code of Canons of the Eastern Churches)

The Church "breathing with two lungs"::the East and the West

The Catechism of the Catholic Church was published in::**1992**, commissioned by the 1985 Extraordinary Synod; it follows the fourfold Structure of the Roman Catechism of Trent (1566)

*Sensus fidei / sensus fidelium*::the supernatural Sense of Faith of the whole People of God, which "cannot err in matters of belief" (LG 12)

The threefold Blending required for Theologizing::the *Sensus Fidei* of the Community · the Charism of the Magisterium · the prophetic Work of the Theologian

Sources versus Resources::**Sources** are the normative, historically fixed Media of the Apostolic Tradition. **Resources** are the wider Field where God still speaks: People, Cultures, Religions, Movements, the Poor

The FABC Declaration of Taipei, 1974::the Great Religions of Asia are "significant and positive elements in the economy of God's design of salvation"

The Epistemological Privilege of the Poor::because the Poor depend wholly on God, they have a privileged Nearness to God and so a privileged Knowledge of God

## CHAPTER 3 — DEVELOPMENT AND PLURALISM

Pluralism versus Relativism::**Pluralism**: one Truth approached from many legitimate Angles. **Relativism**: no one Truth at all, only what seems true. Relativism is "a negation of realism"

The five Seedbeds of theological Pluralism::Fundamental · Speculative · Biblical · Magisterial · Contextual Theologies

The five theological Eras::Biblical · Patristic · Scholastic · Post-Scholastic · Contextual

Old Testament Theologies named::Yahwist, Elohist, Priestly, Deuteronomic, and Wisdom Theologies

Why the Gospels "developed backwards"::the earliest Preaching was the core Proclamation of Death and Resurrection; from it grew the **Passion Narrative**; only later was the Ministry Material prefixed

The three Christologies within Paul::Ascending (from his Humanity) · Descending (from his Divinity) · Cosmic (Christ and the Universe)

The three Phases of Patristic Dialogue::Apologetic · Dogmatic · Theological

The three cultural Streams of Patristic Theology::Semitic (Edessa, **Ephraem**) · Greek (Alexandria, Origen, **Cappadocians**) · Latin (Rome and Carthage, **Augustine**)

The Council that is the Milestone on Christ::**Chalcedon, 451**

Alexandrian versus Antiochene::**Alexandrian** (Cyril) stresses the **Divinity**, downplays the Humanity. **Antiochene** (Theodore of Mopsuestia) asserts the full **Humanity**, loosens the Union

Latin versus Greek Order in Trinitarian Theology::**Latins** (Augustine) begin with the One God, then the Three Persons. **Greeks** (Cappadocians) begin with the Three Persons, then the one God

The five Patriarchates::Rome, Alexandria, Antioch, Constantinople, Jerusalem

Augustine against the Donatists::the Sacrament works by **Christ**, not by the Holiness of the Minister

Augustine against Pelagius::the Priority of **Grace at every stage** of human Life

Origen's two Senses of Scripture::the **corporeal** (literal, grammatical) and the **allegorical**

The four Periods of Scholasticism::Pre 800-1050 · Early 1050-1200 · High 1200-1350 · Late 1350-1500

Gilson's Phrase for the Scholastic Systems::"cathedrals of the mind"

Primacy of Intellect versus Primacy of Will::Intellect: **Aquinas**. Will: **Bonaventure**

Who is called the Father of Scholasticism::**Anselm of Canterbury** (1033-1109)

Bernard of Clairvaux's Project of Life in one Phrase::"to know Jesus and Him crucified"

Duns Scotus on the Incarnation::it is willed by God **from all Eternity** as the supreme Manifestation of infinite Love, independent of Sin; the saved are "co-lovers"

Ockham's Razor, in Latin::*Entia non sunt multiplicanda sine necessitate* — Entities must not be multiplied without necessity

Why Ockham said Theology is not a Science::because its Principles are **not evident** but accepted from Revelation

*Via moderna* versus *via antiqua*::the Modern Way of Ockham against the Ancient Way of the earlier Scholastics

The Encyclical that began the Neo-Scholastic Revival::*Aeterni Patris*, **Leo XIII, 1879**

Maritain's three Degrees of Knowledge::scientific · metaphysical · suprarational

Marcel's two Distinctions::**Problem** (approached objectively) versus **Mystery** (not grasped from outside); and **Having** (Domination) versus **Being** (Relationship)

The three Groups that shaped Vatican II::Europeans (near other Christian Traditions) · Latin Americans (Injustice) · Asians and Africans (**multi-religious Context**)

*Theologia universalis*::the pre-Vatican II Assumption of one universal Theology applicable everywhere

The three Sensitivities of Contextual Theology::Context · Procedure (communitarian) · History

The three Roots of Contextual Theology::Gospel · Church · Culture

Bonhoeffer's Question::how to speak of God in "worldly terms", avoiding a *Deus ex machina*, the Stopgap where human Effort fails

Ecofeminism adds which Category::***naturism*** — the Oppression of the rest of Nature, linked to the Oppression of Women

*Han*, in Minjung Theology::the repressed Anger produced in the Korean People by the protracted colonial Presence

*Minjung*::"the powerless people", those on the Periphery, politically oppressed and economically deprived

## CHAPTER 4 — SEVEN MODELS

The four Ways of relating Faith and Culture::Translation · Anthropological · Synthetic · Transcendental Models

The Transcendental Model focuses on::not **what** is studied but **how** one theologizes — the Subject rather than the Content

Ephraem's Dates and Places::born about **306**; Deacon at **Nisibis**, later **Edessa**; died about **373**

Why Ephraem shuns Definitions::they **fossilize** theological Inquiry, and to define God is to try to tie down the Limitless One

Ephraem's three Attitudes to Theology::**Domination** (rejected) · **Detached Study** (problematic) · **Love and Wonder** (his own)

Ephraem's Image for Faith::**the Luminous Eye** — Faith is a Perspective, the Eye which can see hidden Things

Ephraem's ecological Insight::the Abuse of Free Will damages the whole Cosmos, since the physical and spiritual Ecospheres are linked

When did the Orthodox Churches separate from Rome::**1054**, and again after **1453**

The only significant theological Difference of the Orthodox::the Refusal of the **jurisdictional Primacy** of the Bishop of Rome

Schmemann on how Theology is best expressed::in the form of **Praise (doxology)**

Schmemann's Definition of Tradition::"the life of the Holy Spirit in the Church" — a Summons and Inspiration, not the Memory of a beautiful Past

Why the Christian is "Priest of the Cosmos"::human Beings alone can **bless God in return** for the Life they receive, offering the World back to God

Aquinas's Dates::born **Roccasecca, 1225**; Dominican **1244**; Master **1256**; died **Fossanova, 1274**, on the way to the Council of Lyons

Aquinas's Teacher::**Albert the Great**, at Paris, 1245-1248

Aquinas on Philosophy in Theology::Theology borrows not its **Principles** but only their **Method of Exposition**, using them as Handmaids

Aquinas's Argument that Theology is a Science::some Sciences take Principles from natural Reason; Theology takes its Principles from **the Science of God made known by Revelation**

*Via inventionis* versus *via doctrinae*::the Way of **Discovery** (a posteriori) versus the Way of **Exposition** (a priori). Aquinas substituted the latter, which is valuable only for those who have already travelled the former

Lonergan's two Reasons the Scholastic Development helped::**practical** (the *quaestio* resolved conflicting patristic Interpretations into *Summae*) and **epistemological** (Metaphysics answered what Logic alone could not)

What triggered the Reformation, proximately::the Preaching of **Indulgences**, done ambiguously and taken by some Reformers as literally a Sale

Barth's Description of the God of the Gospel::God exists "neither next to humans, nor merely above humans, but **with humans, by humans, and most important of all, for humans**"

Barth's four required Attitudes::**Astonishment · Concern · Commitment · Loneliness**

*Credere quod* versus *credere in*::believing **that** (assent to Propositions) versus believing **in** (a living Relationship). Barth requires the second

The chief Weakness of Barth's Model::Emphasis on Transcendence to the Detriment of the human; an **Inability to account for other Religions** except negatively

Rahner's Axiom on Philosophy::"non-philosophical theology is bad theology"

The human Being, per Rahner, is::**Spirit**, **embodied Spirit**, and **historical Spirit**

Rahner's Solution to Nature and Grace::**the Supernatural Existential** — our concrete historical State is already raised to the supernatural Level by God's gracious Call; a free Gift, yet never absent in the concrete

Why the Incarnation is not a Freak Event, per Rahner::in our very Being we are searching for "God's free epiphany in history", for an absolute Saviour

The three Questions of the Praxis Model::**From where** do Theologians speak? **Why** do they speak? **To whom** do they speak?

The Interlocutor of Liberation Theology::not the non-believer (Atheist) but **the NON-PERSON** — the poor and oppressed

The three Levels of Liberation::Social-Scientific · Philosophical-Historical · Theological. Distinct but reciprocal and inseparable

The two CDF Instructions on Liberation Theology::*Certain Aspects of the Theology of Liberation* (**6 August 1984**) and *Christian Freedom and Liberation* (**22 March 1986**)

Abhishiktananda's civil Name and Dates::**Henri Le Saux**, 1910-1973; Benedictine of Kergonan; came to India 1948; **Shantivanam** with Monchanin

Abhishiktananda on the Advaita of India::it is "the final Preparation for the Gospel", for it disposes human Beings to experience Revelation

"The cave of the heart"::the Locus of the Indian Experience of the Mystery, requiring a *habitus* of Recollection and Contemplation

The Advaitic Night::the Stage where all sense of Individuality is swallowed up in God; from it one awakens to divine Sonship and the cry "Abba"

*Christophany*::Creation rediscovered, after spiritual Transformation, as already within the Fullness of Christ

Bede Griffiths' Objection to Abhishiktananda::he failed to keep the Balance between the rational and intuitive Faculties, diving into the intuitive **without the Accompaniment of Reason**

## CHAPTER 5 — THE CRITERIA

Eckhart's Rule of Method::"a sensible way to discover what an enterprise means is to look at what its practitioners do"

The twofold Demand of Evangelii Nuntiandi 65::foster theological Expression suited to differing Milieux; **but** the Content of Faith must be "neither impaired nor mutilated"

Panikkar's rhetorical Question::**"What is the criterion of the criterion?"**

The three Greek Words for the Church as Sacrament::*martyria* (Witness) · *leitourgia* (Liturgy) · *diakonia* (Service, Mt 25)

The three Elements of *paradosis* in the Patristic Period::a Deposit handed on · a living Teaching Authority · a Transmission by Succession

On fixing the Canon, the crucial Formula::not that an Act of the Church gave Authority to what was apostolic, but **what was apostolic was received** and interpreted authentically in the Church

The three Criteria for interpreting Scripture (DV 12)::attend to the **Content and Unity** of the whole of Scripture · read within the **living Tradition** · attend to the **Analogy of Faith**

Tillard's Image for Tradition::**Memory** — "the humus from which life never stops borrowing"

The three Witnesses (monuments) of Tradition::the **Liturgy** · the **Writings of the Fathers** · the ordinary **Expressions of Christian Life**

*Sobornost*::the Orthodox Concept: Communion in an Organism at once one and multi-personal, **spiritual rather than juridical** in nature

The Orthodox Principle on the Origin of Authority::**Synodality** — Authority must emerge **out of** the Communion of Churches, not be exercised **over** them

*Solus Deus* and *Sola Scriptura*::it was to restore the Sovereignty of **God Alone** that the Reformers proposed **Scripture Alone**

Scripture sufficient "materially" and "formally"::**materially** = the Object of Faith, containing the whole of Faith. **formally** = the Means whereby we know, the Principle of the Rule of Faith

Dulles's three Objections to Sola Scriptura::the Bible **did not collect itself** · the Bible is **not self-interpreting** · to accept no Authority but the Bible **rejects part of the Bible's own Teaching**

The Pluralism of Authority in the Church::the Bible · the Magisterium · the whole Church as Body of Believers · Pastors and Laity · individual Conscience

The Pope in relation to the College::**both Head and Member**. Without the Pope there is no College

The Magisterium in relation to the Word of God::"not superior to the Word of God, but **its servant**" (DV 10)

The two Forms of the Extraordinary Magisterium::the whole College in an **Ecumenical Council**; and the Pope defining ***ex cathedra***

Dogma, defined::a propositional Formulation that is **divinely revealed** and **proposed as such** by the Magisterium

Doctrine, defined::broader than Dogma — any authoritative or normative Formulation of the Belief of the Church, **whether revealed or not**

Does Doctrine end Discussion::No. A Doctrine tends toward **Clarity**, not Comprehensiveness. "Theological reflection will always be more comprehensive than doctrine"

The four Gradations of Church Doctrine::Definitive Dogma · Definitive Doctrine · Non-Definitive Authoritative Doctrine · Prudential Admonitions

Which Gradation holds most Church Teaching::the third — **Non-Definitive Authoritative Doctrine**

The four Responses owed::Assent of Faith · Firm Acceptance · Religious Submission of Intellect and Will · Obedience to the Spirit of the Law

The Twofold Mediation of the Theologian::toward the **Magisterium** (communicate its Teaching clearly) and toward the **People of God** (discern their Insights and give them systematic Expression)

The Theologian stands where::**"between a creed and a quest"**

The eight Parameters of the Catholic Theological Enterprise::Knowability of God · Faith and Reason as Friends · Universality · Communitarian Context · Catholicity in Time · Sacramental Principle · Scripture within Tradition · Role of Authority

## CHAPTER 6 — THE INDIAN CONTEXT

When did St. Thomas reach India::**52 AD**, on the South Indian coast with Arabian Traders

The Formula describing the St. Thomas Christians::"**Hindu in culture, Christian in faith and Syrian in worship**"

Why the St. Thomas Christians became Syrian in Worship::a Colony of Christian Immigrants from **Persia in the fourth century** brought their Clergy and Bishops

How many Churches now descend from the St. Thomas Christians::**eight**, all Oriental in character

When did the first Division occur, and why::**1665** — one Group affiliated to the **Patriarch of Antioch** (the Jacobites) after Conflict with Portuguese Missionaries who tried to **Romanize and Latinize** the ancient Church

The Syro-Malankara Catholic Church was formed::in **1930**, when a Group led by **Mar Ivanios** and **Mar Theophilus** reunited with Rome, keeping the Antiochene Liturgy

Who is called the Apostle of Latin Christianity in India::**St. Francis Xavier**, who arrived in Goa in **1542**

When was the Indian Hierarchy established::**1886**

The three Catholic Individual Churches of India::**Syro-Malabar · Latin · Syro-Malankara**

When was the CBCI established, and when restructured::established **1944**; restructured into three Episcopal Bodies in **1988**, after the Letter of John Paul II in 1987

CSI and CNI::**Church of South India, 1947**, uniting four Churches. **Church of North India, 1970**, uniting seven

What percentage of India is Christian::about **2.5%** — roughly 25 million

The greatest Challenge of the Indian Churches::that "**the Gospel has not yet fully encountered the Indian genius**" — the Fecundation of Gospel and Indian Culture

The Cosmotheandric Vision::Reality as one, with a triple Dimension: **Cosmic, Divine and Human**, inseparable

The four *Purusharthas*::*artha* (Wealth) · *kama* (Pleasure) · *dharma* (Righteousness) · *moksha* (Liberation)

The four *Ashramas*::*brahmachari* (Student) · *grahastha* (Householder) · *vanaprastha* (Forest-Dweller) · *sanyasi* (Renouncer)

Education in the Indian Tradition is::not a Process of gathering Information but **a Process of Transformation**; education as *sadhana*

What percentage of India is Adivasi::about **8%**. Tribal Religions are *sui generis* and **not part of Hindu Religion**

The Hindu Conception of History::not linear like a flowing River but **like a Pool of Water**: Ripples appear, the Pool remains unchanged

The three *margas*::*jnana* (Knowledge) · *bhakti* (Devotion) · *karma* (selfless Action)

Indian Secularism, defined::**not** the Negation of God or Religion, but the **equal Treatment of all Religions** by the State, with Freedom to believe, practise and propagate

The six classical Schools of Indian Philosophy::Nyaya · Vaisheshika · Sankhya · Yoga · Mimamsa · **Vedanta**

Shankara's Teaching in one Line::only **Brahman** is truly real; *atman* is identical with Brahman; realizing that Identity is Salvation

Who qualified Advaita Vedanta::**Ramanuja, Madhva, Vallabha** — the Absolute as a **personal Deity**, the Soul **distinct** from the Supreme Being though part of the divine Essence

Why the St. Thomas Christians produced little Indian Theology::Isolation · Dependence on East Syrians · **easy Accommodation to the Caste System**, which blunted their prophetic Ministry · lack of theological Training

The two Positions of the St. Thomas Christians condemned at Diamper (1599) but vindicated today::that **each may be saved in one's own Religion**; and that the **Latin Form is not the only true Form** ("one law of St. Thomas and another of St. Peter")

What did Robert de Nobili do::studied **Tamil and Sanskrit**, lived as a **Brahmin *sanyasi*** with holy Thread, *kudumi* and saffron; the **first serious Attempt** to reformulate Christianity in Indian Categories

Why did de Nobili's Approach stop::stiff Opposition from his own Confreres and from Roman Authorities

Brahmo Samaj and New Dispensation::**Ram Mohan Roy, 1828** founded the Brahmo Samaj; **Keshab Chandra Sen, 1881** founded the New Dispensation (*Navavidhan*)

Who wrote *The Oriental Christ*, and when::**Pratap Chandra Mozoomdar, 1883**

Who began Indian Theology, Catholics or Protestants::the pioneering Work was **first initiated mainly by Protestant Christians**

Upadhyaya's Argument::as the Fathers used Greek Philosophy for the Hellenistic World, **we in India must use Vedanta** to make Christian Faith understandable

Chenchaiah's Christology::Christ as the **New Creation, the New Man**; all should become "other Christs" through **integral Yoga**

Chakkarai's Contribution::Christ explained as *avatara*; the **Holy Spirit as the Continuation of the Incarnation**, the living Christ today

The Catholic Ashram Pioneers::**Monchanin, Abhishiktananda, Bede Griffiths, Francis Acharya, Vandana, Sara Grant, Amalorpavadass**

The Theology of Dialogue in one Rule::**first be rooted in your own Faith**; then dive deep into the Depth of other Religions; genuine Encounter makes you **more aware of the Uniqueness of your own**

India's Rank on Social Development, 1997::**128 out of 174** Countries (UN Human Development Report, 1997)

What does the Constitution guarantee in Article 25::Freedom of Conscience, and the Right to **profess, practise AND propagate** one's Religion

What does Hindutva demand::a **Hindu Rashtra** with one homogeneous Culture, reducing Minorities to **second class Citizens** or branding them "foreigners"

Women in the 13th Parliament::**47 out of 543**, about **8.5%**

The 73rd Amendment gave Women::**33% Reservation** in local Panchayat Elections, where they "proved their mettle by their committed action"

Dalits and Tribals together are what percentage::**22.5%** of the total Population

The Etymology of "Dalit"::from the Root ***dal***, "to break", "to cut", "to oppress" — the **oppressed People**

The two Leaders of the Dalit Liberation Movement::**Mahatma Jotirao Phule** (nineteenth century) and **Babasaheb Ambedkar** (twentieth century)

The Double Discrimination of Dalit Christians::**the State** denies them the Rights given to other Dalits because they became Christian; **the Church** in certain Sections still denies them equal Treatment

The Critique of the earlier Indian Theology::it followed a **Translation Model** using the Categories of the **upper Castes**, while the vast Majority are *sudras*, Dalits and Tribals. **Whom should Theology address?**

The Formula on Humanization::"**Authentic humanization is at the same time divinization**"

Who first articulated the "New Humanity" Theme in India::**Paul D. Devanandan** (1901-1962); developed further by **M.M. Thomas** of the Mar Thoma Church

The three Indian Liberation Theologians::**Sebastian Kappen, Samuel Rayan, George M. Soares-Prabhu**

Why an Indian Liberation Theology is unique (Kappen)::the **religiously pluralistic Context** calls for Collaboration with People of all Faiths and the use of **all their Resources, not merely Christian Resources**

Kappen's Starting Point::a new Indian **Christology or "Jesuology"**, placing the prophetic Protest of Christ in Continuity with Indian Movements of Dissent, along the line of the **Buddha and the Bhakti Tradition**

Dalit Theology, defined::"the **cry of the Dalit Christians** by which they try to express their Faith in the midst of their Agonies" — a Dalit Version of Liberation Theology

The Texture of Dalit Theology::**Suffering and Hope**. The Crucified Jesus gives Power to suffer and to revolt; the Risen Jesus gives Hope

The Dalit Re-reading of the Bible::**Israel was an oppressed People, a Dalit Group**; the Church at its Origins was a **Jesus Movement** of the little ones. "He is the God of the Dalits"

The two Principles required in Women's Theology::**Complementarity** (male and female Perspectives alone are incomplete and liable to Distortion) and **Equality**

The two Women Theologians named::**Vandana Mataji** and **Sara Grant**, writing from the Ashram-Life Point of View, calling for **a Theology from the Heart**

The Convergence in Indian Theology today::the **cultural-religious** Trend and the **socio-economic-political** Trend have merged; an integrated Approach is emerging

The Shift in Interreligious Dialogue::from the Dialogue of Doctrines to **the Dialogue of LIFE**

The two Corrections to older Inculturation::respect the **"smaller cultures and traditions"** of the marginalized; and let Inculturation be **natural, not manipulated or artificially created**

The Indian theological Journals named::*Jeevadhara · Vidyajyoti · The Living Word · Journal of Dharma · Bible Bhashyam*
