# 📖 BTh Notes 01 — Vault Home

Bachelor of Theology study vault. Index of units below.

## Units
### Fundamental Theology
- 🔥 [[Introduction to Theology - Course Home|Introduction to Theology]] — the foundational BTh course, on Pathil & Veliath, *An Introduction to Theology* (Indian Theological Series, CBCI). **Start here if you are new to the BTh.** Full unit: the God-Question and analogy, the sevenfold definition and creative polarities, Scripture-Tradition-Magisterium and the Resources, the five historical eras, seven models of theologizing, the criteria of authentic Catholic theology, and theologizing in the Indian context. Leitmotif: the Burning Bush (Ex 3).

### Dogmatic Theology
- 🕊️ [[Theology of the Trinity - Course Home|Theology of the Trinity]] — *De Deo Trino*: one God, three persons. Full unit: biblical foundations, councils, systematic concepts, contemporary theology, heresies, exam prep, key figures, scripture dossier, mind maps, flashcards, revision sheet.

### Church History
- 🏛️ [[Vatican II - Course Home|Vatican II]] — the Second Vatican Council (1962–65). **Assumes no prior knowledge.** Full unit: what a council is, the pre-conciliar Church, how the Council ran, the four Constitutions one by one, the other twelve documents, turning points, reception disputes, Indian context, mind maps, flashcards, revision sheet, glossary + exam prep.

### Theologians
- 🧠 [[Karl Rahner - Course Home|Karl Rahner]] — transcendental method, supernatural existential, *Realsymbol*, Rahner's Rule, anonymous Christians, the World Church. 23 notes + knowledge graph.

## How the units connect
- **Intro to Theology ↔ everything** — this unit is the gateway. It defines theology, its sources, its criteria, and the Indian context the other units presuppose.
- **Intro to Theology ↔ Rahner** — the Transcendental Model and the *supernatural existential* in [[04 - Selected Models of Theology]]
- **Intro to Theology ↔ Vatican II** — *Dei Verbum*, *Lumen Gentium* and *Gaudium et Spes* are cited throughout [[02 - The Sources of Theology]] and [[05 - The Criteria of Christian Theological Reflection]]
- **Vatican II ↔ Rahner** — Rahner was a *peritus* at the Council and shaped *Lumen Gentium*: [[15 - Rahner and Vatican II - The World Church]]
- **Vatican II ↔ Rahner (religions)** — *Nostra Aetate* and [[11 - Anonymous Christians and the Non-Christian Religions]]
- **Indian context** — [[11 - Vatican II in the Indian Context]] and [[18 - Rahner in the Indian Context]]
- **Councils** — Nicaea/Constantinople in [[02 - Historical Development and the Councils]]; Vatican II in [[01 - What a Council Is and Why Vatican II Happened]]
