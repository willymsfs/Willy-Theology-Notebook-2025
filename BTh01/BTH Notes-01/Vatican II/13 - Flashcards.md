# 13 — Flashcards

> [!info] Spaced Repetition
> `::` separates question from answer. Each note in this unit also carries its own Recall section; this is the consolidated deck for daily review.

## Dates and facts

Vatican II ran from::11 October 1962 to 8 December 1965
Vatican II is the::21st ecumenical council
Announced on::25 January 1959, by John XXIII, at St Paul Outside the Walls
Opening speech::*Gaudet Mater Ecclesia*, 11 October 1962
John XXIII died::3 June 1963
Paul VI elected::21 June 1963
Number of sessions::four — 1962, 1963, 1964, 1965
Number of bishops::about 2,500
Working language::Latin
Total documents::16 — 4 constitutions, 9 decrees, 3 declarations
Majority required to pass a text::two-thirds

## The four Constitutions

*Sacrosanctum Concilium*::Constitution on the Sacred Liturgy, 4 Dec 1963 — first document
*Lumen Gentium*::Dogmatic Constitution on the Church, 21 Nov 1964
*Dei Verbum*::Dogmatic Constitution on Divine Revelation, 18 Nov 1965
*Gaudium et Spes*::Pastoral Constitution on the Church in the Modern World, 7 Dec 1965 — last document

## Latin terms

*Aggiornamento*::updating — bringing the Church up to the present
*Ressourcement*::return to the sources — Scripture, Fathers, ancient liturgy
*Participatio actuosa*::full, conscious and active participation (SC 14)
*Culmen et fons*::summit and font — the liturgy (SC 10)
*Subsistit in*::"subsists in" — LG 8, the Church of Christ subsists in the Catholic Church
*Peritus / periti*::expert theological adviser(s) at the Council
*Placet / non placet*::yes / no in conciliar voting
*Placet iuxta modum*::yes with reservations — conditional approval carrying an amendment
*Nota praevia explicativa*::the explanatory note on collegiality attached to LG by Paul VI, Nov 1964
*Societas perfecta*::the pre-conciliar self-image of the Church as a complete, self-sufficient society
*Sensus fidei*::the supernatural instinct of the whole body of the faithful (LG 12)
*Schema / schemata*::draft text(s) prepared for conciliar debate
*Anathema sit*::"let him be cut off" — the condemnation formula Vatican II never used

## People

John XXIII::called the Council; *Gaudet Mater Ecclesia*; withdrew *De fontibus*
Paul VI::completed the Council; *Nota praevia*; *Humanae Vitae* 1968
Cardinal Ottaviani::Holy Office; leader of the conservative minority; *Semper idem*
Cardinal Bea::Secretariat for Christian Unity; behind UR, NA, DH
Cardinal Liénart::stopped the commission elections, 13 Oct 1962
Cardinal Frings::seconded Liénart; Ratzinger was his *peritus*
Cardinal Suenens::proposed what became *Gaudium et Spes*
Karl Rahner::*peritus*; major influence on *Lumen Gentium*
Yves Congar::*peritus*; ecclesiology, ecumenism; previously disciplined
Henri de Lubac::*peritus*; *ressourcement*, later critical of GS's optimism
Joseph Ratzinger::young *peritus* to Frings; later Benedict XVI; 2005 two hermeneutics
John Courtney Murray::principal drafter of *Dignitatis Humanae*
Marcel Lefebvre::rejected the Council, chiefly DH; founded SSPX; excommunicated 1988
Giuseppe Alberigo::Bologna School; *History of Vatican II*; Council as *event*
John O'Malley::*What Happened at Vatican II*; the argument from style and genre
D. S. Amalorpavadass::NBCLC Bangalore; Indian liturgical and catechetical renewal
George M. Soares-Prabhu::Indian biblical hermeneutics
Bede Griffiths::Christian ashram movement — Kurisumala, Shantivanam

## Concepts

Two-source theory::Scripture and Tradition as two separate sources — rejected by *Dei Verbum*
Hierarchy of truths::doctrines differ in their relation to the foundation of faith (UR 11)
Collegiality::bishops as a college with the Pope as head, holding supreme authority together
Universal call to holiness::all the faithful of every rank called to the perfection of charity
Signs of the times::events of the age, to be read and interpreted in light of the Gospel
Hermeneutic of reform::continuity of the Church as subject, with genuine development (Benedict XVI, 2005)
Hermeneutic of rupture::the Council as a break beginning a new Church
"Spirit of the Council"::appeal beyond the texts — legitimate only if anchorable in a paragraph
Inculturation::rooting the faith in a local culture's own forms — SC 37-40, AG 22, GS 58
Twelve Points of Adaptation::the 1969 Indian liturgical adaptations approved for India
All India Seminar::Bangalore, May 1969 — "The Church in India Today"
Dalit critique of inculturation::Sanskritic forms may baptise caste; most Indian Catholics are Dalit
Four forms of dialogue::life, action, theological exchange, religious experience
