## 4. Chronology

```mermaid
timeline
    title Vatican II and after
    1959 : John XXIII announces the Council (25 Jan)
    1962 : Session I opens 11 Oct : Lienart stops elections : De fontibus withdrawn
    1963 : John XXIII dies, Paul VI elected : Session II : Sacrosanctum Concilium
    1964 : Session III : Lumen Gentium : Black Week
    1965 : Session IV : Dei Verbum, Gaudium et Spes : Council closes 8 Dec
    1968 : Humanae Vitae
    1969 : Missal of Paul VI : All India Seminar, Bangalore
    2005 : Benedict XVI - two hermeneutics
    2007 : Summorum Pontificum
    2021 : Traditionis Custodes : Synod on Synodality opens
```
