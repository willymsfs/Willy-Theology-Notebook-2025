# 15 — Glossary, Exam Questions, Bibliography

## Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Aggiornamento** | "Updating." Making the Church's expression and structures able to reach the present age. |
| **Anathema sit** | "Let him be cut off." The condemnation formula of earlier councils. Vatican II used none. |
| **Anjali hasta** | Folded-hands gesture, adopted as an Indian liturgical posture (1969 Twelve Points). |
| **Collegiality** | The bishops as a college, successors of the apostolic college, with the Pope as head, together holding supreme authority (LG 22). |
| **Constitution** | Highest category of conciliar document. Four at Vatican II. |
| **Culmen et fons** | "Summit and font" — the liturgy in relation to the Church's whole activity (SC 10). |
| **Declaration** | A conciliar statement of position on a question facing the world. Three at Vatican II. |
| **Decree** | A conciliar document giving practical direction to a group or activity. Nine at Vatican II. |
| **Deicide** | The charge of "God-killing" historically levelled at the Jewish people; repudiated by NA 4. |
| **Ecumenical council** | Worldwide assembly of bishops, convoked and confirmed by the Pope. |
| **Hierarchy of truths** | Catholic doctrines differ in their relation to the foundation of the faith (UR 11). |
| **Inculturation** | Rooting the faith in the forms of a local culture (SC 37–40, AG 22, GS 58). |
| **Nota praevia explicativa** | "Preliminary explanatory note" on collegiality, attached to LG by Paul VI, Nov 1964. |
| **Participatio actuosa** | Full, conscious and active participation of the faithful in the liturgy (SC 14). |
| **Peritus / periti** | Expert theological adviser(s); drafted the texts but had no vote. |
| **Placet / non placet** | "It pleases" / "it does not please" — yes/no in conciliar voting. |
| **Placet iuxta modum** | "Yes, with reservations" — a conditional approval submitted with an amendment. |
| **Ressourcement** | "Return to the sources" — Scripture, the Fathers, the ancient liturgy. |
| **Schema** | A draft text prepared for conciliar debate. |
| **Sensus fidei / sensus fidelium** | The supernatural instinct of the whole body of the faithful, which cannot err in universal agreement (LG 12). |
| **Societas perfecta** | The pre-conciliar image of the Church as a complete, self-sufficient hierarchical society. |
| **Subsistit in** | "Subsists in" — the Church of Christ subsists in the Catholic Church, with real ecclesial elements outside (LG 8). |
| **Versus populum** | Celebration facing the people. Widely adopted after the Council; **not mentioned in SC**. |

## Sample exam questions

**Short answer**
1. When did Vatican II open and close, and under which popes?
2. Name the four constitutions with their dates and subjects.
3. Distinguish *aggiornamento* from *ressourcement*.
4. What does *placet iuxta modum* mean, and why did it matter procedurally?
5. State what *Nostra Aetate* 4 says about the Jewish people.
6. What are the Twelve Points of Adaptation, and when were they approved?

**Paragraph**
7. Explain the significance of the change from *est* to *subsistit in* at LG 8.
8. Why did *Lumen Gentium* place "The People of God" before "The Hierarchy"?
9. Explain the qualifying clause of DV 11 on inerrancy.
10. What is meant by "reading the signs of the times", and what method does it imply?
11. Why is *Dignitatis Humanae* the hardest document for a continuity reading?

**Essay**
12. "Vatican II changed nothing in Catholic doctrine, and everything in Catholic life." Discuss.
13. Assess the claim that the Council's documents are compromise texts, and evaluate the consequences for their interpretation.
14. Compare the hermeneutic of rupture with the hermeneutic of reform in continuity. Which better accounts for the evidence?
15. "*Sacrosanctum Concilium* did not produce the liturgy we have." Examine.
16. Evaluate the Indian reception of Vatican II, giving due weight to both the inculturation and the liberation trajectories.
17. Was *Gaudium et Spes* too optimistic? Engage with its theological critics.
18. To what extent does the Synod on Synodality implement *Lumen Gentium*'s teaching on collegiality?

> [!tip] Essay technique for this subject
> Almost every good answer has the same shape: **(1)** state what the pre-conciliar position was, **(2)** quote the conciliar text precisely with its paragraph number, **(3)** name what changed and what did not, **(4)** give the strongest objection, **(5)** judge. Skipping (4) is what separates a mid answer from a strong one.

## Bibliography

**Primary — the documents themselves**
- The 16 documents of Vatican II. Available in full on vatican.va; also in your Notion hub 🙏 *Vatican II & Faith Studies*, several with teaching guides attached.
- Recommended editions: Austin Flannery OP (ed.), *Vatican Council II: The Conciliar and Post Conciliar Documents*; Norman Tanner SJ (ed.), *Decrees of the Ecumenical Councils*.

**History of the Council**
- John W. O'Malley, *What Happened at Vatican II* (Harvard, 2008) — the best single-volume account; the argument from style.
- Giuseppe Alberigo (ed.), *History of Vatican II*, 5 vols — the Bologna School's monumental narrative.
- Agostino Marchetto, *The Second Vatican Ecumenical Council: A Counterpoint for the History of the Council* — the principal critique of Alberigo.
- **Ralph M. Wiltgen, *The Rhine Flows into the Tiber: The Inside Story of Vatican II*** — journalist's firsthand account of the Council's internal politics. **Pending in this unit** — see the note below.

**Interpretation**
- Benedict XVI, *Address to the Roman Curia*, 22 December 2005 — the two hermeneutics. Short; read it directly.
- Joseph Ratzinger, *Theological Highlights of Vatican II* (1966) — contemporary commentary by a *peritus*.
- Yves Congar, *My Journal of the Council* — diary of a leading *peritus*.

**Indian context**
- D. S. Amalorpavadass, writings on Indian liturgical and catechetical renewal (NBCLC, Bangalore).
- *The Church in India Today* — proceedings of the All India Seminar, Bangalore 1969.
- George M. Soares-Prabhu, *Collected Writings* — Indian biblical hermeneutics.
- Raimon Panikkar, on Hindu–Christian encounter.
- Dalit theology: writings of Arvind P. Nirmal and successors.

**In your own collection (Notion)**
- *Vatican II: The Battle for Meaning* — 9-chapter study guide, 🏛️ Vatican II & Theology hub. The advanced companion to this unit.
- *Council Speeches of Vatican II* (Küng, Congar, O'Hanlon).
- *The Inside Story of Vatican II* (Wiltgen).

> [!warning] Two verification notes
> **1.** Vote tallies for individual documents are widely cited but vary slightly between sources. Check O'Malley or Tanner before quoting an exact figure in written work.
>
> **2.** This unit was built from the conciliar documents and standard conciliar history. The **Wiltgen layer is not yet incorporated** — his firsthand reporting on the Council's internal politics, the transalpine alliance, and the drafting battles. When added it will chiefly deepen [[03 - How the Council Ran]] and [[09 - The Turning Points]].
