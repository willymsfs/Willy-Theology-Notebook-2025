# 14 — One-Page Revision Sheet

> [!tip] The night before
> Everything essential, nothing else.

## The spine

**21st ecumenical council · 11 Oct 1962 – 8 Dec 1965 · 4 sessions · 2 popes · ~2,500 bishops · 16 documents**

Announced 25 Jan 1959 by **John XXIII**. Opened with *Gaudet Mater Ecclesia*. He died 3 June 1963; **Paul VI** completed it.

**The unique feature:** no anathemas, no condemnations, no new dogma. Pastoral, not condemnatory.

**The method:** *ressourcement* (back to the sources) **in order to achieve** *aggiornamento* (updating). Both together — never one alone.

**John XXIII's licensing sentence:** the deposit of faith is one thing; the manner of its expression is another.

## The four Constitutions — memorise this block

| | Date | Core idea | Key § |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SC** Liturgy | 4 Dec 1963 | *participatio actuosa* by right of baptism | 10, 14, 36, 37–40 |
| **LG** Church | 21 Nov 1964 | People of God before hierarchy; *subsistit in*; collegiality | 1, 8, 12, 21, 22, 40 |
| **DV** Revelation | 18 Nov 1965 | God reveals **himself**; one wellspring, two channels | 2, 9, 10, 11, 12, 21 |
| **GS** World | 7 Dec 1965 | Signs of the times; Christ reveals man to man | 1, 4, 16, 22, 36, 48, 80 |

Mnemonic: **Liturgy, Church, Revelation, World** — worship, ourselves, God's word, everyone else.

## The three documents that changed relationships

- **UR** *Unitatis Redintegratio* — separated brethren; **hierarchy of truths** (11)
- **NA** *Nostra Aetate* — nothing true and holy is rejected (2); **deicide charge repudiated** (4)
- **DH** *Dignitatis Humanae* — religious freedom grounded in **the person**, not in truth (2)

## Six turning points

1. **13 Oct 1962** — Liénart & Frings stop the commission elections → bishops take control
2. **Nov 1962** — John XXIII withdraws *De fontibus* → prepared texts are vulnerable
3. **June 1963** — John XXIII dies; Paul VI continues the Council
4. **1963–64** — People of God moved *before* Hierarchy in LG
5. **Nov 1964** — **Black Week**: *Nota praevia*, religious liberty deferred, UR amended
6. **1964–65** — contraception & celibacy reserved to the Pope → *Humanae Vitae* 1968

## Interpretation

| Rupture | Reform in continuity | Rejection |
|---|---|---|
| Bologna School, Alberigo; the **event** exceeds the texts | **Benedict XVI, 22 Dec 2005**; continuity of subject, reform of expression | Lefebvre, SSPX; DH contradicts prior teaching |

Test for "the spirit of the Council": **can you cite a paragraph?**

## India

- **Doors:** SC 37–40 · NA 2 · GS 58 · AG 22
- **Events:** All India Seminar, Bangalore **1969**; NBCLC; **Twelve Points of Adaptation 1969**
- **Two tracks:** *inculturation* (Indian religious tradition) **and** *liberation* (Dalit, caste, poverty)
- **The critique:** Sanskritic inculturation may baptise caste — whose India is being inculturated?

## Five sentences worth quoting verbatim

1. "The joys and the hopes, the griefs and the anxieties of the people of this age, especially those who are poor or in any way afflicted, these too are the joys and hopes... of the followers of Christ." — **GS 1**
2. "This Church... subsists in the Catholic Church... although many elements of sanctification and of truth are found outside her visible confines." — **LG 8**
3. "It pleased God, in his goodness and wisdom, to reveal **himself**." — **DV 2**
4. "The Catholic Church rejects nothing that is true and holy in these religions." — **NA 2**
5. "This teaching office is not above the word of God, but serves it." — **DV 10**

## Traps

- ❌ "Vatican II abolished Latin" → SC 36 **preserves** Latin
- ❌ "Vatican II turned the altars around" → *versus populum* appears **nowhere** in SC
- ❌ "Vatican II changed doctrine" → it changed **expression, emphasis, discipline, pastoral practice**
- ❌ "All 16 documents carry equal weight" → constitution > decree > declaration
- ❌ "*Subsistit in* means all churches are equal" → fullness remains in the Catholic Church
- ❌ Treating Indian reception as inculturation only → name the Dalit track too
