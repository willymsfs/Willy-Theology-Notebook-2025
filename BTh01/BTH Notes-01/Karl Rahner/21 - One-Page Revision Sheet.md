# 21 — One-Page Revision Sheet

## THE MAN
**Karl Rahner SJ, 1904–1984.** German Jesuit. Freiburg (Heidegger's seminars) → Innsbruck → Munich → Münster. *Geist in Welt* rejected 1936, published 1939. Pre-censored by Rome 1962; *peritus* at Vatican II months later. 23 volumes of *Theological Investigations*. Died 30 March 1984, weeks after defending Gutiérrez.

## THE ONE IDEA
> **A being attains itself by expressing itself in another.**
> Self-expression is not loss of self but the road to self. God does this eternally (Word), historically (flesh), ecclesially (Church), sacramentally (sacraments), personally (body, heart).

## THE CORE TEXT
"The Theology of the Symbol," *TI* IV, 221–252 (1959). Occasion: justifying devotion to the **Sacred Heart**.
- **Thesis 1:** "All beings are by their very nature symbolic, because they necessarily express themselves in order to attain their own nature."
- **Thesis 2:** "The symbol strictly speaking is the self-realisation of a being in the other, which is constitutive of its essence."
- ***Realsymbol*** (reality present; could not be otherwise) vs ***Vertretungssymbol*** (conventional; reality absent).
- **Never say "only a symbol."** A real symbol is the **maximal** presence of a reality outside itself.

## THE FIVE APPLICATIONS
| # | Symbol | of | Symbolised |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Word / Logos | → | the Father |
| 2 | The humanity of Jesus | → | the Logos |
| 3 | The Church (*Grundsakrament*) | → | the risen Christ |
| 4 | The seven sacraments | → | the Church's own essence |
| 5 | The body, the heart | → | the person |

## PHILOSOPHY IN FOUR TERMS
- **Conversio ad phantasma** — no human knowing without the sense image → knowing is incarnate.
- **Vorgriff auf esse** — unthematic reach toward Being in every judgement; God as **horizon**, never object.
- **Potentia oboedientialis** — positive constitutive openness to a possible word of God; fulfilment unowed.
- **Transcendental method** — ask not *what do we know* but *what must the knower be*.

## GRACE IN FOUR TERMS
- **Supernatural existential** — universal + unowed modification of concrete existence orienting all to God. Solves nature/grace: gratuity lies in the **freedom of the giver**, not the rarity of the gift.
- ***Natura pura*** survives only as a *Restbegriff* (remainder concept).
- **Self-communication** — God gives **Godself**; uncreated grace primary, created grace its consequence.
- **Quasi-formal causality** — God determines the graced person from within *as* a formal cause, without composition, without pantheism, freely.
- **Three modalities of one self-communication:** grace (all) · Incarnation (one, irrevocably) · glory (unveiled).

## TRINITY
**Rahner's Rule:** *"The economic Trinity is the immanent Trinity, and the immanent Trinity is the economic Trinity."* (*The Trinity*, 22)
- Half (a) economic→immanent: **accepted**.
- Half (b) "vice versa": **disputed** — endangers divine freedom (Molnar, Kasper).
- Prefers **"three distinct manners of subsisting"** to "three persons"; one consciousness, one will → **modalism charge** (Moltmann).
- Diagnosis: Christians are practically "mere monotheists."

## CHRISTOLOGY
- Humanity of Jesus = *Realsymbol* of the Logos → against every "God in disguise" picture.
- **Direct proportion axiom:** nearness to God and creaturely autonomy vary **directly** → Jesus is the *most* free and *most* human.
- **Evolutionary Christology:** active self-transcendence → hominisation → Incarnation as the world's unsurpassable openness fulfilled.
- **Transcendental Christology** (a priori idea of an absolute saviour) then **a posteriori** identified with Jesus. Does *not* deduce Christ.
- **Searching Christology:** absolute love of neighbour · readiness for death · hope in the future.

## RELIGIONS — THE FOUR THESES
1. Christianity is the absolute religion.
2. A non-Christian religion may be **lawful**, containing supernatural grace, until the Gospel genuinely confronts it.
3. The non-Christian may be an **anonymous Christian**.
4. The Church is the **vanguard**, not the boundary; mission makes explicit, it does not import grace.
→ Fruit: *LG* 16, *GS* 22, *NA* 2. Pressure: *Dominus Iesus* (2000) on Thesis 2.

## CHURCH, SACRAMENT, LAST THINGS
- *Ursakrament* = Christ · *Grundsakrament* = Church · sacraments = the Church's self-realisation for the individual.
- *Significando causant*: signifying and causing are **one act**.
- **Diaspora Church**, "little flock" → hence: *"The Christian of the future will be a mystic… or will cease to be anything at all."*
- **Death** = passion + definitive act; soul becomes **pancosmic**, not acosmic.
- **Eschatology** = present grace in the future tense, **not** advance reportage. Distinguish from **apocalyptic**.

## VATICAN II
*Peritus* to **Cardinal König**. Influence, **not authorship**. *LG* 1 & 16, *DV* 2, *GS* 22, *NA* 2, *SC*.
**Three epochs:** Jewish Christianity (→ Council of Jerusalem) · Hellenistic-European (→ Vatican II) · **World Church** (1962–).

## CRITIQUES — TEN NAMES, TEN CHARGES
| Critic | Charge |
|---|---|
| **Gilson / Fabro** | Not Thomism; the "anthropological turn" inverts theological order |
| **Kantians** | The *Vorgriff* licenses no inference to an actual God |
| **Marion** | Onto-theology — God within the horizon of being |
| **Metz** | Abstract, unhistorical, apolitical subject; needs *memoria passionis* |
| **Kerr** | Pre-linguistic, quasi-Cartesian self; fails after Wittgenstein |
| **Lindbeck** | Experiential-expressivism; religions *form*, not express, experience |
| **Balthasar** | Anthropological reduction; blunts the *Ernstfall*; thin theology of the Cross |
| **Moltmann / Molnar** | Modalism; the "vice versa" destroys divine freedom |
| **O'Neill** | Sacraments manifest but do not confer (Trent DS 1606) |
| **Panikkar / Pieris / Soares-Prabhu** | "Anonymous Hindu?"; religiousness without poverty; wrong starting point |

**Plus the three internal gaps in the symbol ontology:** (1) Hegelian necessity of expression vs. divine freedom; (2) **no theology of the false/lying symbol**; (3) expression-as-plenitude vs. **Cross as kenosis**.

## THE TWO SENTENCES TO WRITE IN ANY ESSAY
1. "For Rahner a real symbol is not less real than what it signifies; it is that reality's own mode of presence — and this single ontological claim unifies Trinity, Incarnation, Church, sacrament and Christian devotion."
2. "Rahner's critics divide into those who think he began in the wrong place (Balthasar, Ratzinger, Lindbeck) and those who think he did not go far enough (Metz, the feminists, the Asian theologians); the first he can largely answer, the second he substantially conceded."
