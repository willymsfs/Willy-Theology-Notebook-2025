# 10 — One-Page Revision Sheet

> [!info] Cram sheet — everything on one screen. Full detail in [[Theology of the Trinity - Course Home]].

> [!quote] The dogma
> **One God · one essence (ousia) · three persons (hypostaseis) · coequal, coeternal, consubstantial · distinguished only by relations of origin.**

## Must-know facts
- **Relations of origin**: Father = **unbegotten**, Son = **begotten**, Spirit = **proceeding**.
- **Two processions**: **generation** (Son ← intellect/Word) · **spiration** (Spirit ← will/love).
- **Perichoresis**: persons mutually indwell without blending.
- ***Opera ad extra***: all outward acts are indivisible (whole Trinity); **appropriation** is only a cognitive aid.
- **Immanent** = God in himself (*theologia*) · **Economic** = God in history (*oikonomia*) · **Rahner's Rule** joins them.

## Councils (2 to know cold)
- **Nicaea 325** → *homoousios*, "begotten not made" — vs **Arius**. Defender: **Athanasius** (salvation argument).
- **Constantinople 381** → deity of the **Spirit** — vs **Pneumatomachi**. Enabled by **Cappadocian** *ousia*/*hypostasis*.

## Heresy → answer (one word each)
| Heresy | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Arianism | homoousios |
| Modalism | real persons |
| Subordinationism | co-equal |
| Tritheism | one ousia |
| Adoptionism | pre-existent Word |
| Pneumatomachianism | Spirit is God |
| Partialism | each God whole & entire |

## Filioque in one line
West (Aquinas): needed so Son ≠ Spirit (opposed relations). East: breaks the **monarchy of the Father**. → East–West split.

## Analogies
**Psychological** (Augustine/Aquinas — one mind: memory/intellect/will) → guards **unity**, risks isolated subject.
**Social** (Cappadocians/Moltmann/Zizioulas — communion of persons) → guards **distinction**, risks tritheism.

## Modern names → keyword
Barth = *revelation* · Rahner = *the Rule* · Moltmann = *social/perichoresis* · Zizioulas = *communion ontology* · Pannenberg = *dependent divinity* · Johnson/LaCugna = *feminist retrieval* · Volf = *social program*.

## Flagship texts
Son's deity → **John 1:1** · Spirit's deity → **Acts 5:3–4** · Triad → **Matt 28:19** · OT bridge → **Prov 8:22–31**.

## Why it matters
Worship (*to* Father, *through* Son, *in* Spirit) · **theosis** (participation in divine life) · ethics ("the Trinity is our social program").
