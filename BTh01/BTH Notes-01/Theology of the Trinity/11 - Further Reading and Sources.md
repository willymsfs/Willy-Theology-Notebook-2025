# 11 — Further Reading and Sources

> [!info] Home: [[Theology of the Trinity - Course Home]]

## Base source
- **The Oxford Handbook of the Trinity** — 32-source NotebookLM notebook these notes are drawn from. All content above is grounded in it.

## Classic primary works (referenced in the unit)
| Author | Work | Why read it |
|--------|------|-------------|
| Athanasius | *On the Incarnation*; *Orations Against the Arians* | Soteriological defence of the Son's deity |
| Basil the Great | *On the Holy Spirit* | Deity of the Spirit |
| Gregory of Nazianzus | *Theological Orations* | Classic Cappadocian synthesis |
| Gregory of Nyssa | *On "Not Three Gods"* | *ousia*/*hypostasis*; against tritheism |
| Augustine | *De Trinitate* | Psychological analogy; relations |
| Thomas Aquinas | *Summa Theologiae* I, qq. 27–43 | Processions, relations, persons, Filioque |

## Modern & contemporary
| Author | Work | Keyword |
|--------|------|---------|
| Karl Barth | *Church Dogmatics* I/1 | Revelation |
| Karl Rahner | *The Trinity* | Rahner's Rule |
| Jürgen Moltmann | *The Trinity and the Kingdom* | Social doctrine |
| John Zizioulas | *Being as Communion* | Communion ontology |
| Wolfhart Pannenberg | *Systematic Theology* I | Dependent divinity |
| Catherine M. LaCugna | *God for Us* | Relational "Father" |
| Elizabeth Johnson | *She Who Is* | Feminist retrieval |
| Miroslav Volf | *After Our Likeness* | Trinity & community |

## Magisterial / creedal
- **Nicene–Constantinopolitan Creed** (325 / 381)
- **Catechism of the Catholic Church** §§ 232–267 (The Profession of Faith — the Trinity)

> [!note] Citation style
> For BTh essays cite the creed by council + year, patristics by work + book/section, and the CCC by paragraph §.
