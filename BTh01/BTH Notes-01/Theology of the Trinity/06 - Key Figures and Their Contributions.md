# 06 — Key Figures and Their Contributions

> [!info] Home: [[Theology of the Trinity - Course Home]] · related: [[02 - Historical Development and the Councils]]

Cheat-sheet: who did what, and the **one line** you must recall for each.

## Patristic era
| Figure | Dates (c.) | One-line contribution |
|--------|-----------|-----------------------|
| **Arius** | 256–336 | Heresiarch — Son a creature, "there was a time when he was not" |
| **Athanasius** | 296–373 | Defended Nicaea; **soteriological** argument — only a divine Son divinizes |
| **Basil the Great** | 330–379 | Cappadocian; deity of the Spirit (*On the Holy Spirit*) |
| **Gregory of Nazianzus** | 329–390 | "The Theologian"; classic *Theological Orations* on the Triad |
| **Gregory of Nyssa** | 335–395 | Cappadocian; *ousia*/*hypostasis* distinction; against tritheism |
| **Augustine** | 354–430 | *De Trinitate*; **psychological analogy**; relations; inseparable operations |

## Medieval
| Figure | Dates (c.) | One-line contribution |
|--------|-----------|-----------------------|
| **Thomas Aquinas** | 1225–1274 | **Subsisting relations**; processions of intellect (Son) & will (Spirit); Western Filioque logic |

## Modern / contemporary
| Figure | Dates | One-line contribution |
|--------|-------|-----------------------|
| **Karl Barth** | 1886–1968 | Trinity as ground of **revelation** (Revealer/Revelation/Revealedness) |
| **Karl Rahner** | 1904–1984 | **Rahner's Rule** — economic Trinity *is* immanent Trinity |
| **Jürgen Moltmann** | 1926–2024 | **Social** doctrine; cross within the Trinity; perichoresis as fellowship |
| **John Zizioulas** | 1931–2023 | **Communion ontology**; monarchy of the Father; relational personhood |
| **Wolfhart Pannenberg** | 1928–2014 | **"Dependent divinity"**; unity as eschatological |
| **Elizabeth Johnson** | b. 1941 | Feminist retrieval — **Spirit/Jesus/Mother-Sophia** |
| **Catherine M. LaCugna** | 1952–1997 | "Father" as **relational** self-giving love (*God for Us*) |
| **Miroslav Volf** | b. 1956 | **"The Trinity is our social program"** — Trinitarian ethics |

> [!tip] Exam use
> Map each figure to its **era + one keyword**: Athanasius=*homoousios/salvation*, Cappadocians=*ousia/hypostasis*, Augustine=*psychological analogy*, Aquinas=*subsisting relations*, Barth=*revelation*, Rahner=*the Rule*, Moltmann=*social/perichoresis*, Volf=*social program*.
