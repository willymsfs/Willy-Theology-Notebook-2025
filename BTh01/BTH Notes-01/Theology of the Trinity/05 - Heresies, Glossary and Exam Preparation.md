# 05 — Heresies, Glossary and Exam Preparation

> [!info] Prev: [[04 - Contemporary Trinitarian Theology]] · Home: [[Theology of the Trinity - Course Home]]

## Heresies vs the orthodox answer
| Heresy | Error | Orthodox answer |
|--------|-------|-----------------|
| **Modalism / Sabellianism** | Father, Son, Spirit are mere **modes/masks** of one God (var. *Patripassianism* — Father suffered) | One substance in **three eternally distinct persons** by relations of origin |
| **Arianism** | Son is a **creature**, "there was a time when he was not" | Son **begotten not made**, **homoousios**, true God from true God |
| **Subordinationism** | Son & Spirit **inferior** in being/status | Three persons **co-equal, co-eternal**; Father's monarchy = source, not superiority |
| **Tritheism** | **Three gods** sharing a nature | **One** ousia, one power/operation; persons indwell (perichoresis), act inseparably |
| **Adoptionism** (Dynamic Monarchianism) | Jesus a **mere man adopted** as Son (at baptism) | Son is the **pre-existent eternal Word** made flesh, fully God & fully human |
| **Pneumatomachianism / Macedonianism** | Spirit is a **creature**, not God | Spirit is **uncreated God**, consubstantial, worshipped with Father & Son (Constantinople 381) |
| **Partialism** | Each person is a **part/fraction** of God | God is **simple**; each person is "**God whole and entire**" |

## Glossary
- **Ousia** — divine substance/essence; what is one in God.
- **Hypostasis** — person; the distinct mode of existing of each of the three.
- **Homoousios** — "of the same substance"; the Son's consubstantiality with the Father (Nicaea 325).
- **Perichoresis** — mutual indwelling/interpenetration of the persons.
- **Procession** — eternal origin within God: **generation** (Son) & **spiration** (Spirit).
- **Relations of origin** — unbegotten / begotten / proceeding; what distinguishes the persons.
- **Appropriation** — ascribing a common attribute to one person by affinity (not exclusively).
- **Opera ad extra** — God's outward works; indivisible, done by the whole Trinity.
- **Monarchy of the Father** — the Father as sole eternal source of Son and Spirit.
- **Filioque** — "and the Son"; Western clause: the Spirit proceeds from Father **and** Son.
- **Immanent / Economic Trinity** — God in himself (**theologia**) vs God in saving history (**oikonomia**).
- **Rahner's Rule** — economic Trinity **is** the immanent Trinity, and vice versa.
- **Theosis** — deification; salvation as participation in the divine life.
- **Partitive exegesis** — reading Christological texts by distinguishing divine vs assumed human natures.

## Sample exam questions
1. **Short:** Define *ousia* and *hypostasis* and state the orthodox Trinitarian formula.
2. **Short:** What did *homoousios* settle at Nicaea (325), and against whom?
3. **Short:** Distinguish the immanent from the economic Trinity; state Rahner's Rule.
4. **Essay:** Trace the development of Trinitarian dogma from the Arian controversy to Constantinople 381. Assess the Cappadocian contribution.
5. **Essay:** "The Trinity was born from the experience of salvation, not from speculation." Discuss with reference to the biblical data and the councils.
6. **Essay:** Explain the Filioque controversy. Why does the West judge it necessary and the East reject it?
7. **Essay:** Compare the psychological and social analogies of the Trinity. What are the strengths and dangers of each?
8. **Essay:** "The Trinity is our social program" (Volf). Evaluate the practical and ethical significance of Trinitarian doctrine.
