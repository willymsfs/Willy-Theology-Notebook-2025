# 03 — Systematic Concepts and Terminology

> [!info] Prev: [[02 - Historical Development and the Councils]] · Next: [[04 - Contemporary Trinitarian Theology]]

## Ousia and hypostasis
- **Ousia** (substance / essence) — what is **common** and numerically **one**: the divine nature.
- **Hypostasis** (person; *prosopon*) — the concrete, distinct **mode of existing** of each of the three.
- Orthodox formula: **mia ousia, treis hypostaseis** — one essence, three persons.
- Guardrail: "one substance" without "three persons" → **modalism**; "three persons" without "one substance" → **tritheism**.

## The two divine processions
God is simple & spiritual → processions are **immanent** (stay within God), not physical:
1. **Generation / begetting** — Son from Father, on the model of **intellect** → the **Word** (*Logos*) shares the knower's likeness.
2. **Spiration** — Spirit from Father (and Son), on the model of **will / love** → movement toward the beloved.
- Why not two Sons? **Aquinas**: intellect produces a *likeness* → "Son"; will is *love* → "Spirit."

## Relations of origin
- Persons constituted **only** by relations: Father **unbegotten**, Son **begotten**, Spirit **proceeding**.
- **Subsisting relations** (Aquinas): in a simple God the relations = the essence itself.
- Distinction requires **mutually opposed** relations (fatherhood vs sonship).

## Perichoresis (co-inherence)
- Mutual **indwelling / interpenetration** of the persons without blending — each wholly in the others because they share one essence.

## Appropriation
- Ascribing a common divine reality to **one** person by affinity:
	- **Power → Father** · **Wisdom → Son** · **Goodness/Love → Spirit**
- **Rule**: never exclusive — all **opera ad extra** are indivisible (whole Trinity acts).

## Psychological vs social analogy
| | Psychological | Social |
|---|---|---|
| Source | Augustine, Aquinas (West) | Cappadocians; Zizioulas, Moltmann |
| Model | one mind (memory/intellect/will) | communion of persons in love |
| Emphasis | **unity** | **distinction / relation** |
| Risk | isolated single subject | **tritheism** |

## The Filioque controversy
- **Filioque** = "and the Son": Spirit proceeds from the Father **and the Son** (Latin addition to the Creed).
- **East (Orthodox)**: reject — added unilaterally; undermines the **monarchy of the Father**.
- **West (Aquinas)**: needed — persons distinguished by **opposed relations**; without it Son & Spirit would be indistinguishable.
- Historic **East–West dividing line**.
