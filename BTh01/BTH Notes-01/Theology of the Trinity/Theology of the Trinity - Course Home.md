# Theology of the Trinity — Course Home (BTh)

**Tract:** *De Deo Trino* — the Christian doctrine of the one God who is Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

> [!quote] The dogma in one line
> **One God, one divine essence (*ousia*), subsisting in three coequal, coeternal, consubstantial persons (*hypostaseis*)** — distinguished only by their relations of origin.

## Council quick-reference
| Year | Council | Key term / result |
|------|---------|-------------------|
| 325 | Nicaea I | **homoousios**; "begotten, not made"; condemns Arius |
| 381 | Constantinople I | full deity of the **Holy Spirit**; condemns Pneumatomachi |
| 431 / 451 | Ephesus / Chalcedon | (Christological — presuppose Trinitarian dogma) |

## Golden thread
Doctrine born not from speculation but from the **experience of salvation** — the God who saves *as* Father, Son, Spirit *is* God eternally. Worship (baptism, doxology) → reflection → dogma.
