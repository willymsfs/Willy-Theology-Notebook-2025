# 04 — Contemporary Trinitarian Theology

> [!info] Prev: [[03 - Systematic Concepts and Terminology]] · Next: [[05 - Heresies, Glossary and Exam Preparation]]

Context: for much of the modern era the Trinity was treated as **speculative and irrelevant**. The 20th c. **revived** it as the grammar of revelation, salvation, and ethics.

## Immanent vs economic Trinity
- **Immanent (ontological) Trinity** — God's eternal inner life & the relations of the persons **in themselves**, apart from creation. Patristic name: **theologia**. = **who God *is***.
- **Economic Trinity** — God's actions & self-revelation **in salvation history** (Father sends the Son; Spirit poured out). Patristic name: **oikonomia**. = **what God *does***.
- Access: the economic is our **only path** to the immanent (God truly reveals himself).

### Rahner's Rule
> **"The 'economic' Trinity is the 'immanent' Trinity, and the 'immanent' Trinity is the 'economic' Trinity."** — Karl Rahner
- Guarantees no hidden "God behind God."
- **Debate**: 2nd half risks compromising God's **eternal freedom** (dependence on history).

## The revival — Barth & Rahner
- **Karl Barth** — Trinity at the *head* of the *Church Dogmatics*; precondition of revelation. God = **Revealer (Father) / Revelation (Son) / Revealedness (Spirit)**.
- **Karl Rahner** — protested the doctrine's marginalization; restored it to the center via his Rule.

## Social trinitarianism
- **Jürgen Moltmann** — "social doctrine"; the **cross** as an event *within* the Trinity; **perichoresis** = egalitarian fellowship, not dominion.
- **John Zizioulas** (Orthodox) — **communion ontology** ("being = communion"); **monarchy of the Father** grounds Trinity in a free personal source; personhood is relational.
- **Wolfhart Pannenberg** — **"dependent divinity"**; unity is **eschatological**, fulfilled at the end of history.

## Feminist critique
- **Elizabeth Johnson** — male-exclusive naming subverts women as *imago Dei*; retrieves Wisdom: **Spirit-Sophia / Jesus-Sophia / Mother-Sophia**.
- **Catherine M. LaCugna** — "Father" is **relational** (self-giving love), not distant patriarch.
- **Moltmann** — the begetting Father transcends gender ("motherly Father").

## Practical significance
- **Worship** — Trinity is the grammar of prayer: *to* the Father, *through* the Son, *in* the Spirit.
- **Theosis** — salvation = **participation in the divine life**; by the Spirit, united to the Son, drawn into the Trinitarian communion.
- **Ethics** — Volf: **"The Trinity is our social program"** → reject domination; foster equality, justice, inclusive fellowship.

> [!warning] The standing tension (good exam essay)
> Social models = relational/ethical richness but risk **tritheism**; substance/psychological models = guard unity but risk an **isolated subject**. Orthodoxy holds **both**: real distinction *and* one essence.
