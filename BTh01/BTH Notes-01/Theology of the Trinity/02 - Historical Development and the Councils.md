# 02 — Historical Development and the Councils

> [!info] Prev: [[01 - Introduction and Biblical Foundations]] · Next: [[03 - Systematic Concepts and Terminology]]

## The Arian controversy
- **Arius** (presbyter, Alexandria) vs bishop **Alexander**, early 4th c.
- Motive: guard the Father's unique, unbegotten transcendence.
- Claim: Son = **creature**, made from nothing, ontologically **subordinate**; slogan **"there was a time when he was not."**

## Nicaea I (325)
- Convened by **Emperor Constantine**; condemned Arius.
- Confessed the Son **"begotten, not made"**, **homoousios** (consubstantial) with the Father — "true God from true God."
- **Athanasius of Alexandria** = chief defender.
	- Argument is **soteriological**: only a truly divine Son can save/divinize — *"God became man that man might become god-like."*
	- Reading tool: **partitive exegesis** — sort texts about Christ's eternal divine nature vs those about his assumed human weakness.

## The terminological crisis
- *homoousios* alone risked **modalism** (persons blurred).
- East/West confusion: *hypostasis* ↔ *substantia* → mutual suspicion of tritheism / modalism.

## The Cappadocian settlement
- **Basil the Great, Gregory of Nazianzus, Gregory of Nyssa.**
- Breakthrough: distinguish **ousia** (essence) from **hypostasis** (person) → **one essence, three persons** (*mia ousia, treis hypostaseis*).
- **Monarchy of the Father** — Father is sole eternal source: Son **begotten**, Spirit **proceeds**.
- Against the **Pneumatomachi** ("Spirit-fighters"): the Spirit gives life & sanctifies → fully God.

## Constantinople I (381)
- Reaffirmed Nicaea; expanded creed to confess the **Holy Spirit** as "the Lord and life-giver, who proceeds from the Father, worshipped and glorified with the Father and Son."
- → completes the confession of the co-equal Triad.

## Augustine (Latin West) — *De Trinitate*
- Stresses **unity of essence** and **inseparable operations** *ad extra* (every divine act = whole Trinity).
- **Psychological analogy** (mind as *imago Dei*): memory / understanding / will.
- **Relation**: persons distinguished purely by eternal reciprocal relations (Father is Father only *toward* the Son).

## Aquinas (High Scholastic synthesis)
- **Processions** modeled on intellect (Son/Word) and will (Spirit/Love).
- Persons = **subsisting relations**; distinguished by **opposed relations of origin**.
- Systematizes the Western case for the **Filioque** → see [[03 - Systematic Concepts and Terminology#The Filioque controversy]].

> [!example] One-line memory hooks
> Nicaea = **Son is God** (325). Constantinople = **Spirit is God** (381). Augustine = **one mind, three faculties**. Aquinas = **subsisting relations**.
