# 07 — Scripture Dossier

> [!info] Home: [[Theology of the Trinity - Course Home]] · builds on [[01 - Introduction and Biblical Foundations]]

The biblical **data** the doctrine rests on. Grouped for quick recall.

## (a) Old Testament adumbrations
| Text | Establishes |
|------|-------------|
| **Deut 6:4** (Shema) | Uncompromising monotheism — the foundation |
| **Gen 1:1–2** | Word and **Spirit** (*ruach*) active in creation |
| **Gen 1:26** ("Let *us* make") | Divine plurality — Father to Word/Wisdom |
| **Gen 18** | Three visitors to Abraham — theophany read as Trinity |
| **Ps 33:6** | Creation by God's **Word** and **breath/Spirit** |
| **Prov 8:22–31** | Personified **Wisdom** as pre-existent agent → identified with the Logos |
| **Wisdom 7:22–28** | Wisdom as emanation of God's glory, reflection of eternal light |
| **Sirach 24:2–9** | Wisdom coming forth from the mouth of the Most High from eternity |
| **Isa 6:3** ("Holy, holy, holy") | Threefold cry read as anticipating the triune God |

## (b) Divinity of the Son
| Text | Establishes |
|------|-------------|
| **John 1:1–14** | Word was **with** God, **was** God, agent of creation, made flesh |
| **John 8:58** ("Before Abraham was, I am") | Pre-existence; divine name applied to himself |
| **John 10:30** ("I and the Father are one") | Unity of nature and power |
| **John 14:9** ("seen me = seen the Father") | Son shares the Father's nature and will |
| **John 20:28** | Thomas: "My **Lord** and my **God**" |
| **Col 1:15–20** | Image of the invisible God; all things created through him |
| **Phil 2:6–11** | Pre-existent equality; self-emptying; exalted, given the name **Lord** |
| **Heb 1:2–3** | Exact representation of the Father's being; radiance of glory |
| **Heb 1:8** (Ps 45:6) | Father addresses the Son as **"God"** |

## (c) Divinity / personhood of the Holy Spirit
| Text | Establishes |
|------|-------------|
| **John 14–16** (14:16,26; 15:26; 16:13) | "Another **Advocate**", Spirit of truth, proceeds from the Father |
| **Acts 5:3–4** | Lying to the Spirit = lying to **God** → full deity |
| **Rom 5:5** | Spirit pours God's love into hearts — personal gift |
| **Rom 8:9–16** | Indwelling Spirit brings **adoption** — "Abba! Father!" |
| **1 Cor 2:10–12** | Spirit searches/reveals the **deep things of God** — personal knowledge |
| **1 Cor 3:16–17; 6:19–20** | Believers = **temple** of the Spirit → Spirit is God |
| **Eph 4:30** | Spirit can be **grieved** → personal agency |

## (d) Explicit triadic texts
| Text | Establishes |
|------|-------------|
| **Matt 28:19** | Baptize in the single **name** of Father, Son, Spirit — shared identity |
| **2 Cor 13:14** | Triadic benediction: grace / love / fellowship |
| **1 Cor 12:4–6** | Same **Spirit, Lord, God** — gifts/services/activities |
| **Eph 4:4–6** | Church's unity grounded in one Spirit, one Lord, one God & Father |
| **1 Pet 1:2** | Salvation pattern: Father's foreknowledge / Spirit's sanctifying / obedience to Christ |
| **1 Thess 1:1–5** | Early Pauline triad: Father, Lord Jesus, power of the Spirit |

> [!tip] Exam use
> Learn **one flagship text per claim**: Son's deity = **John 1:1**; Spirit's deity = **Acts 5:3–4**; the triad = **Matt 28:19**; OT bridge = **Prov 8:22–31**.
