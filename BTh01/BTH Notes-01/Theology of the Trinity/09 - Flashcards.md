## Core dogma
State the Trinitarian dogma in one line :: One God, one essence (*ousia*), three coequal coeternal persons (*hypostaseis*), distinguished only by relations of origin.
What distinguishes the three persons? :: Their **relations of origin** — Father unbegotten, Son begotten, Spirit proceeding.
What are the *opera ad extra*? :: God's outward works — **indivisible**, done by the whole Trinity.

## Terminology
Define *ousia* :: The divine substance/essence — what is one in God.
Define *hypostasis* :: Person — the distinct mode of existing of each of the three.
Define *homoousios* :: "Of the same substance"; the Son's consubstantiality with the Father (Nicaea 325).
Define *perichoresis* :: Mutual indwelling/interpenetration of the persons without blending.
Define *appropriation* :: Ascribing a common divine attribute to one person by affinity (never exclusively).
What are the two processions? :: **Generation** (Son, model of intellect) and **spiration** (Spirit, model of will/love).
What does "monarchy of the Father" mean? :: The Father is the sole eternal source of Son and Spirit.

## Councils
What did Nicaea (325) settle, and against whom? :: The Son is *homoousios*, "begotten not made" — against **Arius**.
What did Constantinople (381) add? :: Full deity of the **Holy Spirit** — against the **Pneumatomachi**.
Who defended Nicaea and with what argument? :: **Athanasius** — soteriological: only a divine Son can divinize humanity.
What was the Cappadocians' breakthrough? :: Distinguishing *ousia* from *hypostasis* → "one essence, three persons."

## Heresies
Arianism error? :: Son is a creature — "there was a time when he was not."
Modalism/Sabellianism error? :: Persons are mere modes/masks of one God.
Subordinationism error? :: Son and Spirit are inferior in being to the Father.
Tritheism error? :: Three separate gods.
Adoptionism error? :: Jesus a mere man adopted as Son (e.g. at baptism).
Partialism error? :: Each person is a fraction/part of God (God is simple — each is "God whole and entire").

## Concepts & modern
Immanent vs economic Trinity? :: Immanent = who God *is* eternally (*theologia*); economic = what God *does* in history (*oikonomia*).
State Rahner's Rule :: "The economic Trinity is the immanent Trinity, and the immanent Trinity is the economic Trinity."
What is the Filioque? :: The Western clause: the Spirit proceeds from the Father **and the Son**; rejected by the East.
Barth's threefold formula for revelation? :: Revealer (Father), Revelation (Son), Revealedness (Spirit).
Volf's one-line Trinitarian ethic? :: "The Trinity is our social program."
What is *theosis*? :: Deification — salvation as participation in the divine life.

## Scripture (flagship texts)
Flagship text for the Son's deity? :: John 1:1 ("the Word was God").
Flagship text for the Spirit's deity? :: Acts 5:3–4 (lying to the Spirit = lying to God).
Flagship triadic text? :: Matt 28:19 (baptize in the one *name* of Father, Son, Spirit).
OT bridge text for a plural-yet-one God? :: Prov 8:22–31 (personified Wisdom).
