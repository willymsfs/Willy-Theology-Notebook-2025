# Old Testament Foundations — Prophets, Wisdom, Anthropology

> [!info] Part of [[000 - BTh Pune - Foundations of Catholic Theology (MOC)]]

## 1. The Prophets (Nevi'im)
### Nature of prophecy
- **Navi'** = called by God to communicate a divine perspective on contemporary life/politics — not a fortune-teller.
- Rose with the monarchy as checks-and-balances against kings vs the Sinai covenant.
- Messengers of the "Divine Council": "Thus says YHWH." True vs false tested by fulfilment.

### Books
- **Former Prophets**: Joshua, Judges, 1–2 Samuel, 1–2 Kings (Deuteronomistic History).
- **Major**: Isaiah, Jeremiah, Ezekiel (Daniel = Major in the Christian canon; Writings/apocalyptic in the Hebrew).
- **Minor (Book of the Twelve)**: Hosea, Joel, Amos, Obadiah, Jonah, Micah, Nahum, Habakkuk, Zephaniah, Haggai, Zechariah, Malachi.

### Message
- God controls history; prosperity/disaster reflects covenant faithfulness.
- Fierce critique of complacent institutional religion ignoring **social justice** (Amos: selling the righteous for silver).
- Cycle: sin → punishment → repentance → deliverance; hope of a **New Covenant** written on the heart (Jeremiah, Ezekiel).

## 2. Wisdom Literature
- **Nature**: empirical — human observation/experience discerning the order God embedded in creation (vs direct revelation).
- **Books**: Proverbs, Job, Ecclesiastes, Song of Songs, Wisdom Psalms; apocryphal — Wisdom of Solomon, Sirach.
- **Themes**:
  - "The fear of YHWH is the beginning of wisdom."
  - **Retribution theology** (Proverbs): right behaviour → blessing.
  - **Theodicy / innocent suffering** (Job): assaults strict retribution; God's wisdom beyond comprehension.
  - **Search for meaning** (Ecclesiastes): *hevel* (vanity); enjoy simple gifts, fear the Lord.
  - **Lady Wisdom** (*Sophia/Hokmah*): personified, pre-existent, God's craftsman — vs Mistress Folly.

## 3. Theological anthropology
- **Imago Dei** (Gen 1): humans as God's representatives, stewards of creation; capacity for communion.
- **Body-person unity**: *nepes* ("living being," not a trapped soul), *basar* (flesh = whole person); no strict Greek dualism; creation is good.
- **The Fall**: sin as ontological rupture — alienation from God, others, nature; essence = grasping autonomy to define good/evil.

## Key terms
*Navi'* · **Theodicy** · *Hokmah/Sophia* · *Vaticinia ex eventu* (prophecy after the event) · Deuteronomistic History · *nepes* · *basar* · *hevel*.
