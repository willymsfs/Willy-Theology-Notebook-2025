# Priesthood

> [!info] Part of [[000 - BTh Pune - Foundations of Catholic Theology (MOC)]]

## 1. Biblical foundation
- **OT**: evolution from "natural priesthood" (patriarchs, kings — Noah, Abraham, David) to "professional" Levitical/Aaronic priesthood (cult, sacrifice, Law). Whole people a "kingdom of priests" (Ex 19:6).
- **Melchizedek** (Gen 14; Ps 110): priest-king, no genealogy → prefigures Christ's eternal, non-Levitical priesthood.
- **Jesus**: not of Levi; never called himself/disciples "priest"; ministry prophetic + Kingdom-centred; replaced sterile cultic priesthood with love, compassion, kenotic service.
- **Hebrews**: Jesus the Eternal High Priest "in the order of Melchizedek" — offers himself once for all in the heavenly sanctuary; superior New Covenant.

## 2. Priesthood in Church history
- **Early Church**: avoided cultic terms (*hiereus/sacerdos*); used *episkopoi*, *presbyteroi*, *diakonoi*; leadership pastoral, not primarily cultic.
- **3rd c.–Middle Ages**: after the Temple's fall + Christianized empire, "sacred–secular divide"; ministers called *sacerdos* on the Levitical model; heavy **hierarchicalism** (Pseudo-Dionysius) — active clerics vs passive laity; priest as "sacred person" defined by power to consecrate.
- **Reformation**: Luther/Calvin — "priesthood of all believers," ministry of the Word.
- **Trent** (1545–63): defensive — Holy Orders as ascending ladder of power, indelible character, focus on Eucharistic sacrifice.
- **Vatican II**: from pyramid to **People of God**; reclaims the **common priesthood of the faithful** — common and ministerial differ "in essence and not only in degree," mutually ordered.

## 3. Theology of ordained ministry
- **Holy Orders**: minister = the Bishop alone; **matter** = laying on of hands; **form** = consecratory prayer.
- **Indelible character** (*sphragis*): permanent seal configuring to Christ; not lost through sin or laicization.
- ***In persona Christi capitis***: sacramental representation through which the Risen Christ leads, teaches, sanctifies.
- **Threefold function**: teach (Prophet/Word), sanctify (Priest/Sacrament), shepherd (King/Pastor).

## 4. Spirituality of priesthood
- **Kenotic + Shepherd** spirituality (Paschal Mystery; the Good Shepherd).
- **Servant of the Word** — listen/contemplate, be God's "microphone," denounce injustice.
- **Servant of the Sacraments** — Eucharist source and summit; become "the body given and blood shed."
- **Servant of the World** — immersed in the human condition, with "the last, the lost, the least."

## 5. Special questions
- **Celibacy**: charism and gift; eschatological sign + pastoral availability; needs affective maturity and prayer.
- **Married priests**: Eastern Catholic Churches ordain married men; Latin Church rare exceptions (Pastoral Provision).
- **Ordination of women**: *Ordinatio Sacerdotalis* (JP II) — Church has no authority to ordain women (Christ chose only men as the Twelve; constant tradition; *in persona Christi*).

## 6. Pastores Dabo Vobis (JP II, 1992) — formation
Title from Jer 3:15 ("I will give you shepherds"). **Four pillars (SHIP)**:
1. **Spiritual** — the axis; union with God, *lectio divina*, Eucharist, Penance, pastoral charity.
2. **Human** — the foundation; "bridge not an obstacle"; psychological balance, affective maturity, friendship.
3. **Intellectual** — philosophy + theology (*fides quaerens intellectum*) for the new evangelization.
4. **Pastoral** — the goal; the "compassion" of Christ; practical shepherding of souls.
- **Stages**: Propaedeutic → Discipleship (philosophy) → Configuration (theology) → Pastoral.
- **Ongoing formation**: permanent, all ages — against burnout, routine activism.
