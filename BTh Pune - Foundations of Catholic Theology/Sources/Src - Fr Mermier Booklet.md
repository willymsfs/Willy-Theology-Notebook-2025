# Src — Fr. Peter Mermier (Booklet by Wilson)

> [!info] Source note · theme: [[10 - Church History and the MSFS]] · summary of Duval's "Green Book"

**Topic**: Concise biography of Pierre (Peter) Mermier (1790–1862) — life, spirituality, apostolates, founding the MSFS and the Sisters of the Cross of Chavanod.

## Life & context
- Born 1790, post-Revolution Savoie (priest shortage, indifference, Jansenism).
- **1821** Le Châtelard — parish mission with Abbé Favre (after mountain fast/prayer).
- **1836–37** La Feuillette Mother House; **1838** canonical establishment (Bishop Rey, King Charles Albert); **1843** Rome's approval.
- Restored pilgrimage sites: Les Allinges, Notre-Dame-de-la-Gorge.

## Spirituality & charism
- Salesian: **gentleness, zeal, patience, love**; charity/self-renunciation over rigid rules; **purity of intention** (motive of love).
- **Ignatian influence**: Salesian spirituality + the *Spiritual Exercises*.

## Apostolates
- **Parish missions/retreats**: intense, dawn-to-dusk; reconciliation, mission crosses.
- **Indian mission**: 1844 Visakhapatnam assigned; **26 priests + 5 brothers, 1845–62**; affectionate correspondence keeping "family unity."
- **Education**: religious foundation essential; took over Evian and Melan colleges.

## Daughters of the Cross (Chavanod)
- **1837** with Mother Claudine Echernier — for poor girls without dowries; teaching + manual labour; pushed the Sisters to get teaching licences; paternal leadership.

## Final years & legacy
- 1855 refuge at Pougny (reluctant parish work); stroke 1858, losing sight — his "high noon" of purification; expansion to England; **died 1862** — a thriving global congregation, humility, Salesian gentleness.
