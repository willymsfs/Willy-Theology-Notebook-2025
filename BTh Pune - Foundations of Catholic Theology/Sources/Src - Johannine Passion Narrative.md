# Src — The Theology of the Johannine Passion Narrative (Jn 19:16b–30)

> [!info] Source note (reading, R. Alan Culpepper, *Neotestamentica* 1997) · theme: [[01 - New Testament Background and World]] (Johannine)

**Thesis**: John's passion = a performative revelation of God's redemptive design; an **ecclesiology of the cross** — the Church is born at the cross.

## Four scenes
1. **Inscription (19:19–22)**: cross as **exaltation** ("lifting up"); Jesus' kingship (Pilate certifies); three languages = universality; Pilate ironically confesses.
2. **Seamless tunic (19:23–24)**: woven "**from above**" (*anothen*) — part of God's design; **untorn** = unity of the Church (one flock, one net).
3. **Mother & Beloved Disciple (19:25–27)**: distancing flesh-ties ("Woman"); Beloved Disciple = apostolic authority; new spiritual family → the Church (corporate redemption).
4. **Last words (19:28–30)**: "It is finished" = mission complete; the irony of thirst (giver of living water); *hyssop* → Passover lamb; **"handed over the spirit"** = death + release of the Spirit.

## Conclusion
- The Church born at the cross (not only the resurrection); new kinship across cultures, unity, crucified king, living water + Spirit.
