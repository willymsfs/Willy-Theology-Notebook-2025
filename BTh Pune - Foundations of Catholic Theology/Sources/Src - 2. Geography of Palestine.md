# Src — 2. Geography of Palestine

> [!info] Source note · theme: [[01 - New Testament Background and World]]

**Topic**: How Palestine's geography/topography shaped its political, commercial, and biblical history (Naveen Rebello SVD).

## Significance
- Toponyms are settings for biblical events and encounters with the Divine; characters' interaction with geography reveals motivations.
- Topographically vulnerable → frequently conquered.
- **Names of the land**: Holy Land, Canaan, Palestine, Israel.
- **Dimensions**: N–S 150 mi (Dan→Beersheba); E–W 75 mi; ~10,000 sq mi.

## Fertile Crescent & land-bridge
- **Fertile Crescent** (coined by James H. Breasted): semicircle Ur → Tigris/Euphrates → Haran → Mediterranean coast → Nile delta.
- **Land-bridge**: the only link between Asia and Africa (Mesopotamia ↔ Egypt) → contested by superpowers.

## Five longitudinal zones (N→S)
1. **Coastal Plains** — Tyre to Sinai, broken by Carmel; few harbours (poor seafarers); fertile. Places: Phoenicia, Sidon, Tyre, Acco, Megiddo, Sharon, Caesarea, Jaffa, Philistine Pentapolis (Gaza, Ashkelon, Ashdod, Ekron, Gath).
2. **Central Mountain Range** — Galilee (N), Samaria (centre), Judah (S); Jezreel Valley divides. **Wadi** = runoff gully (dry, can flood). Places: Hermon, Dan, Caesarea Philippi, Nazareth, Cana, Tabor, Shechem, Shiloh, Bethel, Jerusalem, Bethlehem, Hebron, Beersheba.
3. **Rift/Jordan Valley** — part of the Great Rift; deepest land depression (1,300 ft below sea level at Dead Sea). Upper Jordan (Hermon) → Lake Huleh → Sea of Galilee → Lower Jordan → Dead Sea → Arabah → Gulf of Aqaba.
   - **Sea of Galilee**: also Kinneret, Tiberias, Gennesaret; 212 m below sea level, 21×13 km, 54 m deep. Towns: Tiberias, Magdala, Capernaum, Bethsaida, Chorazin.
   - **Dead Sea**: "Salt Sea"; lowest place on earth; no exit; 26–33% salt. David hid from Saul (1 Sam 26); Herod's forts Masada, Macherus. **1947** — Dead Sea Scrolls found at Qumran.
4. **Trans-Jordanian Plateau** — Hauran (N), Central Highlands (S of Yarmuk), Southern Highlands. Places: Bashan, Gilead, Decapolis, Ammon, Moab, Edom.
5. **Desert** — Syrian desert; **Negeb** (around Beersheba, 10–12 in rain, grazing). Abraham/Isaac sojourned here; in NT times = **Idumea** (Herod the Great was Idumean).
