# Src — 3. Anointing of the Sick

> [!info] Source note · theme: [[04 - The Sacraments]]

**Topic**: Anointing + Reconciliation = the two Sacraments of Healing; "supernatural balm" for body and soul, restoring communion.

## Scripture
- **OT**: sickness linked to sin/fall (Gen 3; Lev 13–15); healing = divine favour; Isaiah's messianic age.
- **NT prime texts**: **Mk 6:7–13** (the Twelve anoint the sick with oil); **Jas 5:14–16** (call the elders, anoint, link healing + forgiveness). Jesus the Divine Healer (Mk 2:5–12).

## Theology
- Christ *Ursakrament*, Church *Grundsakrament*.
- Sickness/death disrupt the Kingdom → healing is part of the Church's identity.
- **Redemptive suffering** (JP II): the Cross gives suffering meaning.
- **Not just for the dying** (Vatican II, Francis): restoration, offered when very ill/elderly.

## Matter / Form / Effects
- **Matter**: Oil of the Sick (*Oleum Infirmorum*), blessed by a Bishop.
- **Form**: "Through this holy anointing may the Lord in his love and mercy help you… save you and raise you up."
- **Effects**: gifts of the Spirit (peace, courage, strength); healing of soul (and body if God wills); forgiveness of sins; union with Christ's Passion; preparation for the final journey.
- **Minister**: ordained **priests/bishops** only (not deacons or laity).

## History
- Early Church: no fixed structure; blessed oil used at home as medicine (Innocent I, Caesarius of Arles).
- 9th c.→: reserved to priests; became **Extreme Unction** (*sacramentum exeuntium*); Aquinas — washes "remnants of sin"; **Trent (1551)** affirms it a true sacrament.
- Vatican II (*Sacrosanctum Concilium*) restores the name **"Anointing of the Sick"**; **1972** New Rite (*Ordo Unctionis Infirmorum*) — communal, with Penance + **Viaticum**.
