# Src — Oriental Canon Law & Marriage

> [!info] Source note · theme: [[07 - Ecclesiology and Ecclesial Documents]] (canon law)

**Topic**: Q&A study guide on Oriental Canon Law + Marriage Canon Law (Eastern Catholic Churches).

## Foundational concepts
- **Two codes**: **CCEO** (Eastern, 1990, JP II) + **CIC** (Latin, 1983). Church "breathes with two lungs."
- **Three-tier law**: Common (CCEO, 1546 canons) · Particular (Church *sui iuris*) · More Particular (statutes).
- **24 Churches *sui iuris*** (23 Eastern + 1 Latin); grades: Patriarchal (6), Major Archiepiscopal (4, papal confirmation of election), Metropolitan (5), Other (8).
- **Unique CCEO**: initiation sacraments together (even infants); marriage needs **priestly blessing** for validity; abolished *latae sententiae* penalties.
- **Clerical state** lost by: invalidity, deposition, dispensation.

## Marriage
- **Ends**: well-being of spouses + procreation/upbringing. **Properties**: unity + indissolubility. **"Consent makes marriage."**
- **Nullity**: defect of form · diriment impediment · defect of consent.
- **Impediments**: *Ligamen* (existing bond), impotence (invalidates; sterility does not). **Disparity of cult** (+ unbaptized, dispensation, non-sacramental) vs **mixed marriage** (+ baptized non-Catholic, permission, sacramental).
- **Defects of consent (c. 1095)**: lack of reason · grave defect of discretion · unable to assume obligations; also simulation, force/fear.
- **Dissolution**: *Ratum et Consummatum* — indissoluble (only death); *Ratum Tantum* — Petrine Privilege; **Pauline Privilege** (two unbaptized, one baptized).
- Terms: **Eparchy** (diocese), **Exarchy** (vicariate), **Ascription** (membership by father's Church).
