# Src — 3. Political World of the NT

> [!info] Source note · theme: [[01 - New Testament Background and World]]

**Topic**: Political history of Palestine shaping the NT world, empire by empire.

## Pre-Greek
- **587 BCE** Davidic kingship ends (Babylonian Exile); **539** Persia defeats Babylon; **520–515** Second Temple rebuilt; priestly influence grows.

## Greek period
- **332 BCE** Alexander conquers Persia (Issus 333); spreads Greek culture + common tongue. **323** dies at Babylon (age 32); empire split among the **Diadochi** (Ptolemy, Antigonus/Seleucus, Lysimachus, Cassander).
- **Hellenism/Hellenization**; **Koine** (common Greek); **Diadochi** ("successors").

## Ptolemaic rule (301–198 BC)
- Ptolemy in Egypt (Alexandria); no meddling in Temple cult; High Priest + Sanhedrin lead.
- **285–246** Ptolemy Philadelphus → **Septuagint (LXX)**. Factions: **Tobiads** (pro-Hellenism) vs **Oniads** (anti). **Diaspora** = dispersion.

## Seleucid rule (198–168 BC)
- **198** Antiochus III beats Ptolemy V. **175–164 Antiochus IV Epiphanes** ("manifest of God"; mocked *Epimanes*, "madman") — replaces Onias III with Jason; bans sacrifice, Torah, circumcision, Sabbath. **168** Apollonius outlaws Judaism; altar to Zeus, swine sacrificed.

## Maccabean revolt & Hasmoneans
- **167** **Mattathias** (Modein) starts the revolt; **164** **Judas "Maccabeus" (the Hammer)** cleanses the Temple → **Hanukkah**; **142** Simon wins independence.
- **142–37 BC Hasmoneans** rule until the Hyrcanus II vs Aristobulus II split + Rome's rise.

## Roman period (63 BC–70 AD)
- **63 BC** Pompey takes Jerusalem; Hyrcanus II High Priest. **Antipater** (Idumean) → Procurator. **27 BC** Octavian → **Augustus**, **Pax Romana**.
- **Herodian dynasty**: **Herod the Great** (37–4 BC, cruel, rebuilt/doubled the Temple); sons — **Archelaus** (Judea, deposed), **Herod Antipas** (Galilee/Perea, married Herodias, mocked Jesus, banished 39 AD), **Philip** (Caesarea Philippi); **Agrippa I** (killed James, arrested Peter, d. 44); **Agrippa II** (heard Paul); **Pontius Pilate** (26–36, condemned Jesus).

## Jewish revolts
- **I (66–70 CE)**: Caligula's statue, Florus' theft, sacrifices halted → **70 CE** Vespasian + Titus destroy the Temple; Sadducees vanish, Pharisaic synagogue system survives; **Masada** suicide.
- **II (132–135 CE)**: **Bar Cochba** (vs Hadrian's Jupiter shrine + circumcision ban); persecuted Jewish Christians; Jerusalem rebuilt as a Roman city, Jews banned; no Jewish state until **1948**.

## Hellenism in depth
- *Polis* + *agora*; non-Greeks = "barbarians." Wealthy middle class but devastated peasants. **Mystery religions** (Eleusis, Dionysus). Christianity emerged Hellenized; NT in Greek.
