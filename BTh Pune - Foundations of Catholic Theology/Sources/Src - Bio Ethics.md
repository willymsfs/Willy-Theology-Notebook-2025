# Src — Bio-Medical Ethics

> [!info] Source note · theme: [[07 - Ecclesiology and Ecclesial Documents]] (moral theology)

**Topic**: Ethics + Catholic theology applied to medicine (John Karuvelil SJ).

## Fundamentals
- Bioethics bridges biology + human values; from Hippocratic Oath, response to Nazi/Tuskegee abuses.
- Health = holistic well-being. **Disease→curing**; **illness→healing**.
- **Human dignity**: theological (from creation/redemption, not achievement); **Sanctity of Life** over "Quality of Life."

## Principles
- **Four**: Autonomy (informed consent), Non-maleficence, Beneficence, Justice (option for the poor).
- **Catholic supportive**: Double Effect · Ordinary/Extraordinary Means · Totality · Co-operation (formal always evil) · Lesser Evil. (Proportionalism rejected by *Veritatis Splendor*.)

## Beginning of life
- Life at **fertilization**; zygote = "human being with potential" (*Donum Vitae*).
- **Abortion** gravely immoral (*Evangelium Vitae*).
- **Contraception**: *Humanae Vitae* — unitive + procreative inseparable; NFP acceptable; direct sterilization immoral.
- **ART**: AI/AID rejected; IVF condemned; **GIFT** allowed (fertilization inside body); surrogacy/cloning illicit; adult stem cells OK, embryonic rejected.

## Medical ethics
- Organ donation (welfare, last resort, consent); human experimentation (consent, risk/benefit); HIV/AIDS (compassionate; condoms via double effect/lesser evil).

## End of life
- Church accepts **brain death** (not PVS).
- **Euthanasia/assisted suicide** always unacceptable; **letting die** (stop disproportionate treatment) morally right; **palliative care** permitted via double effect.
