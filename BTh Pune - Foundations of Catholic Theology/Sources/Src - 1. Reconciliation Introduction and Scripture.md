# Src — 1. Reconciliation: Introduction & Scripture

> [!info] Source note · theme: [[04 - The Sacraments]]

**Topic**: Introduction to Reconciliation — restoring broken relationships with God, community, creation.

## Definitions
- **Sacraments**: actions of Christ transmitting grace — Initiation (Baptism, Confirmation, Eucharist), Healing (Reconciliation, Anointing), Growth (Orders, Marriage).
- **Ursakrament** = Christ, primary sacrament; **Grundsakrament** = the Church, foundational sacrament.
- **Sin**: "Seeing **I** in **N**eed of everything" (I at the centre) — rupture, refusal to love, estrangement.
- **Grave/Mortal sin**: grave matter + full knowledge + deliberate consent → spiritual death. **Venial**: compromise not turning away, can pave the way to grave sin.
- **Metanoia**: change of heart, "spirit overturned to turn toward God." **Penance** (*paenitentia*): inner turning away, restoring harmony.

## Key points
- **Four components**: Contrition, Confession, Absolution, Satisfaction (in an ecclesial context).
- **Four stages of Christian living**: Faith → Repentance → Conversion → Commitment.
- **Names**: Conversion, Penance, Confession, Forgiveness, Reconciliation.
- **Threefold alienation** (Gen 3–4): from God (false autonomy), from one another (blame, hatred), from creation ("cursed is the ground").
- **Four levels of sin**: individual · communitarian (no purely private sin) · structural (racism, caste-ism, inequality) · cosmic (pollution, deforestation).
- Christ the divine physician "became sin" for us; the priest as head of the community makes Christ's presence visible.
- **Institution**: Jesus grants the power to forgive/retain and to bind/loose.

## Key scripture
- Institution: **Jn 20:22–23**; **Mt 16:19 / 18:18** (bind and loose).
- Ministry of reconciliation: **2 Cor 5:16–20** ("ambassadors for Christ").
- Fall: **Gen 3–4**. Mercy: **Lk 15** (prodigal son, lost sheep/coin), **Lk 7:36–50** (sinful woman), **Jn 8:1–11** (adulteress), **Mk 2:1–12** (paralytic).
