# Src — Medieval & Modern Church History

> [!info] Source note · theme: [[10 - Church History and the MSFS]]

## Rise of Christendom & Middle Ages
- **476** fall of Rome (Ostrogoths depose Romulus Augustulus); **610** Islam; **711** Arabs take Spain.
- Papal temporal power: **754 Kiersy** (Pepin); **800** Leo III crowns Charlemagne (*Reductio ad unum*).
- **Iron Age of Papacy (880–1046)** — *Pornocracy*; Cadaver Synod (896); John XII. Popess Joan legend.
- **Gregorian Reform** (from Cluny 910): vs lay investiture, concubinage, simony; **Gregory VII** *Dictatus Papae* (1075); Henry IV excommunicated, Canossa (1076).

## Crusades & reactions
- **1st (1095, Urban II)** — Jerusalem 1099; 2nd (Bernard, failed); 3rd (Saladin, 1193); Children's Crusade (1212).
- Movements: Albigensians/Cathars (dualist), Valdensians (Peter Waldo, 1173), Joachim of Flora (condemned Lateran IV 1215), Mendicants.

## Schism & Councils
- **Avignon (1309–77)**; Boniface VIII *Unam Sanctam* (1302). **Western Schism (1378–1417)**. Conciliarism; **Constance (1414–17)** — *Haec Sancta*, *Frequens*, Martin V.

## Reformation
- **Luther** — 95 Theses (1517), *Exsurge Domine*, Diet of Worms; Peace of Augsburg (1555, *cuius regio eius religio*).
- Calvin (Geneva, predestination, *Institutes* 1536); Zwingli (symbolic Eucharist); Henry VIII (1531).

## Catholic Reform
- **Trent (1545–63, Paul III)** — justification, scripture+tradition, seminaries. Pius V (Roman Catechism); Gregory XIII (calendar); Ignatius/Jesuits.

## Enlightenment → Revolution
- Galileo (1633); Quietism (Molinos), Jansenism (Jansen), Gallicanism (1682), Josephenism.
- **French Revolution (1789)** — Civil Constitution of the Clergy (1790), Reign of Terror (1793); Napoleon's concordat (1801).

## Vatican Councils
- **Vatican I (1869–70)** — *Syllabus of Errors* (1864); *Dei Filius*, *Pastor Aeternus* (infallibility, 18 July 1870).
- **Vatican II (1962–65)** — John XXIII, *aggiornamento*; 16 documents, four Constitutions; People of God.
