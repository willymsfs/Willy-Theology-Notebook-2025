# Src — Indian Church History

> [!info] Source note · theme: [[10 - Church History and the MSFS]]

**Five implantations**: Syrian (St Thomas) · Mercantile (Goa/Portuguese) · Agra (Jesuits + Mughals) · Tamil Nadu/Andhra (caste) · Tribal (Chotanagpur, NE India).

## St Thomas Christians
- Proofs: oral + written (Cosmos Indicopleustes 535; Acts of Judas Thomas) + Mylapore tomb (Marco Polo 1295).
- Thomas of Cana (~345); East Syriac/Nestorian hierarchy until 1599 (Catholicos of Babylon). High social status (*Nasrani Mappila*); led locally by the **Archdeacon**.

## Portuguese & Diamper
- Vasco da Gama **1498**; Goa seized **1510**, "Rome of the East"; Goa Inquisition **1560**.
- **Synod of Diamper (20–26 June 1599, Menezes)** — condemn the Patriarch, papal supremacy, correct/burn books; later reported not in proper form (forced signatures).
- **Coonan Cross Oath (3 Jan 1653)** — revolt (Ahatallah arrested) → split: Old Group (Syro-Malabar) / New Group (Malankara/Jacobite).

## Missionaries
- **Francis Xavier** (1542) — **Paravas** (mass conversion 1535); bell catechism, *Kanakkapillais*.
- **Mughal missions** (1580+) — Acquaviva, Jerome Xavier at Akbar's court (never converted).
- **de Nobili** (Madurai 1606) — Brahmin *sannyasi*, "accommodation" (later criticized for validating caste).

## Modern — tribal
- **Chotanagpur**: **Constant Lievens** (1885, defended tribals in court); **J.B. Hoffmann** (*Encyclopaedia Mundarica*, Cooperatives, **Chotanagpur Tenancy Act 1908**).
- **NE India**: Salvatorians (1890, Khasis); Salesians (1922, Louis Mathias, Constantine Vendrame "Apostle of the Khasis"); Garos, Nagas, Assam tea-garden Adivasis.
