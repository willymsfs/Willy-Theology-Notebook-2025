# Src — Pope Francis: Documents

> [!info] Source note · theme: [[07 - Ecclesiology and Ecclesial Documents]]

**Themes**: environment/social justice, mercy, pastoral accompaniment, synodality, reform, youth, interreligious dialogue.

## Key documents
- **Evangelii Gaudium** (2013, joy of the Gospel, missionary transformation).
- **Laudato Si'** (2015, integral ecology).
- **Amoris Laetitia** (2016, love in the family).
- **Gaudete et Exsultate** (2018, holiness).
- **Christus Vivit** (2019, young people).
- **Fratelli Tutti** (2020, fraternity/social friendship).
- **Querida Amazonia** (2020, Amazon).
- Mercy: *Misericordiae Vultus* (2015), *Misericordia et Misera* (2016). *Patris Corde* (2020, St Joseph).
- Law reforms: *Mitis Iudex* (2015, annulment), *Vos Estis Lux Mundi* (2019, abuse), *Spiritus Domini* (2021, women lectors/acolytes), *Traditionis Custodes* (2021, pre-1970 liturgy).

## Other
- **Document on Human Fraternity** (2019, with Grand Imam of Al-Azhar); Letter to the People of God (2018, abuse); UN address (2015); TED Talk (2017).
