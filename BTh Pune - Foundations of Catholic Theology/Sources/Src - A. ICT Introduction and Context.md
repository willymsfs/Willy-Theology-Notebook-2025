# Src — A. Indian Christian Theology: Introduction & Context

> [!info] Source note · theme: [[02 - Indian Christian Theology and Inculturation]]

## Context
- **Three confrontations**: Jewish, Greek, and now **Hindu** (adds depth to the gospel).
- **Religio-cultural**: India a "multi-source civilization"; Hinduism a banyan tree rooted in *Sanatana Dharma*, accommodating (*Sarva-Dharma-Sama-Bhava*).
- **Socio-political**: Christianity's "identity crisis" (foreign/colonial); caste, Hindutva, marginalization.
- **Subaltern confrontation**: response to Dalits, Tribals, Women.

## Definition
- Theology = "faith seeking transformation." **ICT** = reflection on God, Christ, Church where faith meets Indian people and worldviews.
- **Robin Boyd's "pot-plant"**: break the pot (Western structures) → the plant (message) blooms rooted in Indian soil.

## Need
- **De-westernize**; paradigm shift **Classical → Contextual**: from pyramid / "theology from above" / "helicopter theology" → inverted pyramid / "theology from below" / "bullock-cart theology" / orthopraxis.

## Sources (Robin Boyd)
- **Syrian** (St Thomas, 52 CE); **Bartholomew** (Kalyan); **Roman Catholic** (16th c. — Xavier, de Nobili, *sannyasa*); **Protestant** (18th c. — Ziegenbalg, Carey, translation); **Ashram Movement** (Monchanin, Abhishiktananda, Bede Griffiths); **Indian Renaissance** (Ram Mohan Roy, K.C. Sen).

## Methods
- **Dialectical dialogue** — "fusion of horizons" (NT text ↔ Indian context); "hermeneutical spiral."
- **Triple dialogue** (Poor, Cultures, Religions) + **Fourfold dialogue** (Life, Action, Theological Exchange, Religious Experience) — FABC.
- **Margas**: Jnana→Advaita (*Sat-Cit-Ananda*, God Beyond); Bhakti→Avatar (*Guru-Mauli*, God Within); Karma→Abba (*Satyagrahi*/Dalit, God With); Prema→Agape (The Way).
- **Subaltern**: Dalit (Crucified Jesus outside the gate), Tribal (*Jal, Jangal, Jamin*; Jesus as Drummer), Feminist.
