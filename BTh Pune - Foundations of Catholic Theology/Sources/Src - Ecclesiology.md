# Src — Ecclesiology

> [!info] Source note · theme: [[07 - Ecclesiology and Ecclesial Documents]]

## Nature
- ***Ekklesia*** = Hebrew *qahal* (assembly called by God) — quality/belonging, not quantity.
- **Origins**: Jesus laid foundations; the Church a "post-Easter phenomenon" (risen Lord + Spirit).
- **NT plurality**: Mark (service), Matthew (righteousness), Luke (Spirit), John (life/interdependency), Paul (Body of Christ).

## Models (Dulles)
Institution (*societas perfecta*; critiqued clericalism/juridicism) · Herald · Servant · Mystical Communion · Sacrament · Community of Disciples. No single model adequate.

## Vatican II (Lumen Gentium)
- **Mystery & Sacrament** — sign/instrument of communion; not identical to the Kingdom but its agent/sign.
- **People of God** — replaces "perfect society"; equal discipleship; pilgrim people.
- **Body of Christ** (unity in diversity); **Temple of the Spirit** (charisms).
- **Communion (*communio*)** — universal Church as a communion of local churches.

## Structure
- **Episcopal collegiality** — bishops' power from consecration, not delegation.
- **Subsidiarity/decentralization** — Synod of Bishops, Episcopal Conferences.
- **Papacy** — primacy of service; Vatican I (jurisdiction, infallibility) within collegiality.
- **Magisterium** — serves the Word; infallibility (extraordinary: ex-cathedra/councils; ordinary: bishops in agreement).

## Ecumenism
- Church of Christ **"subsists in"** the Catholic Church; **real but imperfect communion** via baptism; **hierarchy of truths**. In India: vital for a minority.

## Pope Francis
- **Poor Church for the poor** (peripheries); **listening/synodal** Church (*sensus fidei*, beyond clericalism); **Church of mercy** ("dispensary of mercy" — sacraments as medicine).
