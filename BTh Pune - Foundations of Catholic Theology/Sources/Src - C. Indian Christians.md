# Src — C. Indian Christian Theologians

> [!info] Source note · theme: [[02 - Indian Christian Theology and Inculturation]]

## Non-Catholic (mainline)
- **Brahmabandhav Upadhyay** (1861–1907) — "culturally Hindu, Christian by faith"; Vedanta; Trinity as **Saccidananda**; Christ as ***Nara-Hari*** (God-Man); Vedas = Indian OT.
- **Narayan Vaman Tilak** (1861–1919) — *bhakti*; ***abhangas*** (hymns), ***Christayana***; Christ as **Mother-Guru (*Guru-Mauli*)**; "Darbar of the Lord Jesus."
- **Pandita Ramabai** (1858–1922) — Sanskrit scholar; upliftment of women/child widows (Mukti Mission); translated the Bible into simple Marathi.
- **Dhanjibhai Fakirbhai** (1895–1967) — **Khristadvaita**; union of love; **Way of Love (*prema yoga*)**.
- **A.J. Appasamy** (1891–1975) — John via Tamil bhakti; **Moksa** = eternal life in Christ; Logos = *Antaryamin*; rejects Advaitic avatar.
- **Vengal Chakkarai** (1880–1958) — "Rethinking Group"; **Christhood of God**; Jesus the *satpurusha*; Christus-Victor.
- **Sadhu Sundar Singh** (1889–1929) — wandering *sadhu*; mystical, Christo-centric; *shanti, prema sagara, moksha*.
- **M.M. Thomas** (1916–1996) — humanization, secularization; Christianity as ***karma marga***; keeps the prophetic core.
- **Stanley J. Samartha** (1920–2001) — inter-religious dialogue; **theocentric (Mystery-centred) Christology**; "Bullock Cart Theology."

## Catholics
- **Raymond Panikkar** (1918–2010) — **"Unknown Christ of Hinduism"**; **Cosmotheandric Christology**; Rainbow/River models.
- **Sebastian Kappen** (1924–1993) — Marxist praxis; Jesus as Prophet/Liberator; avoids dogmatic titles.
- **George Soares-Prabhu** (1929–1995) — "Jesus of faith" and his **Dharma** (Abba, freedom, fellowship, justice); integral liberation, option for the poor. See [[George Soares-Prabhu]].
- **Samuel Rayan** (1920–2018) — grassroots praxis; Church as "Jesus movement"; ecological theology. See [[Samuel Rayan]].
- **Michael Amaladoss** (1936– ) — Jesus as **symbol** of the universal Christ; other religions as allies.
- **Felix Wilfred** (1948–2025) — "uniqueness" a Western issue; Jesus as *guru, satyagrahi, jivanmukta*; suffering as the connection point.
