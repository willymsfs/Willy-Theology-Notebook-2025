# Src — Social Teaching of the Church (STC)

> [!info] Source note · theme: [[08 - Contextual and Liberation Theologies]] · [[07 - Ecclesiology and Ecclesial Documents]]

**Foundation**: human dignity from the *imago Dei*; moral guidance (not ideology) via faith, tradition, the Spirit.

## Popes & encyclicals
- **Leo XIII** *Rerum Novarum* (industrial revolution; critiques abolition of private property).
- **John XXIII** *Mater et Magistra* (individual as foundation; adaptable).
- **Pius XI** *Mit Brennender Sorge* (vs Nazism); **Pius XII** (saving Jewish lives).
- **JP II** *Centesimus Annus*, *Sollicitudo Rei Socialis* ("reflection, judgment, action").
- **Francis** *Laudato Si'*, *Fratelli Tutti*.
- Also *Gaudium et Spes*, *Octogesima Adveniens*, **Justice in the World** (1971 Synod).

## Nature
Historical/adaptable · ethical (not technical) · sin/selfishness as root of exploitation · faith–reason harmony · **preferential option for the poor**.

## Sources (four)
1. Sacred Scripture (Amos, Acts, James). 2. Apostolic Tradition (Chrysostom, Basil — hunger neglect = murder). 3. Experiences of the Church (socialism, fascism, racism). 4. Discoveries of the sciences.

## Human dignity & rights
- *Imago Dei* → intelligence, freedom, stewardship. Dignity inherent, equal, universal; persons as ends.
- **Rights** universal/inalienable/indivisible; foremost = **right to life**; + duties.

## Five key principles
1. **Common Good** · 2. **Universal Destination of Goods** (priority over private property; option for the poor) · 3. **Subsidiarity** (most local level) · 4. **Participation** (right + duty) · 5. **Solidarity** (principle + virtue).
