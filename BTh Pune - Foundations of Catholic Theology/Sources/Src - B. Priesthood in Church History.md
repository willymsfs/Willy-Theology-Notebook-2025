# Src — B. Priesthood in Church History

> [!info] Source note · theme: [[05 - Priesthood]]

**Topic**: Evolution of the ministerial priesthood, NT → Vatican II.

## Early Church / NT
- Two views of origin: Jesus set structures / post-Easter Spirit-guided development.
- **Shunned cultic titles** (*hiereus/sacerdos*); used *apostoloi, presbyteroi, episkopoi, diakonoi*.
- Pastoral office preceded cultic function; laying on of hands = installation by the Spirit.

## Patristic — sacerdotalism
- After 70 CE "new Israel"; by **110 CE Ignatius** — 3-tier hierarchy.
- Late 3rd–4th c.: ministers called "priests"; sacred–secular divide; state religion → clergy "set apart" like Levites; cultic purity → celibacy, tonsure.

## Medieval — hierarchicalism
- **Pseudo-Dionysius** — strict tiers, passive laity, grace "trickles down."
- Corruption: simony, nepotism, selling indulgences; upper vs lower clergy.
- **Scholasticism**: priesthood ≈ divine power to consecrate the Eucharist; "sacred person."

## Reformation
- **Luther** — common priesthood of all; ordination doesn't change a person; pulpit over altar.
- **Calvin** — only Christ is priest; denies Holy Orders as a sacrament.

## Trent (1545–63)
- Defends 7 sacraments; **indelible character** of Orders; valid *ex opere operato*.
- Shortcoming: no "People of God" context; only cultic power, ignored the Word.

## Vatican II →
- **People of God**, communion model. *Lumen Gentium*: common + ministerial priesthood **differ in essence not only degree**, mutually ordered.
- Three degrees: Episcopate (fullness), Presbyterate, restored permanent **Diaconate** (married men).
- *Presbyterorum Ordinis*: Prophet/Priest/King; servant of Word, Sacraments, community; *in persona Christi*.
- Formation (*Optatam Totius* → *Pastores Dabo Vobis*): four stages + **SHIP** (Spiritual, Human, Intellectual, Pastoral).
