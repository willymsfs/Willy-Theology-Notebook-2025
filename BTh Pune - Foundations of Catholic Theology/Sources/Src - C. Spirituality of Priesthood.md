# Src — C. Spirituality of Priesthood & Special Questions

> [!info] Source note · theme: [[05 - Priesthood]]

## The priest today — Servant Model
Integrates Jurisdictional, Cultic, Pastoral, Prophetic, Monastic, Servant models. **Servant** the key:
- **Servant of the Word** — *alter Christus*; the *Vox* transmitting the *Verbum* (Augustine/Ratzinger); prophet sowing the Word (crucified/risen Christ).
- **Servant of servants** (*diakonos*) — empower community, foster *koinonia*.
- **Servant of the Sacraments** — *Sacramentum Christi*; Francis: identity in the Eucharist + Reconciliation.
- **Servant of the World** — kingdom-oriented, solidarity with the poor, justice, ecology.

## Spirituality
- Holiness = wholeness; harmony between "Altar of the World" and "Altar of the Eucharist."
- **Kenotic** (Paschal Mystery) + **Shepherd** (Good Shepherd). Eucharist at the centre.

## Special questions
- **Celibacy**: charism, eschatological sign. Clergy mostly married to the 3rd c.; Elvira → continence; **2nd Lateran (1139)** makes it law. Paul VI (*Sacerdotalis Caelibatus*), JP II, Francis uphold it (undivided heart).
- **Married priests**: banned in the Latin Church since 1123; exist in Eastern rites; 1980 Pastoral Provision (convert Protestant clergy).
- **Sexual abuse scandal**: Francis' Aug 2018 letter — acknowledge suffering, beg forgiveness, prevent cover-ups, condemn "clericalism."
- **Ordination of women**: "only a baptized male validly receives ordination" (*Ordinatio Sacerdotalis*, JP II 1994) — Jesus chose male apostles, tradition, *in persona Christi*. Arguments in favour (Mary, Mary Magdalene; early prophetic/diaconal roles); dissent (Ludmila Javorova) declared invalid. Protestants ordain women (priesthood of all believers).

## Francis' 10 insights (selected)
Anchor in Christ · "smell of the sheep" · quality preaching · simple life, the poor · integrity/no clericalism · authority for service · mercy · blessing · joy of the Gospel · missionary disciple. *Bewares*: activity without spirituality, pessimism, elitism, not empowering laity/women.
