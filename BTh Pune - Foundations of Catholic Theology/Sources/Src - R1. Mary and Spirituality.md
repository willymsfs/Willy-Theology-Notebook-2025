# Src — R1. Mary and Spirituality

> [!info] Source note (reading, C. O'Donnell) · theme: [[09 - Christian Spirituality and Mary]]

**Thesis**: Marian spirituality is not one option among many but the **fundamental paradigm** of all Christian spiritualities, rooted in Mary's total "yes."

## Definitions
- Spirituality a "chameleon" word (Schneiders: self-transcendence toward the Triune God via the paschal mystery).
- **Devotion vs spirituality** (Beinert): not praying *to* Mary but **praying *like* Mary**; Mary the model, never the goal (Christ is).

## Spirituality OF Mary (four images)
1. **Servant** (*doulé*; ~ Philippians 2 self-emptying). 2. **Servant of the Word / Disciple** (Augustine — disciple counts more than mother). 3. **Woman of Faith** (Vatican II "pilgrimage of faith"). 4. **Woman of the Spirit & the *anawim*** ("a woman for others").

## Marian spirituality
- **East vs West**: East unites dogma + spirituality (iconic Mother); West split (Late Middle Ages) → Mary as near-divine miracle-worker.
- **Von Balthasar**: Mary resolves all spiritualities into the one spirituality of the Church; **any authentic Christian spirituality is inherently Marian.**

## Marian mysticism
- Teresa of Avila, John of the Cross (supreme contemplative); **"Mariform" mysticism** ("God in Mary and Mary in God"); the Scapular.
