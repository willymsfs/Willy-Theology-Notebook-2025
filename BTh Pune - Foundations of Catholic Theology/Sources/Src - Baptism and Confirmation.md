# Src — Baptism and Confirmation

> [!info] Source note · theme: [[04 - The Sacraments]]

## Baptism
- **Topic**: Sacrament of Regeneration; gateway to life in the Spirit, "identity card," entry into the Paschal Mystery.
- **Scripture**: prefigured — creation waters (Gen 1:1–2), Flood/Ark, Red Sea, rock at Horeb. Jesus' baptism (Mt 3:13–17); mandate (Mt 28:19); necessity (Jn 3:5, born of water and Spirit); dying/rising (Rom 6:1–11).
- **Theology**: "Sacrament of the Dead" (restores lost grace); necessary for salvation — also Baptism of Blood (martyrdom) / Desire; **infant baptism** (Augustine, Trent) frees from Original Sin.
- **Matter**: water (immersion/affusion). **Form (Latin)**: "I baptize you in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit"; **(Eastern)**: "The servant of God, N., is baptized…". **Minister**: bishop/priest/deacon; in necessity anyone with right intention.
- **Effects**: *res tantum* — forgiveness of all sins, adoptive sonship, incorporation into the Church; *res et sacramentum* — indelible character (unrepeatable).

## Confirmation
- **Topic**: Sacrament of Intensification — completes baptismal grace, mature discipleship, strength to witness.
- **Scripture**: Messiah endowed with the Spirit (Is 11:2); promise (Lk 12:12; Jn 16:7–15; Acts 1:8); Pentecost (Acts 2:1–4); laying on of hands (Acts 8:14–17; 19:5–6); "sealed" (2 Cor 1:21–22; Eph 1:13–14).
- **Theology**: originally one rite with Baptism; separated in the West (4th–6th c.) for the Bishop; "Sacrament of the Living"; Aquinas — strength for spiritual battle, supernatural maturity.
- **Matter**: Chrism on the forehead with laying on of hands. **Form**: "Be sealed with the gift of the Holy Spirit." **Minister**: ordinarily the Bishop (delegated priest in mission/danger).
- **Effects**: outpouring of the Spirit; **seven gifts** (wisdom, understanding, knowledge, wonder & awe, reverence, right judgment, courage); indelible character; perfects the common priesthood.
