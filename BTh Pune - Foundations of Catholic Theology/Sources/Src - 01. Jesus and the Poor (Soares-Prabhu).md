# Src — 01. Jesus and the Poor (Soares-Prabhu)

> [!info] Source note (reading) · themes: [[03 - Christology and Indian Christology]] · [[08 - Contextual and Liberation Theologies]] · author [[George Soares-Prabhu]]

**Thesis**: Jesus offered no economic blueprint but a **religious response** rooted in the *Abba* experience → radical identification with the poor, effective/structural concern, a "contrast community" abolishing oppressive poverty + "spiritual poverty."

## Context
- 1st-c. Palestine: Roman exploitation, heavy taxation, absentee landlords → rural proletariat.
- **Poor (*anawim*)**: economically destitute + socially marginalized (outcasts, lepers, despised trades).
- Poverty = result of sin/injustice, **not** fate or *karma*.

## Jesus' fourfold response
1. **Radical identification** — de-classed itinerant (begging); fellowship with outcasts; rural mission.
2. **Effective concern (Love Commandment)** — healing; love of neighbour on a level with love of God; fight structural causes.
3. **End to oppressive poverty** — manifesto (Lk 4:16–21), Jubilee; Beatitudes end evil structures (not afterlife consolation).
4. **Kingdom / contrast community** — free, fraternal, non-exploitative; shared goods; "spiritual poverty" (anti-consumerism) + change of heart.

## Conclusions
- **Differentiated love**: in an unjust world love "takes sides with the oppressed"; blesses the poor, condemns riches; cannot tolerate caste.
- **For today**: not a strategy but the *Abba* experience, humanity as family, values of freedom/love, commitment to a non-exploitative community.
