# Src — B. Pioneers and Reformers of ICT

> [!info] Source note · theme: [[02 - Indian Christian Theology and Inculturation]]

**Context**: 19th c. = most vital period; Neo-Hindu movements engage Christ; making theology indigenous.

## Non-Indian Catholic pioneers
- **Thomas Stephens** (1549–1619) — "Father of Inculturation"; **Khristapurana** (Marathi/Konkani, Vaishnavite tradition); avoided *avatar/punarjanma*; *puja of Swami* (sacrifice), *adipurushache karma* (original sin), *jnana-snana* (baptism).
- **Robert de Nobili** (1577–1656) — Brahmin *sannyasi* in Madurai; top-down (Brahmins first); kept Indian customs; coined vocabulary — Christ as **"Guru."**
- **C.J. Beschi** (1680–1746, *Viramamunivar*) — Tamil *Pandarasami*; shift Sanskritization → **Tamilization**; **Thembavani** (Tamil epic on St Joseph).
- **Abhishiktananda / Henri Le Saux** (1910–1973) — *sannyasi*, Saccidananda Ashram; Trinity as **Saccidananda**; Advaitic "I AM"; Jesus the Awakened One, *Sadguru*.
- **Bede Griffiths** (1906–1993) — **Christian Advaita** (soul remains distinct); **Cosmic Christ** hidden in all religions.

## Indian reformers
- **Raja Ram Mohan Roy** (1774–1833) — Brahmo Samaj; *The Precepts of Jesus*; Christian **ethics not dogma** (Arian — rejects deity/Trinity/atonement); love of God + *seva*.
- **Keshab Chandra Sen** (1838–1884) — *Navavidhan*; **"Asiatic Christ"**; first to expound the Trinity as *Saccidananda*.
- **Mahatma Gandhi** (1869–1948) — *Satya* + *Ahimsa*; Jesus the **"Prince of Satyagrahis"**; orthopraxis; rejects Jesus' historical uniqueness (all religions equal).
- **Swami Vivekananda** (1863–1902) — Ramakrishna Mission; Jesus as Vedantin/yogi, *jivanmukta*; rejects sin, cross, atonement.
