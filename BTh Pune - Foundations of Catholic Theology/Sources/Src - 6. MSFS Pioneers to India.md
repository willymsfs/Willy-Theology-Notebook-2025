# Src — 6. MSFS Pioneers to India

> [!info] Source note · theme: [[10 - Church History and the MSFS]]

**Topic**: The six MSFS pioneers from Savoy to India, 1845 (compiled by Fr. Yves Carron for the 175th anniversary, 24 Oct 2020). Mission established under **Fr. Peter Marie Mermier**; 23 May 1845 letter appoints Fr. Martin director.

## Journey
- Embarked **Bordeaux, 5 June 1845** on **"Courrier de l'Inde"** (23 passengers, 16 priests — incl. 4 MEP, 8 Jesuits).
- **19 Aug** Mauritius (met Jacques-Désiré Laval); **8 Sept 1845** Pondicherry; **Feb 1846** Yanam + Visakhapatnam (the goal).

## The six pioneers
1. **Fr. Jacques Martin** (43) — Superior of the batch; **architect** (Mother House Annecy 1837); first to die, Yanam **5 May 1846**.
2. **Fr. Jean-Marie Tissot** (35) — 2nd Superior (1846); **Vicar Apostolic 1863, Bishop of Visakhapatnam 1886**; Vatican I (1870); **45 years** in India.
3. **Fr. Jean Thévenet** (28) — convinced Mermier to request an overseas mission; built churches at **Aurangabad, Nagpur cathedral, Kamptee**.
4. **Fr. Joseph Lavorel** (44) — parish priest Kamptee; eyesight problems; died on a bullock cart **5 Nov 1865**.
5. **Bro. Pierre Carton** (40) — carpenter; **first lay Brother of the MSFS**; copied a Tamil dictionary; died Visakhapatnam after 5 years.
6. **Bro. Sulpice Fontanel** (26) — youngest; professed one day before sailing; gardener; struggled with languages; died of cholera at Samalkot after 15 years.
