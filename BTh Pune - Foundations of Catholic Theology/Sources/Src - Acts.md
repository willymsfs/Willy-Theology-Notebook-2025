# Src — The Acts of the Apostles

> [!info] Source note · theme: [[01 - New Testament Background and World]]

**Topic**: Christianity's expansion from Jerusalem to Rome; sequel to Luke (**Luke–Acts**), continuing the Risen Lord's work through His witnesses. For "Theophilus"; defends the Gentile mission, no political threat to Rome.

## Authorship
- Traditionally **Luke** (Syrian physician, Paul's companion). **"We-sections"** (16:10–17; 27:1–28:16) → eyewitness. Date **70–90 CE** (mid-80s), likely Rome.

## Structure (geographical, Acts 1:8)
1. Preparation (1:1–26). 2. Jerusalem (2:1–5:42). 3. Judea/Samaria/Antioch (6:1–15:35). 4. Paul to Rome (15:36–28:31).

## Themes
- **God controls history** (*dei*, "it is necessary"). **Promises kept** to Israel (righteous remnant). **Gentile mission** (not Plan B; Spirit-directed). **Jesus absent yet present** (Spirit, Word, the Name). **"Gospel of the Holy Spirit"** (external power for witness). **Lukan generosity** (poor, outcasts, pagans). **Growth through suffering** (joyful martyrdom).

## Key events
- Pentecost (Ch 2, 3,000 converts); communal sharing; Ananias & Sapphira. **Stephen** first martyr (6–7). **Philip** — Samaritans, Ethiopian eunuch (8). **Conversion of Saul** (9). **Cornelius** — Gentiles, no partiality (10–11). **Jerusalem Council** (15) — no circumcision for Gentiles; "Christians" first at Antioch. Paul's journeys (Areopagus at Athens, Ephesus riot); arrest/trials (Felix, Festus, Agrippa); shipwreck at Malta; Rome under house arrest.
