# Src — 2. Reconciliation (theology, history, practice)

> [!info] Source note · theme: [[04 - The Sacraments]] · continues [[Src - 1. Reconciliation Introduction and Scripture]]

## History
- **Apostolic**: no formal sacrament; mutual correction, Baptism; excommunication for the unrepentant.
- **Patristic (1st–6th c.)**: **Canonical Penance** — once in a lifetime, severe, public; bishop as judge.
- **Middle Ages (7th–15th c.)**: Irish missionaries → private, repeatable "tariff" penance; **Fourth Lateran 1215** mandates annual confession + strict seal; Scholastics distinguish eternal/temporal punishment → indulgences → abuses.
- **Reformation & Trent**: Reformers reject priestly forgiveness (faith + baptism alone); **Trent** defends institution by Christ (Jn 20:22–23), defines matter/form, affirms private confession + necessity of absolution.
- **Vatican II**: from juridical pyramid to community/Christocentric model — reconciled with God *and* the wounded Church.

## Theology
- **Matter** (acts of the penitent): **Contrition** (perfect = love of God / imperfect = fear), **Confession** (all mortal sins ≥ once a year), **Satisfaction** (prayer, fasting, almsgiving).
- **Form**: words of **Absolution** ("I absolve you…").
- **Effects**: forgiveness, healing, removal of eternal punishment, reintegration into communion.
- **Minister**: validly ordained priest/bishop with jurisdiction, *in persona Christi* (healer, judge, father, physician).

## Key points
- **Sacramental seal**: absolute secrecy; direct violation → automatic (*latae sententiae*) excommunication.
- **Three rites (*Ordo Paenitentiae*)**: individual · communal with individual confession · communal with general absolution (danger of death only).
- **Three models**: Therapeutic · Juridical · Relational.
- **Challenge**: modern "loss of the sense of sin" (secularism, relativism); Francis — mercy, "Missionaries of Mercy."
