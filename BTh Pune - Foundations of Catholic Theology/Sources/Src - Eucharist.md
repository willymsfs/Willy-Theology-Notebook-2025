# Src — Eucharist

> [!info] Source note · theme: [[04 - The Sacraments]]

**Topic**: Eucharist as meal + memorial sacrifice — "source and summit," a program for the whole of Christian life.

## Scripture
- **OT**: Jewish meals (Gen 18:1–8 hospitality; Jer 16:7), Passover/Exodus, Sinai covenant sealed with blood + meal (Ex 24:8).
- **Jesus**: meals symbolizing God's reign, eating with sinners (Lk 15; Mk 2:17); Last Supper self-gift; Emmaus (Lk 24) + Jn 21 — "breaking of the bread."
- **Paul**: 1 Cor 11:20–34 — criticizes divisions; criteria (giving, body, for you, covenant, proclaiming Christ's death). Institution: Mk 14 / Mt 26 (cup, blood, covenant, forgiveness).

## Theology
- **Sacrifice**: self-giving of Jesus; Aquinas distinguishes immolation vs oblation; **Trent** — the Mass re-presents (not repeats) the one sacrifice.
- **Memorial (anamnesis)**: active re-enactment holding past + present (Spirit) + future (eschatology).
- **Real Presence**: Reformation split — Luther "in, with, and under"; Zwingli (believer transformed); Calvin (Spirit lifts us to Christ). **Vatican II** — presence in bread/wine, Word, community, Christ.
- **Transubstantiation**: accidents remain, substance changes into body and blood (11th c.; Aquinas; Trent).

## Matter / Form / Effects
- **Matter**: bread and wine (whole person/life of Jesus). **Form**: the *Anaphora* — words of institution + **Epiclesis** (Spirit transforms gifts + community).
- **Effects**: union/communion (makes the Church), forgiveness, transformation of self-seeking individuals into a self-giving community, **social mission** ("Eucharistic toil" — justice, hunger, ecology).

## Indian understanding
- **Yajnam** — sacrifice maintaining *Rta* (cosmic order); self-giving mirrors the Eucharist.
- Meals in India = "Communion and Division" — sharing/restriction across caste and class.
