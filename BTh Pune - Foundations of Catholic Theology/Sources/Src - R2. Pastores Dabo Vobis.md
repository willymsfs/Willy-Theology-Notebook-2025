# Src — R2. Pastores Dabo Vobis ("I Will Give You Shepherds")

> [!info] Source note (reading, JP II 1992) · theme: [[05 - Priesthood]]

**Thesis**: form priests as living images of Christ the Head/Good Shepherd via holistic, lifelong formation (human, spiritual, intellectual, pastoral).

## Context & identity
- World: thirst for values + obstacles (rationalism, subjectivism, practical atheism, consumerism). "Scrutinize the signs of the times."
- Priest **configured to Christ** (Head, Shepherd, Spouse); animating force = **pastoral charity**; radicalism of the Gospel (obedience, celibacy, poverty). Celibacy a "priceless gift."

## Four pillars
1. **Human** (foundation) — personality as bridge not obstacle; affective maturity, integrity, freedom.
2. **Spiritual** (heart) — union with the Trinity; *lectio divina*, Eucharist ("summit and source"), Penance, Christ in the poor.
3. **Intellectual** — philosophy + theology for the new evangelization; fidelity to the magisterium.
4. **Pastoral** (goal) — true shepherds; inner sensitivity, not just technique; communion + mission.

## Setting & ongoing formation
- Major seminary = "educational ecclesial community." Agents: bishop, rector, directors, professors; family/parish; ultimately **self-formation**.
- **Ongoing formation** — lifelong ("rekindle the gift"); for young/middle/elderly priests; overcome activism.
