# Src — MSFS before the Indian Foundation

> [!info] Source note · theme: [[10 - Church History and the MSFS]] · from Adrien Duval, *Monsieur Mermier (1790–1862)*

**Topic**: Fr. Pierre-Marie Mermier's life, the post-Revolution crisis in Savoy, parish missions, and the founding of the MSFS before India.

## Mermier's life
- Born **28 Aug 1790**, Vouray (Chaumont en Genevois); witnessed the Reign of Terror (secret Mass on the farm).
- Seminary of Chambéry 1807; ordained **21 March 1813** (age 23); curate at Magland, Melan, Samoens.
- **1819** parish priest of Le Châtelard (spiritually indifferent); **1821** parish mission with **Abbé Joseph-Marie Favre** → both dedicate their lives to the mission apostolate.
- **1823** spiritual director at the Annecy major seminary.

## Situation in Savoy & parish missions
- Post-Revolution: religious ignorance, indifference, priest shortage; Geneva's anti-Catholic influence.
- **Jansenism** (rigorist) discouraged the sacraments; Mermier championed **St Alphonsus Liguori** — trust and love.
- **Parish Missions** (1–5 weeks): catechesis, mass confessions, reconciliations, huge enthusiasm.

## Founding the congregation
- Favre's "Oblates of St Francis de Sales" (Chambéry) collapsed; Mermier gathered Annecy missioners (Martin, Ducroz, Gaiddon, Petitjean, Cheminal).
- **La Roche-sur-Foron (1834–37)** — community life; Mermier + Petitjean draft the **Rule** (own rule for parish missioners; inspired by Lazarists, Jesuits, Redemptorists); Bishop Rey provisional approval **29 Sept 1836**.
- **La Feuillette** (Annecy Mother House) — foundation stone 6 April 1836, blessed **8 Aug 1837**.
- **First vows 24 Sept 1838**; canonical establishment of the **MSFS** by Bishop Rey **24 Oct 1838**; patron **St Francis de Sales** (gentleness, zeal); entrusted with Les Allinges.
