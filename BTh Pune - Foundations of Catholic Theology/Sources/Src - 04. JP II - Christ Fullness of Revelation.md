# Src — 04. JP II: Christ, Mediator and Fullness of Revelation

> [!info] Source note (reading) · themes: [[03 - Christology and Indian Christology]] · [[07 - Ecclesiology and Ecclesial Documents]]

**Context**: JP II address to the CDF (Jan 28); refutes relativism, reaffirms uniqueness of Christ + Church.

- **Thesis**: Christ is "mediator and the fullness of all revelation"; definitive/full revelation given entirely in Jesus; human language does not diminish it — "full and complete."
- **Vs relativism**: condemns the "indifferentist mentality" ("one religion is as good as another"); rejects a "limited"/complementary Christ.
- **Uniqueness of the Church**: one Body — "one, holy, catholic and apostolic"; necessary for salvation; not merely one way among religions.
- **Non-Christians**: can gain eternal life under grace if seeking God sincerely, but in an "unsatisfactory situation" vs the fullness of the means of salvation → duty to proclaim Christ.
- **Ecumenism**: *Ut Unum Sint*; the Church "subsists in the Catholic Church."
