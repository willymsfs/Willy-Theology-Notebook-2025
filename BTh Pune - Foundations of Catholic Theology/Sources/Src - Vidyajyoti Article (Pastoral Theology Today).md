# Src — Pastoral Theology Today (Vidyajyoti)

> [!info] Source note (reading, G. Lobo SJ, *Vidyajyoti* Jan 1976) · theme: [[07 - Ecclesiology and Ecclesial Documents]]

**Thesis**: Pastoral Theology is no longer practical "tips" for priests but a comprehensive discipline — the **whole Church** (clergy + laity) discerning its mission to transform society, integrating human sciences.

## Key themes
- **Evolution**: 18th-c. priest-centred directives → post-Vatican II (*Gaudium et Spes*) corporate mission transforming society.
- **Laity**: "declericalize"; laity share Christ's priestly/prophetic/kingly functions; transform structures.
- **Family apostolate**: the "domestic church"; marriage prep; human love as channel of grace.
- **Territorial parish**: enduring sign, but challenged by mobility → open to professional/social organizations.
- **Ecumenism & evangelisation**: healing divisions; salvific value of non-Christian religions (preserve "true and holy").
- **Indigenisation**: express Christianity in local culture (liturgy free of superstition), not imposition.
- **Development/liberation/politics**: social justice, transform unjust structures; non-involvement favours the status quo.
- **Moral guidance**: form mature consciences (not ready-made solutions); balance psychology with moral principles.
