# Src — R8. Eucharist: An Indian Christian Understanding

> [!info] Source note (reading, Richard Lopes SJ, *Jnanadeepa* 2022) · themes: [[04 - The Sacraments]] · [[03 - Christology and Indian Christology]]

**Full title**: "Eucharist as Thanksgiving for Khrist-Preet-Samskar."

**Thesis**: the Eucharist maps to the three Indian *margas* (Jnana, Bhakti, Karma), merging in the **Prema Marga (*Khrist-Preet-Samskar*, Sacrament of Love)**.

## The four paths
1. **Jnana Marga** — *Khrist-Pragatya-Samskar* (Real Presence): intellect, Word + Transubstantiation; Christian *Advaita* = communion of Jesus and the Father.
2. **Bhakti Marga** — *Khrist-Prasad-Samskar* (Grace/Communion): receive the Lord in the heart; *Antaryamin*; grace (*Prasad*) augments union.
3. **Karma Marga** — *Khrist-Yagna-Samskar* (Self-giving): "break and share"; *Nishkama Karma*, *Lokasamgraha*; "Church of the Apron" (foot-washing); table fellowship with Dalits.
4. **Prema Marga** — *Khrist-Preet-Samskar* (Love): Jesus' agapic love; integrates knowing (jnana), abiding (bhakti), following (karma) — head, heart, hands.

## Conclusion
Eucharist = primordial symbol of Christian spirituality; fulfils the longing for *Moksha* via a complete three-way journey integrated by love.
