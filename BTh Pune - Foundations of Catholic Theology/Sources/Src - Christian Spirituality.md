# Src — Christian Spirituality (schools & traditions)

> [!info] Source note · theme: [[09 - Christian Spirituality and Mary]]

## Nature
- Term coined by **St Paul** — "spiritual" vs "flesh" (not vs matter).
- **Definition**: conscious life-integration through self-transcendence toward ultimate value; Christian = the Triune God in Christ, paschal mystery, Spirit.
- **8 marks**: grace/faith · life in the Spirit · Christocentric · Trinitarian · communitarian · just · prayerful · divinizing.
- **3 levels**: real/existential · groups/traditions · academic discipline.

## Schools & figures
- **Martyrdom** — Ignatius of Antioch (martyrdom as ultimate eucharist).
- **Alexandria** — Origen (threefold way; spiritual senses).
- **Desert (Eastern monasticism)** — eremitic/cenobitic; Anthony (Father of Monasticism), Pachomius (cenobitic Rule), Evagrius (*praktike/gnostike*, **Eight Evil Thoughts**).
- **Mystical theology** — Pseudo-Dionysius (Kataphatic/Apophatic).
- **Western monasticism** — John Cassian (Purity of Heart, monologistos → Jesus Prayer); **Benedict** ("Ora et Labora"; humility, hospitality; vows stability/fidelity/obedience).
- **Cistercian** — Bernard of Clairvaux (Four Degrees of Love: carnal, mercenary, filial, mystical).
- **Mendicant** — Francis of Assisi (*vita evangelica*, kenosis, joy, creation).
- **Late medieval mysticism** — Meister Eckhart (Godhead vs God, inward poverty), *Cloud of Unknowing* (Cloud of Forgetting), Julian of Norwich (Motherhood of God, "All shall be well").
- **Devotio Moderna** — Thomas à Kempis (*Imitation of Christ*).
- **Carmelite** — Teresa of Avila (*Interior Castle*; four degrees of prayer — well, windlass, stream, rain).
- **Salesian** — Francis de Sales (*Introduction to the Devout Life*; universal call to holiness, heart-centred, "little virtues").
