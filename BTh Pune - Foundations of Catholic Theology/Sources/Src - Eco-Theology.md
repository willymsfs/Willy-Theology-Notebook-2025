# Src — Eco-Theology

> [!info] Source note · theme: [[08 - Contextual and Liberation Theologies]]

**Topic**: Theological/spiritual response to the ecological crisis — **Cosmotheandric Spirituality** (Divine–human–cosmos link).

## Concerns ("deformed face of Mother Earth")
Contaminated water · deforestation · vanishing species · environmental refugees · excess consumption (20% faster than regeneration) · climate chaos · declining health (Bhopal) · developmental destruction (Narmada dams) · globalization commodifying nature · illogic of the Green Revolution.

## Integral ecology
- **Panentheism**: universe as "visible sacrament of the invisible God."
- **Trinitarian communion**: cosmos reflects the Trinity; Spirit as life-force.
- Cosmos as extended family (*vasudhaivakutumbakam*); ecology = *oikos* (home).

## Laudato Si'
- *Not* explicitly named in this document; aligns with it. Cites **JP II** (*Sollicitudo Rei Socialis*).

## Biblical basis
- Creation "very good" (Gen 1); stewardship not domination (*kabash/radah* misread; *shomrei* — guardians); cosmic covenant (Noah, Gen 9); Sabbath/Jubilee (Lev 25); prophets link injustice + devastation; **Jesus the Cosmic Liberator** (Col 1:15–20).

## Thinkers
Leonardo Boff · Teilhard de Chardin ("nothing here below is profane") · Ramanuja (*Visistadvaita*, world as Body of God) · Daniel Quinn (*Ishmael*, takers/leavers) · prophets: Rachel Carson, Sundar Lal Bahuguna (Chipko), Medha Patkar (Narmada), Vandana Shiva.

## Indian eco-theology
- **Hinduism**: *Isavasyam idam sarvam*; *Rta*, *Yajna*, *Dharma*; sacred rivers/trees.
- **Tribal**: earth a gift of *Singbonga*; "anti-greed"; web of nature.
- **Buddhism/Jainism**: *Ahimsa*. **Sikhism**: shared divine light.
