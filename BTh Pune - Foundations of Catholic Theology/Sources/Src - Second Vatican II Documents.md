# Src — Second Vatican II: Documents & Decrees

> [!info] Source note · theme: [[07 - Ecclesiology and Ecclesial Documents]]

**Themes**: role of the laity · dialogue with the modern world · ecumenism · collegiality · *ressourcement* · *aggiornamento*.

## Four Constitutions
- **Gaudium et Spes** — Church in the modern world (dignity, social justice).
- **Sacrosanctum Concilium** — liturgy (vernacular, active participation).
- **Lumen Gentium** — the Church (universal call to holiness, hierarchy + laity).
- **Dei Verbum** — Scripture, Tradition, Magisterium.

## Nine Decrees
*Inter Mirifica* (media) · *Orientalium Ecclesiarum* (Eastern Churches) · *Unitatis Redintegratio* (ecumenism) · *Christus Dominus* (bishops) · *Perfectae Caritatis* (religious life) · *Optatam Totius* (priestly training) · *Apostolicam Actuositatem* (laity) · *Presbyterorum Ordinis* (priests) · *Ad Gentes* (mission).

## Three Declarations
*Dignitatis Humanae* (religious freedom) · *Nostra Aetate* (non-Christian religions) · *Gravissimum Educationis* (education).
