# Src — Jesus and Spirituality: Reading John in Indian Culture

> [!info] Source note (reading, Johnson Thomaskutty, *Religions* 2021) · themes: [[02 - Indian Christian Theology and Inculturation]] · [[03 - Christology and Indian Christology]]

**Thesis**: John's universalistic, inclusive spirituality accommodates pluralistic India; parallels create a "third space" for dialogue and transformation.

## Parallels
- **Early receptions**: Ram Mohan Roy (John + Upanishads), Sundar Singh (love), Appasamy (*Bhakti Marga*).
- **Word/Logos**: *Sat/Cit*, *Saccidananda* (Upadhyaya); *Avatara* (Jn 1:14); *Om/Adisabda*.
- **Three margas**: Jnana ("knowing"/"believing"), Karma (the Father's "work"), Bhakti (devoted following).
- **Guru-Shishya**: Jesus as walking teacher (~ Dronacharya, Buddha, Sikh Gurus).
- **Signs (*sēmeia*)**: unconditional signs generate faith (appeal to India's sick/poor).
- **"I AM" + action**: "walks his talk" (~ Gandhi, Ramabai, Mother Teresa).
- **Character resonance**: Nathanael under the fig tree ~ Buddha under the Bodhi tree; Samaritan woman ~ Ananda + Matanga woman.
- **Mysticism**: oneness ~ *Atman is Brahman* / *tathata*; qualified dualism closest to **Madhva's Dvaita**.
- **Socio-political**: expelled minority ~ Indian Christians; subaltern focus (Dalits, Tribals, women).
