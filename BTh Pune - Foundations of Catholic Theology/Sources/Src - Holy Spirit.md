# Src — Holy Spirit (Theology of the Holy Spirit)

> [!info] Source note · theme: [[06 - Holy Spirit (Pneumatology)]]

**Topic**: Exam study guide (Mohan Doss SVD) — 12 viva topics on the Theology of the Holy Spirit.

## Who the Spirit is
- A living, dynamic, loving **Person** dwelling in believers; the **"GIFT"** par excellence (Augustine: a proper name); Eastern "golden link" to Son and Father.
- **Spirit of Christ**: "no Pneumatology without Christology."

## OT / NT
- **OT**: Spirit foreshadowed in God's presence (Tent, Ark); Moses, Abraham; Pentecost = partial fulfilment of Moses'/Joel's visions.
- **NT**: fullness a **post-Easter gift**. Luke — overshadows Mary (Lk 1:35), anoints Jesus. John — Risen Christ gives the Spirit ("living water," "breath"). Paul — justification, **"new creation," temple** (1 Cor 6:16).

## Paraclete / titles / symbols
- **Functions**: Helper in Jesus' self-surrender; does what Jesus did — teaching, judging, guiding into truth.
- **Titles**: "Lord" (Vatican II), "Creator Spirit," "Spirit of adoption/freedom/holiness," "the Helper," "GIFT."
- **Symbols**: water ("living water"), breath (~ *Prana*), fire (~ *Agni*), temple.

## Work of the Spirit
- In Jesus: Creator, Shaper, Helper, Universal donor.
- In the believer: justifies, transforms into temples; renewal, sanctification, prayer, resurrection.
- In Church/mission: animates, distributes charisms (Charismatic Renewal), empowers evangelization.
- In the world: beyond the visible Church — liberation, ecology, women's rights, justice.

## Indian/Asian pneumatology (FABC 81)
- Pneumatology **"from below"**; Asian pluralism = "great gift of the Spirit"; **Power of the Powerless** (Church of the poor).
- Hindu resonances: *Atman* (Self), *Prana* (breath ~ ru'ah/pneuma), *Antaryamin* (inner controller), *Sakti* (energy).
