# Src — R3. Religious Communities of and for the Kingdom of God

> [!info] Source note (reading, Richard Lopes SJ on Soares-Prabhu) · themes: [[05 - Priesthood]] · [[03 - Christology and Indian Christology]]

**Thesis**: priests/religious must ground in Jesus' **Abba experience** and **Agape Marga**, becoming prophetic **contrast communities** of freedom, fellowship, justice. (Closely parallels [[Src - R1. George Soares-Prabhu]].)

## Key points
- **Indian context**: poverty, pluriform religiosity, caste = *samsara*.
- **Abba experience** → all are siblings (no caste/gender/race discrimination) → freedom, fellowship, justice.
- **Agape Marga** (not jnana/karma/bhakti) — love of God ≡ love of neighbour.
- **Threefold mission**: Priest (solidarity, holiness = mercy), Prophet (confrontation), Pastor (liberative pedagogy — change of heart + structures).
- **Table fellowship** — "Christian Dalit" a contradiction; Eucharist binds divided peoples.
- **Call today**: cultic *pujaris* → community builders; prophetic "salt and light"; **contrast community** ("Little Flock"); embrace the Cross (Samuel Rayan, Stan Swamy).
