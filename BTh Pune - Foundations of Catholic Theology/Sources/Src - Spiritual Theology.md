# Src — Spiritual Theology (discipline)

> [!info] Source note · theme: [[09 - Christian Spirituality and Mary]]

## Definition & method
- Branch of theology using revelation + religious experience to define the supernatural life and direct growth to perfection; focus on the **experiential/lived**.
- **Balthasar**: spirituality = existential fundamental attitude ("spirit" not matter/instinct). Three general human spiritualities: **Eros** (Platonic inward), **Action/Praxis** (Aristotelian), **Apatheia** (Stoic).

## Stages of growth
- **Classical three ways**: Purgative (beginners) · Illuminative (proficient) · Unitive (perfect).
- **Rahner's critique**: too formal/empty; virtues can harden into sub-personal habits. The **"Situation"** (concrete life) defines the task; growth = increasing **"existential depth."**

## Key concepts
- **Universal call to holiness** (*Lumen Gentium*).
- Saints = *Analogatum Princeps*; Marian "Ecce ancilla" = "spirituality of spiritualities."
- **Kenosis** (self-emptying love), **Perichoresis** (mutual indwelling), **Christ the Concrete Universal** (integrates eros/action/apatheia).
- Christology: Descending / Ascending.
- **Conversion (Lonergan)**: Intellectual, Moral, Religious ("falling in love" with God).

## Theologians
- **Balthasar**: starts from God's self-revelation; Christ's descent = kenosis; Gospel the absolute norm.
- **Rahner**: anthropological start; Grace = God's Self-Communication; "Economic Trinity is the Immanent Trinity"; Mysticism of Everyday Life.
