# Src — MSFS in India

> [!info] Source note · theme: [[10 - Church History and the MSFS]]

**Topic**: Origins and early growth of the MSFS Indian Mission — the Vicariate of Visakhapatnam, mid-19th c.

## Call & departure (1842–45)
- **1842** Propaganda asks Annecy to take a mission; **10 May 1845** Visakhapatnam assigned (under Vicariate of Madras).
- First: 4 priests (Martin, Lavorel, Tissot, Thévenet) + 2 brothers (Carton, Fontanel); Pro-Vicar Gailhot (MEP).

## Arrival & challenges
- 88-day voyage → Pondicherry Sept 1845; stranded (delayed funds; Mgr Fenelly of Madras).
- Language study: Lavorel/Thévenet — Tamil; Tissot/Martin — Telugu.

## Key places
- **Yanam** — Fr. Martin in charge Feb 1846 (~120 Catholics); **died of sunstroke May 1846**.
- **Visakhapatnam** — Gailhot + others Feb 1846 (only 3 priests for 3,000 Christians before).
- Dispersal: Lavorel → **Kamptee**; Thévenet → **Aurangabad** (built a community despite opposition).

## Leadership — Neyret
- **Sébastien Théophile Neyret** — Pro-Vicar (Madras, March 1847); **Episcopal Consecration 24 Feb 1849**, first MSFS Bishop, **Vicar Apostolic of Visakhapatnam**.

## Sisters of St Joseph of Annecy
- Requested for schools; arrived Pondicherry Oct 1849; orphanage at Yanam Dec 1849; **24 Sisters** sent in Mermier's lifetime.

## Khonds' mission (tribal, from 1850)
- Tissot + Sermet (Sermet died at 26 of forest fever); Guillermin + Dupont persevered — **73 baptisms by July 1854**.

## Growth & support
- Heavily dependent on Annecy (Propaganda funds scarce).
- **1845–1862: Mermier sent 26 Fathers + 5 Brothers** — at times half the Congregation in India.
