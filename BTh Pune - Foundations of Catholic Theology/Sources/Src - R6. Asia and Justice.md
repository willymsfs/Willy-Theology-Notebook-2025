# Src — R6. Asia and Justice

> [!info] Source note (reading, Samuel Rayan) · theme: [[08 - Contextual and Liberation Theologies]] · author [[Samuel Rayan]]

**Thesis**: Asia's quest for justice = search for full humanity; the Church must move beyond spiritual/relief responses to **structural change** and **praxis** — faith and justice mutually constitutive.

## Context
- Recovery from colonial breakdown; small Asian Church, massive poverty; grassroots groups living among the poor (Dalits, Tribals, landless).

## Five responses of the Church
1. **The Spiritual Alone** — faith/society dualism (alien to the Incarnation).
2. **God's Will (fatalism)** — poverty as divine disposition (defective view of creation).
3. **Relief & Welfare** — emergency care (needs prophetic criticism).
4. **Technology (modernization)** — ignores imperial underdevelopment; widens the gap.
5. **Structural Change (ideal)** — replace exploitative structures with egalitarian ones.

## Justice
- Faith without justice impossible (mutually constitutive). God's justice = fidelity to creation (all flourish), **justice as the basic form of love**; contextual (Egypt→liberation, Galilee→fellowship).

## Six suggestions (praxis)
1. Social analysis. 2. Plan of instruction (kerygma as program of life). 3. Restructure liturgy (Eucharist egalitarian). 4. Equality for women. 5. Collaboration (critical collaboration with non-dogmatic Marxists). 6. Readiness for the Cross (Romero, grain of wheat).
