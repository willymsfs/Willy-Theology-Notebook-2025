# Src — R1. The Holy Spirit in the Gospel of John

> [!info] Source note (reading) · theme: [[06 - Holy Spirit (Pneumatology)]]

**Thesis**: sequential study of Johannine passages → a cohesive Johannine pneumatology.

## Passage analysis
- **Jn 1:32–34** — Spirit as **Identifier** (Jesus' baptism); the "dyad" marking Jesus as God's. Calvin (figurative) vs Tenney (literal dove).
- **Jn 3:5–8** — **Regeneration** (Nicodemus); Spirit as wind; OT (Joel, Ezekiel) eschatological outpouring.
- **Jn 3:34** — Spirit given **without measure** to Christ; full divinity.
- **Jn 4:21–24** — **"God is Spirit"**; correlation Father/Spirit (Raymond Brown — God's dealings, not essence).
- **Jn 6:63 & 7:39** — **Life-Giver**; conditioned on belief; given only after Jesus is "glorified."
- **Jn 14–15** — **Paraclete/Helper**; Spirit of Truth; "proceeds from the Father" (*filioque* debate).
- **Jn 16:5–15** — advantage of Christ's departure; **conviction of the world** (sin, righteousness, judgment); guides disciples into all truth; **glorifies Christ** (litmus test for charismatic movements).

## Summary
Spirit = Identifier · medium of new birth · vital for proclaiming/understanding · given on faith after ascension · Guide + Convictor.
