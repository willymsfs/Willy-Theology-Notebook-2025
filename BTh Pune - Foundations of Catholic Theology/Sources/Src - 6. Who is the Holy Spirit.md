# Src — 6. Who is the Holy Spirit (catechetical lesson)

> [!info] Source note · theme: [[06 - Holy Spirit (Pneumatology)]]

**Topic**: Simple catechesis — the Spirit is real though unseen.
- **Nature**: God's invisible presence living in believers; like wind, Wi-Fi, scent, love.
- **Trinity analogy**: Sun (Father), Light (Jesus), Heat (Spirit) — one God.
- **Work**: comforts, guides, helps us pray, teaches, gives peace, makes us brave, helps us live like Jesus.
- **Bible**: Jn 14:26 (teaches/reminds); Acts 1:8 (power); Gal 5:22–23 (fruits — love, joy, peace).
- **Symbols**: dove, fire, wind, oil, water.
- Ends with reflection questions + a prayer to the Spirit.
