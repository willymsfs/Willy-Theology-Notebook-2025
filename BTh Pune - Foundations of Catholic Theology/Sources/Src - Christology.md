# Src — Christology (and Trinity)

> [!info] Source note · theme: [[03 - Christology and Indian Christology]] · (also Trinity)

**Topic**: Exam guide (Mohan Doss SVD) — 15 topics: Christology (1–6, 15) + Trinity (7–14).

## Christology points
- **Jesus' knowledge**: limited/developing secular knowledge; profound mission-consciousness; did not know the date of the Parousia.
- **Freedom/sinlessness**: genuine human freedom, real temptations; sinlessness from total commitment to God's mission.
- **The Cross**: eschatological saving act; representative, expiatory service.
- **Resurrection**: cornerstone; appearances + empty tomb + narratives.
- **Uniqueness (J. Thoppil)**: definitive, unsurpassable revelation; mediates salvation for all without denying grace in other religions.

## Councils
- **Nicaea (325)** — vs Arianism; Son *homoousios*.
- **Constantinople I (381)** — vs Macedonianism; full divinity of the Spirit.
- **Toledo XI (675)** — inseparable unity, "Trinity."
- **Lateran IV (1215)** — one God, three persons.
- **Florence (1439)** — Filioque (West "and the Son" / East "through the Son").

## Titles of Jesus
Messiah · Son of God · Lord · Word/Logos · Servant of God · Absolute Saviour (Rahner) · Liberator (Latin America) · **Mahasamnyasananda** ("great renouncer of bliss," Indian).

## Models
- **Modern Christology**: Rahner (Transcendental, "anonymous Christian"); Latin American (Liberation); Pannenberg (Universal "from below," inclusive substitution); Moltmann (Messianic/Spirit, Theology of Hope).
- **Trinity**: Cappadocians (*Perichoresis*, Father as fountainhead); Augustine (psychological analogies — memory/understanding/will).
- **Trinity & religions**: *Trimurti* / *Sat-Cit-Ananda* (Hindu); *sunyata* / *Trikaya* (Buddhist); *Tawhid* (Islam).

## Heresies
- **Christological**: Monophysitism (denies human will), Docetism (denies humanity).
- **Trinitarian**: Dynamic Monarchianism, Modalism (Sabellius), Subordinationism/Arianism, Macedonianism, Tritheism (Joachim of Fiore).
