# Src — R7. Dalits and the Mission of the Church

> [!info] Source note (reading, L. Stanislaus) · theme: [[08 - Contextual and Liberation Theologies]]

**Thesis**: the Church's mission = holistic (social/economic/political/religious) liberation of Dalits; challenge caste + globalization via "Jesus as Dalit," "Dalitheothics," prophetic revolution.

## Present condition
- Social discrimination (3,000 years — housing, worship, water; violence).
- **Globalization** (WTO/IMF/World Bank): *Homo Consumerismus*, corporatized agriculture, threatens reservations.
- ~80% below poverty line; "neo-caste divide" in education.
- **Dalit women**: triple discrimination (caste + class + gender).
- **Dalit Christians**: **six-fold discrimination**; the Church tolerates caste (condemned as sinful).

## Theological paradigms
- **Jesus as Dalit** — self-emptying (Phil 2:7); ate with sinners; "Samaritan," executed "outside the camp"; resurrection = liberation.
- **Jesus as Drum** — Paraiyar drum = resistance + divine presence; emancipatory resistance + reconciliation.

## Missiological implications
- **Dalitheothics**: no faith/economics split ("cannot serve God and mammon"); challenge the profit market.
- Political empowerment ("politicise evangelisation, evangelise politics").
- **Option for justice**: look (Dalit perspective) · listen · share.
- Counter-cultural identity (children of God vs Hindutva); **prophetic diakonia**; **Dalit revolution (*Kranti*)** (Ambedkar — annihilate caste); collaborate with secular forums.

## Challenges to the Church
Re-read Revelation from victims; transform liturgy; cross ecclesial borders; Dalit literacy/leadership; **spirituality of resistance** ("spirituality for combat").
