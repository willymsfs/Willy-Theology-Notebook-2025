# Src — R3. The Jordan, the Tiber and the Ganges

> [!info] Source note (reading) · themes: [[02 - Indian Christian Theology and Inculturation]] · [[08 - Contextual and Liberation Theologies]]

**Thesis** (Panikkar): Christianity must move from **exclusivism (Jordan)** and **inclusivism (Tiber)** to true **pluralism (Ganges)**. Religions meet "in the skies" (Spirit), keeping their identities.

## Three rivers
- **Jordan** — Jewish origins, basic spirituality.
- **Tiber** — Helleno-Roman-Western intellect.
- **Ganges** — Asian/non-Abrahamic spiritualities.

## Five historical periods (kairological)
Witnessing (–430) · Conversion (Constantine–medieval, *conversio morum*) · Crusade (8th c.–1571) · Mission (1492–20th c.) · **Dialogue** (contemporary).

## Three geo-theological moments
- **Jordan → Exclusivism**: water, faith, historical event; saved = privileged few.
- **Tiber → Inclusivism**: fire, institution; Christianity the culmination ("anonymous Christians").
- **Ganges → Pluralism**: earth/secularity, outwardness, cosmic confidence; open without conquest.

## Christendom / Christianity / Christianness
- **Christendom** (civilization, past) · **Christianity** (institution, one among many) · **Christianness** (*Christlichkeit* — personal Christlike attitude, the "christic principle"). The Ganges = Christianness.

## True pluralism
- Not mere plurality nor merger; **incommensurability** (no neutral measuring stick); non-dualistic cosmic confidence.
- **Christic principle**: centre of reality (divine–human–cosmic); every being a "christophany."
