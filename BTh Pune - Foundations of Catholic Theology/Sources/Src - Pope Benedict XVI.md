# Src — Pope Benedict XVI: Documents

> [!info] Source note · theme: [[07 - Ecclesiology and Ecclesial Documents]]

**Themes**: continuity, faith & reason, biblical interpretation, liturgy, new evangelization, bioethics.

## Encyclicals
- **Deus Caritas Est** (2005, love/charity); **Spe Salvi** (2007, hope); **Caritas in Veritate** (2009, social doctrine, integral development).

## Apostolic Exhortations
- **Sacramentum Caritatis** (2007, Eucharist); **Verbum Domini** (2010, Word of God); *Africae Munus* (2011); *Ecclesia in Medio Oriente* (2012).

## Letters / Motu Proprio
- **Summorum Pontificum** (2007, 1962 Missal); *Omnium in Mentem* (2009, canon law); *Ubicumque et Semper* (2010, New Evangelization council); *Porta Fidei* (2011, Year of Faith).

## Books
- **Jesus of Nazareth** trilogy (2007/2011/2012); *Light of the World* (2010 interview).

## Other
- *Dignitas Personae* (2008, bioethics); **Regensburg Lecture** (2006, faith/reason); Westminster Hall (2010).
