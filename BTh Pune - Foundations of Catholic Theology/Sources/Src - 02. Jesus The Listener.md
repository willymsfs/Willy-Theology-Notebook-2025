# Src — 02. Jesus The Listener

> [!info] Source note (reading) · theme: [[03 - Christology and Indian Christology]]

**Thesis** (Mohan Doss SVD): Listening is a sacred art that transforms lives; Jesus the perfect listener — his listening to the Father fuels compassionate, non-judgmental listening to humanity.

## Listening to the Father
- Rooted in "Abba" (unprecedented tenderness, trust, surrender); prayer as intensive listening; the Cross as ultimate obedient listening.

## Public ministry — four encounters
- **Zacchaeus** — listened to his brokenness → transformation.
- **Blind man (Jericho)** — "stood still" to listen; compassion as response.
- **Woman in adultery** — non-judgmental listening restores dignity.
- **Samaritan woman** — transcends gender/cultural boundaries → new community.

## Barriers & dimensions
- Barriers: fear of diversity, "knowing everything," impotence. **Hearing (passive) vs listening (intentional).**
- Three dimensions: personal option · gift (undivided presence) · art (empathy; acceptance ≠ agreement).

## Conclusion
Jesus a blueprint for being human; listening "with head and heart" lets people "unfold, expand, grow and come alive"; pathway to peace and fellowship.
