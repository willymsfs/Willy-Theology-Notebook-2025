# Src — Indian Popular Religiosity

> [!info] Source note · themes: [[02 - Indian Christian Theology and Inculturation]] · [[09 - Christian Spirituality and Mary]]

## Terms
- **Religion**: behaviours/relations to the holy (creeds, codes, cults, symbols).
- **Spirituality**: inner experience, hidden root of religion.
- **Religiosity**: "habitual involvement in religious activity" — *extrinsic* (means) / *intrinsic* (end).
- **Popular religiosity**: expressions of common people; relies on classical religion as base.

## Characteristics of religion
Experience of the Transcendent · myths/sacred symbols (fire, water, Aum, cross) · sacred actions (worship, ritual) · concepts of salvation (Moksha, Nirvana) · sacred places/objects/animals · sacred community (Sangha, Church, Varna).

## Forms
- **Pilgrimages (Tirthas)** — barefoot, fasting, circumambulation, ritual bathing.
- **Sacred spaces/mountains** — temples (*Garbhagrih*), Kailash, Sinai.
- **Rituals/fasts** — daily Hindu rituals; Vrats (Vat Savitri, Karwa Chauth, Chatt Puja).
- **Festivals** — Diwali, Makar Sankranti, Maha Shivaratri, Holi.
- **Objects/symbols** — OM, Swastika, Lotus, Sindoor, bells/Shankh.
- **Sacred persons** — living figures, saints, avatars.

## Theological evaluation
- **Pilgrimages**: socio-cultural corrective to individualism; social cohesion; Catholic — earthly journey to heaven.
- **Murti (idol) worship**: divine immanence (world as God's "gross body"); *nirguna → saguna*; *laghu avatara*; **real presence** via consecration (*Prana-pratishta*, *Avahan*) — object for *bhakti*.
