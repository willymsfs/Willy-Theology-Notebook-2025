# Src — R1. George Soares-Prabhu's Vision (Priesthood & Religious Life)

> [!info] Source note (reading) · themes: [[03 - Christology and Indian Christology]] · [[05 - Priesthood]] · author [[George Soares-Prabhu]]

**Thesis**: the Indian Church must adopt Jesus' **Abba experience** and **liberative pedagogy**; via the **Agape Marga**, priests and religious build a Kingdom of **Freedom, Fellowship, Justice** as "contrast communities."

## Context
- India's *samsara*: **Poverty, pluriform Religiosity, Caste**. Critiques westernized "sahib culture" theology.

## Abba experience & Kingdom
- God as *Abba* (loving Parent) → all are siblings (universal love).
- **Kingdom** = Freedom (liberation from compulsions), Fellowship (mutual concern), Justice (just structures).

## Agape Marga
- Not *jnana*/*karma*/*bhakti* but **Agape Marga** — active concern; love of God ≡ love of neighbour; in an unjust society → struggle for justice.

## Threefold mission
- **Priest in solidarity** (not cultic; holiness = mercy → Cross); **Prophet** (confronts establishment, love-based ethics); **Pastor / liberative pedagogy** (change of heart + change of structures).

## Table fellowship
- "Revolutionary praxis"; "Dalit Christian" a contradiction — no caste in Christ.

## Vision for priests/religious
- Community builders not *pujaris*; prophetic communities ("salt and light"); the **contrast community** ("Little Flock") vs consumerism/destitution. Examples: Samuel Rayan, Stan Swamy.
