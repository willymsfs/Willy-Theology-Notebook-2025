# Src — A. Priesthood: Biblical/Foundational Theology

> [!info] Source note · theme: [[05 - Priesthood]]

**Topic**: Catholic priesthood patterned on the priesthood of Christ, the Great High Priest; call to be Prophet, Priest, Pastor — service, self-giving, agape.

## Common vs ministerial
- **Christ's priesthood** = source of both.
- **Common** (baptized) — all share Christ's priestly/prophetic/kingly offices.
- **Ministerial** (ordained) — Episcopate, Presbyterate, Diaconate via the Sacrament of Order; in hierarchical communion, **at the service** of the common priesthood.

## OT priesthood
- Institutionalized mediation: descending (God's will → humans) + ascending (prayers/sacrifice → God).
- **Pre-monarchical** (before 1050 BCE): natural priests (family heads) + professional (wandering Levites); priesthood of the Word.
- **Monarchical** (1050–587): King as natural priest; **Zadokites** in Jerusalem; *Urim*/*Thummim*, *Torah*, sacrifice.
- **Post-exilic** (after 587): purely cultic; stratified under the High Priest.
- **Priesthood of the people**: "kingdom of priests" — foreshadows the "People of God."

## Melchizedek (Gen 14; Ps 110; Heb 7)
- Priest **and** King (impossible under the Law); Jesus (of Judah) fulfils both.
- No genealogy → eternal, mirrors the pre-existent Son. Abraham tithed him → superior to the Levitical priesthood.

## Priesthood of Jesus
- Not of a priestly family; avoided the title "priest." **Theocentric** (Abba experience), **serving shepherd** (washes feet), **prophetic** (Kingdom, *anawim*), **mediator** (*kenosis* — victim and offerer).

## Hebrews
- Merciful/trustworthy High Priest (shared our humanity).
- **vs OT priests**: many, sinful, animal blood, earthly sanctuary, daily → **Jesus**: one eternal, blameless, His own blood, in heaven, once for all; interior not exterior. Mediates the New and Eternal Covenant.
