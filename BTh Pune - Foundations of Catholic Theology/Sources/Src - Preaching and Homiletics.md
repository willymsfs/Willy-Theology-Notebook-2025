# Src — Preaching & Homiletics (Roman Catholic)

> [!info] Source note · theme: [[05 - Priesthood]] (ministry of the Word)

## Historical foundations
- Roots in Jesus (Mk 3:14–15; 6:7–9). **Chrysostom** (eloquence, capture attention); **Augustine** (Gospel truth + Cicero's teach/delight/persuade). Call to preach rooted in **baptism** (all Christians).

## Vision, roles, dispositions
- **Forms**: Evangelization, Catechetical, Mystagogical, Liturgical.
- **Roles**: Herald · Teacher · Witness · Hermeneut · Mystagogue · Prophet.
- **Dispositions**: humility, compassion (no compassion = not preaching), patience, respect/love, joy.

## Homiletic habits
- Spiritual praxis (*lectio/visio/audio divina*); lifelong study (exegesis, theology, human condition); imagining/incubation; **practicing aloud**; seeking feedback/group prep ("not in India").

## Post-Vatican II documents
- **Sacrosanctum Concilium** (1962) — homily integral to the liturgy (omit only for serious reason).
- **Dei Verbum** (1965) — Scripture nourishes preaching; not "empty preachers... not hearers."
- **Presbyterorum Ordinis** (1965) — preaching the primary task of priests; contextualized.
- **Evangelii Nuntiandi** (1975) — evangelization central; simple, clear, faithful to the Magisterium; witness of life; never betray truth to please/shock.
