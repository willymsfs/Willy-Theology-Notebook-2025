# Src — Pope John Paul II: Documents

> [!info] Source note · theme: [[07 - Ecclesiology and Ecclesial Documents]]

**Categories**: Encyclicals · Apostolic Exhortations · Apostolic Letters · Other. **Themes**: dignity/life, family, social justice, new evangelization, laity, ecumenism.

## Key documents
- **Theology of the Body** (1979–84, 129 lectures); *Catechesi Tradendae* (1979).
- *Dives in Misericordia* (1980, mercy); *Familiaris Consortio* (1981, family); *Laborem Exercens* (1981, work).
- *Reconciliatio et Paenitentia* (1984); *Dominum et Vivificantem* (1986, Holy Spirit); *Redemptoris Mater* (1987, Mary); *Sollicitudo Rei Socialis* (1987, social).
- *Christifideles Laici* (1988, laity); *Redemptoris Missio* (1990, mission); *Centesimus Annus* (1991, social/post-communism).
- **Pastores Dabo Vobis** (1992, priestly formation); **Catechism of the Catholic Church** (1992).
- *Veritatis Splendor* (1993, moral); *Ordinatio Sacerdotalis* (1994, ordination to men); *Evangelium Vitae* (1995, life); *Ut Unum Sint* (1995, ecumenism).
- *Vita Consecrata* (1996); *Fides et Ratio* (1998, faith & reason); *Novo Millennio Ineunte* (2001); *Rosarium Virginis Mariae* (2002); *Ecclesia de Eucharistia* (2003); *Memory and Identity* (2005).
