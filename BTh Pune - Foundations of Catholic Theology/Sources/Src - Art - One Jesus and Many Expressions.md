# Src — One Jesus and Manifold Expressions of His Mission

> [!info] Source note (reading, Scaria Kuthirakkattel SVD) · theme: [[03 - Christology and Indian Christology]]

**Thesis**: all four Gospels stem from one root — Jesus' *Abba* experience — but each uses distinct Christology tailored to its community's crisis.

## Four Christologies
- **Mark — Suffering Messiah** (Rome ~70 CE, Neronian persecution): Kingdom vs Satan; rejects glorious Messiah; disciples "blind"/faithless → warning to endure.
- **Matthew — New Moses / New Israel** (Antioch, Jewish-Christian identity conflict): Sermon on the Mount ~ Sinai; ten miracles ~ ten plagues; Twelve = twelve tribes; commission to all nations.
- **Luke — the Prophet / universal mission** (Gentile community): Spirit-anointed (Isa 61); like Elijah/Elisha, option for the marginalized (women, Samaritans, poor); seventy disciples = 70 nations.
- **John — Revealer of the Father** (Ephesus, conflict with 'Jews'): intimate Father–Son; signs/discourses; to reject Jesus = reject the Father. Discipleship = **faith + love** (new commandment; the Beloved Disciple).

## Conclusion
No single title exhausts the mystery; "Kingdom of God" (Synoptics) and "Revealer" (John) share one root — the *Abba* experience.
