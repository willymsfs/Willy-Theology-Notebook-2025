# Src — Theological Anthropology, Grace, Eschatology

> [!info] Source note · themes: [[11 - Old Testament Foundations (Prophets, Wisdom, Anthropology)]] · [[09 - Christian Spirituality and Mary]]

**Topic**: Christian Anthropology, Eschatology, Grace — integral vision of God, world, self via the "Christic paradigm."

## The human person
- Asks "why" of existence; a mystery (angelic love / demonic cruelty).
- *Gaudium et Spes*: dignity, community, human action.
- **Fourfold communion**: with God, self, others, nature.

## Imago Dei
- **Relational** (dialogue with God/others) · **Substantive** (soul, intellect, will — Augustine, Aquinas) · **Functional** (steward/representative). Male and female equally the image.

## Body-soul
- Critiques Greek dualism. **Biblical unity**: *nepes*, *basar* = whole person; Paul's *sarx*/*pneuma* = sinful humanity vs God's Spirit, not body vs soul.
- Humans as **"body-persons."** Soul: Church teaches **Creationism** (each soul immediately created by God).

## Creation
- Genesis = mythic/pre-scientific religious truth, not science; evolution presupposes creation (Francis). Total dependence (love, not enslavement). Rejects dualism + pantheism.

## Grace
- **Self-communication of God's life and love** (not a "thing").
- OT: *Hanan*, *Hesed*, *Rahum*. NT: *Charis* (justification, redemption, reconciliation).
- **Rahner**: "supernatural existential" — human nature already drawn to God's self-gift; grace empowers freedom.

## Sin & the Fall
- Sin = refusal of relationships, alienation; "be their own gods." **Original sin** developed by **Augustine** vs Pelagianism; privation of holiness (*concupiscence*); Christ's grace necessary.

## Destiny (eschatology)
- Not futurology. **Death** = surrender into God; **Resurrection** = transfiguration of the whole person (not resuscitation); **Judgment** = God ratifies our free choices; **Purgatory** = purifying encounter with the glorified Jesus; **Hell** = total separation; **Heaven** = Trinitarian communion; **Parousia** = growing presence of Christ consummated at death.
