# Src — D. Indian Christology

> [!info] Source note · themes: [[03 - Christology and Indian Christology]] · [[02 - Indian Christian Theology and Inculturation]]

**Core quest**: answer "Who is the Son of Man?" from Indian frameworks; shift "who is he?" → "where is he?"; present Jesus as **Marga (Way)** and **Moksha (Goal)**.

## Images & titles for Christ
- **Cit of Saccidananda** · **Jivanmukta Yogi** (awakened one, *Aham Brahmasmi*) · **Nara Hari** (God-Man) · **The Fulfiller** · **Anonymous Christianity** (Rahner) · **Prajapati-Purusha** (sacrificial man) · **Cosmotheandric Mystery** · **Avatar / Prema-Avatar** (Crucified Kenotic Avatar) · **Antaryami** (Inner Ruler) · **Guru / Guru-Mauli** (Crucified Guru) · **Yatri** (pilgrim) & **the Dancer** (Nataraja) · **Tribal** (Divine Drummer, *Deonra* exorcist, *Karam* branch) · **Supreme Satyagrahi** · **the Dalit / Vedanamurti Sevak**.

## The margas
- **Jnana Marga → Advaita Christology (Head)**: *Cit of Saccidananda*; *Aham Brahmasmi* → *Aham Christamayasmi* (communion, not absorption). *Critique*: abstract, elitist.
- **Karma Marga → Abba Christology (Hand)**: Supreme Satyagrahi, Nishkama Karma Yogi; Kingdom praxis, *kenosis*, orthopraxis, Cross as solidarity. *Critique*: risks reducing Jesus to a revolutionary.
- **Bhakti Marga → Avatar Christology (Heart)**: *Antaryamin*, incarnate Avatar; grace, love-union. *Critique*: docetic risk, "one avatar among many."
- **Prema Marga → Agape Christology (Integral Person)**: Jesus as Incarnate Love (*Prema-Avatar*); integrates jnana + karma + bhakti. The **Eucharist** symbolizes it (see [[Src - Eucharist]]). Jesus is both the Way to God-realization and God-realization itself.
