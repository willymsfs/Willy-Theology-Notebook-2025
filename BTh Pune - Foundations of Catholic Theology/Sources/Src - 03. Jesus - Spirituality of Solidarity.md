# Src — 03. Jesus: Paradigm for a Spirituality of Solidarity

> [!info] Source note (reading) · themes: [[03 - Christology and Indian Christology]] · [[08 - Contextual and Liberation Theologies]]

**Thesis** (Mohan Doss SVD): authentic spirituality = **liberation and solidarity**, not world-negating withdrawal. Jesus radically identified with the powerless and confronted oppression.

## Context
- Divided society: Roman exploitation + purity principle (pure/impure).
- Alternatives — Sadducees (accommodate), Zealots (rebel), Pharisees/Essenes (withdraw); **Jesus → Love** (from the *Abba* experience).

## Practices of solidarity
- Identification with sinners/poor (John's baptism; itinerant, "nowhere to lay his head").
- Centre → periphery (women, tax collectors, the sick, fisher folk).
- **Scandalous table fellowship** with outcasts.

## Conflict & Cross
- Solidarity → inevitable conflict; negation of legalism/ritualism; **the Cross = ultimate expression of solidarity and love**.

## Dimensions today
Humans as sacred encounter (God in suffering humanity) · take the side of the periphery ("remove sandals of comfort") · journey with powerlessness (power of God) · active + contemplative listening · nourishing relationships (Good Samaritan) · **the poor as evangelizers** · walk in the Spirit.

## Conclusion
**Kenotic** life; Oscar Romero + the grain of wheat (Jn 12:24); courage to risk, wisdom to celebrate, vision to create, strength to persist.
