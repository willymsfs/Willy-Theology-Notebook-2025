# Src — Wisdom Literature (and Psalms)

> [!info] Source note · theme: [[11 - Old Testament Foundations (Prophets, Wisdom, Anthropology)]]

## The books
- Five strictly: **Proverbs, Job, Ecclesiastes, Sirach, Wisdom of Solomon** (Jews/Reformers reject Sirach + Wisdom). + Song of Solomon + Psalms → **Catholic 7 / Jewish-Reformer 5**.

## Nature of wisdom
- Hebrew ***hokmah*** (318×), Greek ***Sophia***.
- **Secular** (pragmatic, right time/place, moderation) + **Sacred** (righteous before God; wisdom from God, tied to Torah/piety).
- Characteristics: late origin, anthropocentric/individualistic, creation theology, poetry/*mashal*.

## Lady Wisdom
- Personified female (Prov 1–9): *hokmah/Sophia* feminine; ~ Egyptian Ma'at; polemic vs fertility cults (Asherah).
- **Lady Wisdom vs Lady Folly** (fidelity vs infidelity to Yahweh).
- Prov 8: God's "master worker"/architect (*amon*), pre-existent. Sirach: Wisdom = Torah, dwells in Israel. Wisdom of Solomon: emanation of God's glory.

## Themes by book
- **Retribution** (central; Proverbs upholds, Job/Ecclesiastes contest); **fear of the Lord** (prerequisite).
- **Proverbs** — practical primer, diligence, prudence, the "fool."
- **Job** — theodicy shattering retribution; suffering ≠ always punishment; submissive trust.
- **Ecclesiastes (Qohelet)** — *hebel* (vanity); limitations, joy in daily gifts, "fear God, keep his commandments."
- **Sirach** — vs Hellenism; Wisdom = Law; social justice, family, almsgiving.
- **Wisdom of Solomon** — Greek, for Alexandrian Diaspora; introduces **immortality of the soul / eschatology**.
- **Song of Solomon** — human married love; allegorized (God/Israel, Christ/Church).
- **Psalms** — prayers/hymns/laments; "Wisdom Psalms" venerate Torah.
