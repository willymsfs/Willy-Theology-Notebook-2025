# Src — Feminist Theology

> [!info] Source note · theme: [[08 - Contextual and Liberation Theologies]]

## Fundamentals
- Feminism: rights/dignity/freedom for the excluded (not "hating men"). Term coined by **Charles Fourier** (adopted by Hubertine Auclert).
- **Androcentrism** (male-centred), **Patriarchy** (father-supremacy; Gnanadason: "graded subjugation"), **Kyriarchy** (Schüssler Fiorenza — pyramidal ruling), **Intersectionality** (Kimberlé Crenshaw). **Sex vs gender** (Ann Oakley: biological vs socially learned).

## Concerns
- From **feminist consciousness** (Gerda Lerner). Elizabeth Johnson: **transform the system**, not join it.
- **Five concerns**: equality/dignity/freedom · correct oppressive structures · reclaim liberating Scripture · gender sensitivity/partnership · include women's experience.

## Method — Review, Recover, Reconstruct
- **Review** (whose perspective? e.g. Lot, Gen 19) · **Recover** (hidden women — Shiprah/Puah, Huldah) · **Reconstruct** (renewed symbols).

## Hermeneutics — Schüssler Fiorenza's four principles
Suspicion · Remembrance · Proclamation · Creative Visualization.

## Key thinkers
- **Elizabeth Cady Stanton** (*The Woman's Bible*, 1895); Mary Madeleva Wolff; Valerie Saiving.
- **Asian**: Marianne Katoppo, Mary John Mananzan, Kwok Pui-Lan, Chung Hyun Kyung; group **EWA**.

## Indian feminist theology
- **Savitribai Phule** (first woman teacher); Swarnakumari Devi (*Bharathi*), Sarala Devi Chaudarani, Pandita Ramabai.
- Post-independence: Jessie Tellis-Nayak, Aruna Gnanadason, Monica Melanchthon (first Indian woman OT PhD); Shalini Mulackal, Kochurani Abraham.
- **CBCI Gender Policy (2010)** — gender equality roadmap for the Indian Church.
