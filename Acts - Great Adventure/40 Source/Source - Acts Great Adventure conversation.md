## Flagged inferences
The guide's own note on sources: two passages fill narrative detail from the text of Acts itself where the workbook excerpts were summary-level — the riot/trial details in [[S05 - First Resistance]], and parts of the courtroom sequence in [[S18 - The Prisoner Speaks]] (the forty conspirators, Felix's hope of a bribe). Everything else traces to the workbook's talk notes, study questions and boxes.

## Structural note
The guide numbers the sessions slightly differently from the framework overview: Session 11 covers Acts 11–12, Session 19 covers Acts 25:13–28:31 (including the storm and Rome), and Session 20 is a synthesis and commissioning rather than a new passage. These notes follow the guide.
