# Luke–Acts Parallels

The intertextual key of the whole course: what Jesus *began* to do, he *continues* to do through Peter and Paul.

## Jesus // the Church

| Jesus (Luke) | The Church (Acts) |
|---|---|
| Prays at his baptism (Lk 3:21) | The disciples pray awaiting Spirit-baptism (Acts 1:14) |
| Anointed by the Spirit (Lk 3:21-22) | The Church anointed at Pentecost (Acts 2:1-4) |
| Heals a paralytic (Lk 5:17-26) | Peter heals the lame man (Acts 3:1-10); Paul at Lystra (14:8-13); Eutychus (20:9-12) |
| Four trials (Lk 22–23) | Paul's four trials (Acts 23–26) |
| Dying words: forgiveness, commendation (Lk 23:34, 46) | Stephen's dying words (Acts 7:59-60) |
| Pilate: "he has done nothing deserving death" (Lk 23:16, 22) | Agrippa: "this man could have been set free" (Acts 26:32) |
| Raises Jairus' daughter (Mk 5:35-43) | Peter raises Tabitha (Acts 9:36-42) |

## Peter // Paul

| Peter | Paul |
|---|---|
| Heals a man lame from birth (3:1-10) | Heals a man lame from birth (14:8-13) |
| "Men of Israel, why do you wonder?" (3:12) | "Men, why are you doing this?" (14:15) |
| Pentecost sermon (Acts 2) | Pisidian Antioch sermon (Acts 13:16-41) |
| Struck the magician Simon with rebuke (8:20-24) | Struck Elymas with blindness (13:11) |
| Freed from prison by an angel (12:6-11) | Freed by an earthquake at Philippi (16:25-26) |
