# Thread — Suffering as seed

## The idea, in one sentence
Persecution does not stop the gospel; it scatters it - the blood of the martyrs is the seed of the Church.

## Explanation
Beaten apostles leave the council rejoicing to be counted worthy of dishonour for the Name (Acts 5). Stephen's death scatters the Church into Judea and Samaria, and the scattering becomes sowing (Acts 8:4). Saul is told at his call how much he must suffer for the Name (9:16), and the promise unfolds through Acts 14, 16, 21 and the letters (1 Cor 4; 2 Cor 11; Phil 1; Col 1:24). Read with John Paul II, *Salvifici Doloris* 27: suffering shares in the treasure of the Redemption.
