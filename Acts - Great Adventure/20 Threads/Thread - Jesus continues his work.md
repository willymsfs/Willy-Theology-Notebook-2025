# Thread — Jesus continues his work

## The idea, in one sentence
Acts is volume two of one work: what Jesus "began to do and teach" (Acts 1:1) he continues to do through his Spirit-filled Church.

## Explanation
Luke builds deliberate parallels between Jesus, Peter and Paul. The baptism/Pentecost anointing (Lk 3:21-22 // Acts 2:1-4); the healed paralytic (Lk 5:17-26 // Acts 3:1-10; 14:8-13; 20:9-12); the dying words of Jesus and of Stephen (Lk 23:34, 46 // Acts 7:59-60); the four trials of Jesus and the four trials of Paul (Lk 22-23 // Acts 23-26), closing with Agrippa's "this man could have been set free" echoing Pilate (Acts 26:32 // Lk 23:16, 22). Peter raises Tabitha as Jesus raised Jairus' daughter (Acts 9 // Mk 5:35-43).
