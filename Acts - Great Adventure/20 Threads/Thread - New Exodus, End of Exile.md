# Thread — New Exodus, End of Exile

## The idea, in one sentence
The signs and wonders of Acts renew the signs of the Exodus: the Church is leading a new exodus out of the bondage of sin.

## Explanation
Signs and wonders punctuate the narrative (Acts 2:19; 4:30; 6:8; 14:3; 15:12). Daniel's seventy weeks lead to Jesus, who ends the exile of sin (Dn 9). "The Way" is the way of the Lord out of Egypt (Ex 13:21; Is 40:3; Jn 14:6). Peter's prison escape is staged in Exodus language on Passover night: "move quickly," sandals on (Ex 12:11), "brought out" of the hand of Herod (Ex 3:7-8).
