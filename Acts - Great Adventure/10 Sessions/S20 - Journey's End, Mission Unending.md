# Session 20 — Journey's End, Mission Unending

> **Synthesis and commissioning** · **Passage:** Conclusion · **Leg:** [[Leg III - To the Ends of the Earth]]

## Slide 1 · Trailhead (5 min)
Look back over the whole map before you leave base camp for good.

## Slide 2 · Story Script (15 min)
Close the loop with the class: Jesus' life recapitulated Israel's life; the disciples' lives recapitulated Jesus' life; and now the baton is in the room. Retell in five minutes the whole arc — upper room, fire, gate, stones, sheet, chains, storm, Rome — and end where Luke ends: unhindered, unfinished.

## Slide 3 · Deeper Look (30 min)
- Jesus chooses people like you and me — "do not be afraid, I will be with you."
- He establishes his Kingdom by a New Covenant: reconstitutes Israel, brings in the Gentiles, fills the Church with the Spirit at Pentecost.
- **The spread of the Kingdom:** seeds of growth in persecution; Peter (first half), Paul (second half); Satan opposes, the Spirit empowers; the reward is the beatific vision.
- **The key verse, one last time — Acts 1:8:** witness = *martus* = martyr, giving ourselves for others daily.
- **Personal appropriation of the map:** your Jerusalem = those close to you; your Judea and Samaria = those outside your circle; your ends of the earth = an adventure or mission.
- **The Eucharist as the source of apostolic zeal.**
- **Continuing the adventure:** Acts as the framework for reading Paul's letters; choose one epistle and read it prayerfully with a journal (*Dei Verbum* 21 — in the sacred books the Father meets his children with great love).

## Slide 6 · Campfire (10 min)
1. How would you describe what Acts is about to someone who has never read it?
2. What did you learn about God's purpose for the Church — and how does that affect your actions?
3. What did you learn about the Holy Spirit that has had, or will have, an effect on your life?
4. What stands out most from Acts 1–8? What was most important in Acts 8–12? What intrigued you in Acts 13–28 for future study?

Close with a written **"Dear Lord…"** prayer, as every session in the workbook ends.
