# Session 15 — Anchored in Corinth

> **Ministry in Corinth; the Third Journey Begins** · **Passage:** Acts 18 · **Leg:** [[Leg III - To the Ends of the Earth]]

## Slide 1 · Trailhead (5 min)
Eighteen months in a city famous for commerce and cult prostitution — and the birth-context of the Corinthian letters.

## Slide 2 · Story Script (15 min)
Paul arrives in Corinth alone and, by his own admission, in weakness and fear. He finds work and family with Aquila and Priscilla, tentmakers expelled from Rome. When opposition mounts, the Lord speaks in a night vision: "Do not be afraid… for I have many people in this city." Paul stays. The synagogue ruler Crispus believes with all his household; Gallio the proconsul throws the case out of court; and the Corinthian church — brilliant, gifted, quarrelsome — is born.

## Slide 3 · Deeper Look (30 min)
- **Corinth:** capital of southern Greece, political centre, centre of cult prostitution. Paul there AD 50-51; Gallio proconsul AD 51-52 — a fixed date for Pauline chronology. Claudius' expulsion of the Jews from Rome.
- **Aquila and Priscilla:** a house church (1 Cor 16:19); partners in Corinth, Ephesus (18:18-19) and Rome (Rom 16:3-5).
- **Reading the epistles inside Acts:** at least three letters to Corinth; 1 Corinthians as instruction in faith and conduct — divisions and immaturity (1 Cor 1–4), immorality and apostolic authority (1 Cor 5).
- **Gifts of the Spirit (1 Cor 12:7-11):** source and purpose — building up the body; one body, many members (12:12-20); the "still more excellent way" — love (1 Cor 13); the attributes of love (13:4-8a).
