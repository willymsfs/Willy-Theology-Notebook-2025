# Session 1 — Reading the Map

> **Introduction to Acts** · **Passage:** Introduction · **Leg:** [[Leg I - Base Camp Jerusalem]]

## Slide 1 · Trailhead (5 min)
Acts completes the three-part Great Adventure series (Bible Timeline → Matthew → Acts). A thirty-year span after the Resurrection, in which the Church "turned the world upside down" (Acts 17:6).

## Slide 2 · Story Script (15 min)
Begin with Luke the physician at his desk, writing to one man — Theophilus, "lover of God" — telling him volume two of a story: what Jesus *began* to do (the Gospel) and what he *continues* to do through his Spirit-filled Church. Invite the class: every lover of God is Theophilus; this letter is addressed to you.

## Slide 3 · Deeper Look (30 min)
- **Author:** Luke, physician, sometime companion of Paul. **Date:** likely before or in the early AD 60s — no mention of Nero's persecution (AD 65), Paul's martyrdom, or the fall of the Temple (AD 70).
- **Audience:** Gentile Christians and potential converts; Luke omits Hebrew-Aramaic presuppositions.
- **Structure from Acts 1:8:** Jerusalem (1:1–8:3) → Judea & Samaria (8:4–12:25) → ends of the earth (13:1–28:31). Peter dominates chapters 1–12, Paul chapters 13–28.
- **Intertextual key — the Luke–Acts parallels:** Jesus prays at his baptism / the disciples pray awaiting Spirit-baptism (Lk 3:21 // Acts 1:14); anointing by the Spirit (Lk 3:21-22 // Acts 2:1-4); healing of a paralytic (Lk 5:17-26 // Acts 3:1-10; 20:9-12); four trials of Jesus // four trials of Paul (Lk 22–23 // Acts 23–26); dying words of Jesus // of Stephen (Lk 23:34, 46 // Acts 7:59-60). See [[Luke-Acts Parallels]].
