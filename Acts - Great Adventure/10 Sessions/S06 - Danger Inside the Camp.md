# Session 6 — Danger Inside the Camp

> **Problems Within, Persecutions Without** · **Passage:** Acts 4:32-5:42 · **Leg:** [[Leg I - Base Camp Jerusalem]]

## Slide 1 · Trailhead (5 min)
The camp's greatest dangers are twofold: deception within, persecution without. Barnabas versus Ananias and Sapphira.

## Slide 2 · Story Script (15 min)
Barnabas lays a field's price at the apostles' feet — freely, wholly. Ananias and Sapphira stage the same gesture with a hidden discount, lying not to men but to the Holy Spirit. Both fall dead, and "great fear came upon the whole church." Meanwhile Peter's mere *shadow* heals the sick in the streets, an angel opens prison doors by night, and beaten apostles walk out of the council *rejoicing* to be counted worthy of dishonour for the Name.

## Slide 3 · Deeper Look (30 min)
- **One heart and soul:** land-and-blessing theology (Gen 12:1-3; 15) transposed — *Eretz Israel* is no longer the point; the New Covenant blessing is in the heart; the Kingdom is not limited by geography (Dt 30:1-10; Dn 9's seventy weeks leading to Jesus, who ends the exile of sin).
- **Ananias and Sapphira:** Satan fills their hearts; parallel to Achan's sin (Josh 7, *herem*); there is no private sin; lying to the Spirit = lying to a divine Person (Constantinople, AD 381).
- **Signs and wonders:** Exodus signs renewed in the Church's new exodus (Acts 2:19; 4:30; 6:8; 14:3; 15:12); sacrament vs. sacramental; God uses even shadows (5:15); the communion of saints.
- **Angels free the apostles** (Heb 13:2). **Gamaliel's counsel** — if this is of God, you cannot overthrow it (cf. CCC 269); the "changing of the guard" in Israel's leadership.
- **Civil disobedience** in charity and justice (CCC 450, 2242); the blood of the martyrs as the seed of the Church.
