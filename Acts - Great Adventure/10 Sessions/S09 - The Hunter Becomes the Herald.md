# Session 9 — The Hunter Becomes the Herald

> **The Persecutor Becomes the Preacher** · **Passage:** Acts 9 · **Leg:** [[Leg II - Crossing Borders]]

## Slide 1 · Trailhead (5 min)
The Church's fiercest enemy is about to become its greatest missionary.

## Slide 2 · Story Script (15 min)
Saul rides for Damascus with arrest warrants, breathing threats against "the Way." At noon a light from heaven throws him to the ground and a voice speaks his name twice — the way God spoke to Abraham, Jacob, Moses, Samuel: "Saul, Saul, why do you persecute *me*?" Not "my followers" — *me*. Three days blind, then a frightened disciple named Ananias lays hands on the hunter and calls him "Brother Saul." Meanwhile in Joppa, Peter raises Tabitha from the dead, exactly as Jesus raised Jairus' daughter.

## Slide 3 · Deeper Look (30 min)
- **"The Way" (Acts 9:2):** also an Essene self-description; a code name for the movement; the way of the Lord out of Egypt (Ex 13:21). Jesus leads the return from the exile of sin (Is 40:3; Jn 14:6).
- **Route:** Saul travels through Zebulun and Naphtali — "the people who walked in darkness have seen a great light" (Is 9:1-2).
- **Background:** Hebrew name Shaul (after Israel's first king), tribe of Benjamin, zealous student of Gamaliel.
- **Doubled name (9:4)** = solemn divine call: Gen 22:11; 46:3; Ex 3:4; 1 Sam 3:10.
- **"Why do you persecute *me*":** the union of Christ and his body the Church — Paul's later theology (1 Cor 12; Eph 5:21-32; CCC 787-791, 795).
- **Blind Saul represents blind Israel;** converted Saul fulfills Israel's calling — a light to the nations (Acts 13:47).
- **Suffering for the Name (9:16):** Acts 14; 16; 21; 1 Cor 4; 2 Cor 11; Phil 1; Col 1:24 — Jesus makes room for our participation (*Salvifici Doloris*); redemptive suffering.
- **Chronology:** three years pass before Jerusalem, via Arabia (Gal 1:15-18).
- **Peter heals Tabitha:** parallel to Mk 5:35-43 — Peter's words and works mirror Jesus'.
