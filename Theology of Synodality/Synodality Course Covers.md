**THEOLOGY OF SYNODALITY**

Course Description: A foundational course introducing the biblical, theological, historical, and pastoral dimensions of Synodality. It equips learners to understand and practice communion, participation, and mission in the Church today.

1 – Introduction to Synodality

2 – Biblical Foundations I: Old Testament
·         People of God on a journey
·         Covenantal assemblies and communal discernment
·         Prophetic listening and obedience

3 – Biblical Foundations II: New Testament
·         Jesus forming a community of disciples
·         The early Christian community (Acts 2–4)
·         The Council of Jerusalem (Acts 15) as a synodal model

4 – Synodality in the Early Church
·         Local synods and ecumenical councils
·         Role of bishops, deacons, and local communities
·         Monastic and communal discernment traditions

5 – Synodality Through the Middle Ages to Modern Times
·         Development and decline of synodal practices
·         Centralization, reform movements, and challenges
·         Recovery of synodal practices before Vatican II

6 – Vatican II and the Renewal of Synodal Vision
·         Lumen Gentium: People of God and collegiality
·         Gaudium et Spes: dialogue with the world
·         Restoring participatory structures in the Church

7 – Magisterial Foundations: Synod of Bishops
·         Paul VI’s motu proprio Apostolica Sollicitudo
·         Evolution of synodal processes
·         Bishops in communion with the Pope

8 – Pope Francis and the Synodal Church
·         The 50th Anniversary Address (2015)
·         Key themes: listening, discernment, encounter
·         Clericalism and the need for co-responsibility

9 – Synodality and the Sensus Fidei
·         Meaning of sensus fidei and sensus fidelium
·         The role of the whole People of God
·         Discerning the voice of the Spirit in the community

10 – The Synod on Synodality (2021–2024)
·         Global listening process
·         Themes: communion, participation, mission
·         Insights from diocesan, continental, and universal stages

11 – Theological Principles of Discernment
·         Ignatian and biblical roots of communal discernment
·         Spiritual conversation method
·         Listening to the marginalized and “the least”

12 – Participation and Co-Responsibility
·         Baptismal dignity and mission
·         Role of laity, women, youth, families, religious, clergy
·         Building participatory pastoral structures

13 – Synodal Leadership and Ministry
·         Servant leadership
·         Humility, dialogue, and accountability
·         Leading parishes through discernment and shared mission

14 – Challenges and Opportunities
·         Fear of change, polarization, and resistance
·         Misunderstanding Synodality as parliamentary democracy
·         Opportunities for evangelization, unity, and renewal

15 – Synodality and the Mission of the Church
·         Synodal Church as a missionary Church
·         Synodality in ecumenism and interreligious dialogue
·         Concluding vision: “A Church that listens, walks, and serves together”
